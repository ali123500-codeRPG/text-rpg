# BG-1514

**What was wrong:** Two buttons showed as the browser's plain gray box instead of the game's style: the quick-heal slot on the health box above the minimap (a bandage or draught icon with its count), and Copy for Claude on the Bugs page, beside Send open bugs to Claude.

**Root cause:** Neither button had any rule in `src/ui/styles.css` that matched it, so the browser's own button look (gray fill, square border) showed.
- Quick-heal slot (`healBtn`, `src/ui/play.ts`): it was made for the action bar, where `.hs-picker button` and `.inbar button.itm` gave it its look. The action deck change (AD1-AD16, #858) moved it into the `.vitals` box beside the health bars and kept its classes, `itm heal-slot`. Outside `.inbar .hs-picker` none of those rules match, and `.vitals` only had a rule for `[hidden]`.
- Copy for Claude (`src/ui/bugs.ts`, #1489): its class is `hs-btn`, which the stylesheet never defines. The house buttons are `hs-btn-main`, `hs-btn-text` and `hs-icon-btn`.

**What was done:** The quick-heal slot takes the house icon button, `hs-icon-btn` (no fill, muted colour, accent on hover, dimmed when disabled), plus a small gap and padding for its count in `.vitals .heal-slot`. Copy for Claude takes `hs-btn-text`, the house secondary button. Checked on the built page (`?fake=1`) by listing every visible button whose fill is the browser default, on every rail page: before, the heal slot (Play) and Copy for Claude (Bugs); after, none.
