# BG-1596

**What was wrong:** In the IAA-108 simulated playtest the narration called a man "the headman" on turn 2, before he had been introduced to a newcomer.

**Root cause:** The Weaver set him `knownOnSight` and put his role in his public label. `knowsName` in `src/engine/relations.ts` gives every viewer, a newcomer included, the name of a `knownOnSight` actor (BG-95), and every agent that sees an actor gets its label. The Weaver's shared plan rules (rule 4 in `PLAN_RULES_SHARED`, `src/agents/prompts.ts`) said public text is only what anyone present could see or hear, but never said what `knownOnSight` means, nor that a role nobody can see does not belong in a label. The field appeared only in the output shape, so the Weaver used it for anyone important in the place.

**What was done:** Rule 4 now says a label is what a stranger sees, with a role, rank or title only when it shows (a uniform, a badge, a priest's robes), and that `knownOnSight` is only for someone famous beyond the place, whose name and face any newcomer would know (PQ9 a). The rule is shared, so the World builder gets it too. How much each person knows on sight by skill (common sense, people, the supernatural) is the open part of PQ9, asked separately.
