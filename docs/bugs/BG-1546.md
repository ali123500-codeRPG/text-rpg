# BG-1546

**What was wrong:** "I take the coins" with the headman holding them out ended on "Which item do you mean?", twice, though the Parser named exactly the item in his `holdsOut`. The deck's Take button would have hit the same question.

**Root cause:** `checkParserLegality` (`src/agents/legality.ts`) lets an action's item be only one of the player's own or one lying in reach (with its own lists for filch, buy and barter). A take (BF34) names something the target holds out or carries, which is neither, so every take of a held-out thing, or from someone yielded, down or asleep, was illegal. The engine's `doTake` handles these; the gate before it never let them through, and the case tests play their intents past the gate. `checkNpcLegality` had the same gap: an agent-run NPC taking what the player held out to it had the item dropped (`invalid_gear`), and its take then found no item.

**What was done:** A take may name what its target holds out to the player or what the player sees on them (the Parser), or what anyone in view holds out to it or is seen carrying (an NPC); the engine still decides whether the take works. `tests/cases/s305-give-take.test.ts` now also runs the take through the Parser's gate. On the playtest save, the turn-9 order was refused with `action.item rw0_0_IT25` before and passes after.
