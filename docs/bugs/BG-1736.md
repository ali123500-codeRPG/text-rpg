# BG-1736

**Symptom:** A bed was reached by title on top of metres. Someone 0.5 m from a cot across an open title edge (a street
and its square) could not lie down on it; and the metres themselves ran straight through walls (a bed 1.16 m away
behind the tavern wall measured as 1.16 m), hidden only because the title test came first.

**Root cause:** `src/engine/resolve.ts` `thingInReach` asked `a.zone === bed.zone` before `bedGap`, and
`src/engine/sleep.ts` `bedToLieOn` looked only at beds in the sleeper's title (`freeBeds(state, a, a.zone)`).
`bedGap` measured a flat gap to the footprint, with no floor height and no walls. The title stood in for "same room and
floor" (AGENTS.md rule 8: a title never decides what can be reached).

**Why it caused the symptom:** the bed across the edge was in another title, so neither check looked at it; and with the
title test gone, the flat gap alone would have reached beds through walls and on other floors.

**Fix:** `bedGap` counts the floors' height apart and is Infinity when a wall (a counter too) with no open way through
lies on the walking line to the bed. `thingInReach` and `bedToLieOn` use it over every bed, whatever its title.
`freeBeds(zone)` still lists the beds of a routine's named sleeping place (naming, kept). Spec: game-clock.md FT8 line.

**Verified:** a temporary test reproduced both on `main` (the cot across the open edge: none to lie on; the bed behind
the wall: 1.16 m) and passed with the fix (lies on the cot; Infinity behind the wall). s278 (fatigue, beds), s317,
s318 and s213 pass. The temporary test was deleted.

## Part 2: leaving a place

**Symptom:** a readied "leaves" fired when someone stepped over an open edge between two titles while still in plain
sight, and did not tell a door from such an edge. A follow ended at once when the one followed went out of sight through
a door the player saw them take ("slipped_away" or "out_of_sight" by title), and an attack ordered on someone just gone
through a door was refused ("Who do you mean?"), though the owner expects to go after them.

**Root cause:** `src/engine/ready.ts` `readyHits` counted `x.zone !== was.at.zone` as leaving; `src/engine/resolve.ts`
`keepFollowing` ended the follow on the first turn out of sight and chose "slipped_away" by `who.zone === wo.move &&
p.zone === wo.move`; `src/agents/legality.ts` allowed an attack only on someone in view. Leaving was never defined
apart from titles.

**Owner's rule** (21/11/2026, project thread, answering the question in part 1, option a): "define leaving, if I am
attacking someone and they go through a door I expect that I will go after them". Leaving a place is going through a
way (a doorway, stairs, a window); an open edge between two titles is no way out.

**Fix:** `wentThroughWay` (src/engine/position.ts): the walk between two points passes a way other than a gap. "leaves"
fires on going out of the watcher's senses or through a way. A follow lost from sight after the player saw them through a
way walks on to where they were last seen (the BG-203 lastSeen walk), and is lost only there; "slipped_away" now means no
way was taken since they were last seen, "out_of_sight" that one was. An attack on someone out of sight but in
lastSeen (not seen gone) becomes a move after them to that spot. Spec: ACT-84 and AE6, with the owner's words.

**Verified (part 2):** temporary tests: `wentThroughWay` false over the street's open edge and true through the tavern
door; a follow whose patron was last seen through the front door walks out after them instead of ending (on `main` it
ended at once); an attack on that patron becomes a move to the street, where on `main` legality refused it. s279
(follow), the readied-order tests and the legality tests pass. The temporary tests were deleted.
