# BG-1618

**What was wrong:** Someone the player saw clearly stayed seen clearly for as long as they were anywhere in the title
they were seen in. Any change of point that was not a seen-or-unseen move (pushed, carried, re-placed in the title,
standing up somewhere else in it) kept full sight in the dark across a whole square or field, and a step across a
title's line lost it though they stood a metre from where they were seen.

**Root cause:** `journal.clearSight` (`src/types/actor.ts`) stored a title per person (`Record<id, title>`), and both
checks compared titles: `sightOf` in `src/perception/index.ts` (`clearSight[id] === event.zone`) and `seenActors` in
`src/views/index.ts` (`clearSight[id] === a.zone`). "Stays put" was measured as "stays in the same title". AGENTS.md
rule 8: a title only names a space; it never decides what is near or still where it was.

**What was done:** `clearSight` keeps the point and height (Pt3) where the person was seen clearly (`noteSightings`:
the event's point; `lookAround` and `keepClearSight`: where they stand). `keepsClearSight` in
`src/perception/index.ts` keeps clear sight while they stay within CLEAR_SIGHT_STAY_M (`src/config/perception.ts`,
placeholder, WT14 a) of it, measured by `pointsM` (`src/engine/position.ts`), heights included. Both checks use it.
Save schema 16: MIGRATIONS 15 turns an old title entry into the point of someone still standing in that title and
drops the rest (they are seen afresh at the next look or turn end).

**Other places:** `lastSeen` (views) read the title from clearSight; it now takes `lastKnown`, or the title at the
stored point. `witness.ts` and `playerFound` (quests) read only the keys. The `?fake` fixtures store points.

**Verified:** a throwaway test (deleted): a person seen clearly by a look who shifts 0.3 m stays full sight in
perception and in the view; 1.8 m off in the same title they are half seen. s108 and s177 now give their events
and journal points; save.test covers MIGRATIONS 15.
