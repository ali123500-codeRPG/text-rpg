# BG-1708

**Symptom:** Routes the game walks or words were still picked by the fewest ways, not walked metres. Where the
fewest-ways route is the longer walk, a sound heard from another title was placed "out the side" (the first way of the
fewest-ways route) while the walk there leaves by the front door; whether a move counts as a road trip was judged on a
route the walker would not walk; a ranged-or-melee check before a move looked from a title the walk never enters; and a
walk round a shut way could be called a walk straight in.

**Root cause:** `shortestPath` (src/engine/relations.ts), a breadth-first search counting ways, picked the route in
`src/engine/travel.ts` `tripGoal`, `src/views/index.ts` `whereLabel`, `src/engine/resolve.ts` `rangedHere` and, through
`wayPath`, `roundShut` (BG-148). BG-1614 (#1694) moved NPC routes to the walk over the floor plan (`walkRoute`) but
left these callers. AGENTS.md rule 8: nearness and routes are never link counts.

**Why it caused the symptom:** the walk itself (`walkLeg`) goes by metres, so wherever the fewest ways and the fewest
metres part (a long lane joining two places directly beside a short street with one more way), these callers named or
judged a route other than the one walked.

**Fix:** a new `routeOf` in `src/engine/position.ts` gives the titles of the walker's own walk (`walkRoute` with the
walker: round the ways it knows are shut, as `walkLeg` walks). `tripGoal`, `whereLabel` (now from the viewer's point to
the event's point), `rangedHere` and `roundShut` use it. `wayPath` had no other caller and is removed; s361 (SZ7) now
checks the same body-size rule on `routeOf`. Spec: the scenarios.md barriers line and travel.md TV1.

**Other affected places:** `src/ui/minimap.ts` (the Map: which titles are reachable, ~45) still asks `shortestPath`; the
Map belongs to the D-1558 thread and is left to it. `shortestPath` stays for it (and its s189 tests).

**Verified:** a temporary test on the village with a long lane added from the common room to the gate reproduced it:
`shortestPath` went by the lane, `routeOf` (now asked by all four callers) by the street, 45.2 m. The
temporary test was deleted.
