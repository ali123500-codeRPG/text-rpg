# BG-1617

**What was wrong:** When a world is carried on, the foes who loot the fallen character were everyone standing and
hostile in the same place, with those in the fallen's title first. A hostile who never saw the fall, at the far end of
a big place, could loot the body, and a foe standing 2 m away in the next title came after one 80 m off in the same
title.

**Root cause:** `lootTheFallen` in `src/save/world.ts` chose looters by title (`placeId` equal, then `zone` equal
first). AGENTS.md rule 8: a title only names a space, never who is near. The rule for any other body
(`lootFoundBodies` in `src/engine/loot.ts`, D-253 b) already chose by who found or saw the body (`bodiesFound`) and
sorted by `distM`; the save path had its own second rule.

**What was done:** One rule for who loots: `lootersOf` in `src/engine/loot.ts` (everyone standing who found the body
or saw it fall, nearest by `distM` first). `lootFoundBodies` uses it with `takesFrom`; `lootTheFallen` uses it with
"hostile to them" (WT13 a). The spec line in Part 4 (what is saved) now says so.

**Other places:** `stripBody` is called only from these two. No other looting path picks by title.

**Verified:** s272 (BG-1617): a hostile in the same title who never saw the fall takes nothing, and the nearer of two
witnesses takes the coin. The existing WP6 and BG-1478 cases now set that their foe saw the fall.
