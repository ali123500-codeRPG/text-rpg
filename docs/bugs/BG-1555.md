# BG-1555

**What was wrong:** Someone the player perceives now at less than full sight (half seen in the dark, or a suspected sneak) was drawn twice on the map, which showed as a "+1" group of one person with themself, and was offered twice under the deck's "Talk to whom?".

**Root cause:** `PlayerView.lastSeen` (`lastSeen` in `src/views/index.ts`) leaves out only people seen in full, on purpose: a half-seen seller stays there so their known wares travel with them (BG-155, `tests/cases/s245-buy-from-half-seen-seller.test.ts`). So a half-seen person is in both `actors` and `lastSeen`. `knownMap` (`src/ui/mapData.ts`) added a person for each list, and the deck's Talk listeners (`src/ui/deck.ts`) joined both lists, without dropping the ones already in `actors`. The deck's sellers and the Parser's legality check already dropped them; these two did not.

**What was done:** The map and the Talk list now skip a `lastSeen` entry whose person is in `actors`, as the sellers list does. `lastSeen` itself is unchanged, so BG-155 still holds. Checked with a temporary test (deleted): a suspected sneak on bent-nail was 2 people on the map and 2 Talk picks before, 1 and 1 after. Every other reader of `lastSeen` was checked: `actionWords` only looks a name up, `legality.ts` already keeps the two apart, and `play.ts` builds sets of names.
