# BG-1792

**What was wrong:** A town's strongest person, rolled at Paragon (100) most of the time, filled the Seat of the Great
hook, so the Weaver was told to call them one of the Great. The Great are past the mortal limit (TL26 a), and 100 is the
limit itself.

**Root cause:** Every town roster must hold the Seat of the Great hook (library `rosters`), and its rank comes from
CONFIG.ROSTER_RANKS.seat.town, which is Paragon (TL24 a: a town holds at least a Paragon), one step higher only on
SEAT_STEP_UP_PCT. The hook's text names its holder "past ordinary power" and the Great whatever rank was rolled. Villages
had the same clash and got the Warden hook instead (PQ13 a2); towns did not, so most towns were "held" by a Great who was
not one.

**What was done:** The owner chose GW7 a (Questions hub page "Achievements for the Great (GW)", 21/11/2026): the hook
follows the rank. `siteRoster` (src/land/roster.ts) gives a seat rolled below CONFIG.GREAT_FROM (Beyond) the Warden
hook, never called one of the Great; a seat stepped up to Beyond stays the Seat of the Great. Found while building the
achievements for the Great (#1783, GW5).
