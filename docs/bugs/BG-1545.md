# BG-1545

**What was wrong:** The Parser's view of a turn did not match the Narrator's view of the moment before it. The headman and a patron the opening named in full sight at 19 m were a half-seen "someone" to the Parser; a slime the Narrator called "a slime" at 9 m was "someone" to the Parser and also listed in `lastSeen` as gone from its spot; and a walk to the lower field read 29 m to go where the Narrator had 9 m.

**Root cause:** Three faults, all in how the turn-start `PlayerView` (`src/views/index.ts`) is built.
1. Seen clearly at a turn's end was not kept. `seenActors` judges full sight from the turn's events (their visibility) and this turn's make-out rolls; the Narrator's view is built from those. `advance()` (`src/agents/game.ts`) then empties `state.events` and moves the turn on, so the make-outs (`src/perception/madeOut.ts`, keyed to the turn) go too, and the next turn's views judge everyone as a still figure (Low visibility: clear only to 15 m). The only bridge is `journal.clearSight` (Q17), but `noteSightings` writes it per event, where each event happened: someone seen clearly only by where they ended the turn (a slime whose earlier leg was half seen), and everyone in the opening (`openingEvents` never calls `noteSightings`), was never written.
2. `goneFromSpot` marked someone gone when their last spot was in sight, without checking that the player still perceives them. A half-seen person stays in `lastSeen` on purpose (BG-155), and a small body (a slime) standing at a spot 9 m off is half seen while the spot itself counts as in sight, so the same id was "someone" in `actors` and `gone` in `lastSeen`. The Narrator's `notThere` used the same check.
3. `walkingOf` measured a walk to a title to the title's centre, but a walk ends as soon as the player gets into the title (`walkThere` in `src/engine/resolve.ts`); the Narrator's `toGo` measures to that edge. A walk to someone without an action was also measured to their title, not to them.

**What was done:** `keepClearSight` (views) records everyone the player sees clearly at the end of each turn, the opening included, into `journal.clearSight`; `finishTurn` calls it before the save, so a reloaded story starts the next turn the same. `goneFromSpot` is never true for someone the player perceives now. `walkingOf` measures to the walk's spot when it has one (as `moveGoal` does), else to where the path gets into the title (`wayLeftM` with `enter`). Checked with temporary tests (deleted): a guard seen clearly at 20 m by a turn's events was "someone" at the next turn start before and named after; a half-seen slime at its own last spot was `gone` before and not after. On the playtest save, a walk to the lower field read 21 m before (to the field's centre) and 1 m after, the 1.25 m the walk has left.
