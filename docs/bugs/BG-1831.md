# BG-1831

**What was wrong:** A long walk across open land with no road on its route rolled no encounters, however long it was.
The owner chose D-245 a (Questions hub page "Open parked items 4", 21/11/2026): "each hour off the road rolls at the
wild rate (ROAD_ENCOUNTER_PCT wild, ×2 at night), wild roster, one rate for all wild land".

**Root cause:** D-245 a was built only inside `travel()` (src/engine/travel.ts, #1805), which runs only for a trip.
`tripGoal` makes a trip only of a route that uses a road, so a walk that never touches a road is a walk on the world
clock (`runStretch` in src/engine/resolve.ts), and nothing there counted minutes or rolled. ACT-244 then recorded the gap
as the rule instead of building the rest of the owner's answer.

**What was done:** The roll moved into one shared function, `legEncounter` (travel.ts), which trips and walks both call.
`runStretch` counts each step of a walk that ends on open land off the road (`wildGround`; roads too since the follow-up below) into `Actor.roadMin`, as a
trip does, and a full TRAVEL_LEG_MIN rolls at the wild rate (× NIGHT_ENCOUNTER_MULT in the dark, with the night event
roll modifier). A meeting stops the walk (`stretch_stopped`, why `encounter`) and breaks the scenario with the encounter,
as on a trip; the Weaver sees the danger as wild and the wild roster for the land cover there, as before. ACT-244 and
Travel, Legs updated. Test: tests/cases/s432-wild-walk-encounters.test.ts, on a region drawn by the land generator.

**Follow-up (#1838 review):** the first fix counted only open land, so a walk along a road that was refused as a trip
(TV1: someone hostile knows of the player) counted nothing there. The owner turned that down (21/11/2026: "I don't like
the point you made about me knowing that a foe is watching me, sounds like it breaks my rules"), so road minutes on a
walk now count too.
