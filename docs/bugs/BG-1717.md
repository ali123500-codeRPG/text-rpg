# BG-1717

**What was wrong:** Furniture hid a target only when it stood in the target's own title (PC2, `objectHidden`). A counter
on the sight line just across a title line hid nothing, though the same counter in the target's title hid over half of
it.

**Root cause:** `objectHidden` in `src/ruleset/objectCover.ts` took its pieces from `furnitureIn(state, zone.id)`, the
target's title, so a title decided which pieces could hide (AGENTS.md rule 8).

**Why it caused the symptom:** A piece in any other title was never measured against the sight line or the body, so it
gave no cover, wherever it stood.

**What was done:** `objectHidden` now takes every piece on the target's floor (`state.furniture`), each boxed by
`pieceBox` as `src/engine/sleep.ts` already does, and the sight line and boxes decide as before. Its unused title
argument is gone (the caller in `src/ruleset/gear.ts` and `s383-object-cover` updated). The spec (PC2) already words the
rule by the sight line and distance, so it needs no change.

**Also affected:** BG-1672 was the same cause in `openingShown`. Nothing else in `src/ruleset`, `src/perception` or
`src/views` picks cover pieces by title.

**Verified:** A temporary case test (a counter 1 m in front of a target, shot from 4 m: once all in one title, once with
the target just across the title line) gave 0.56 and 0 before the fix, 0.56 and 0.56 after; deleted before merge.
`s383-object-cover` and `s384-windows` pass.
