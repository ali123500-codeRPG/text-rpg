# BG-1683

**What was wrong:** The Map's scale bar read "NaN m", with no bar, at some zooms: on the dev page's bent-nail stage at
the 5 km step (MAP-10).

**Root cause:** The bar takes the longest round length (1, 2.5 or 5 of a power of ten) up to a fifth of the view's
width. Its power of ten was `10 ** Math.floor(Math.log10(span))`. When the span is a hair under a power of ten
(999.99… m, from the zoom's floating-point sum), `Math.log10` rounds it up to 3, so the power was 1000, larger than the
span itself; none of 5, 2.5 or 1 times it fits under the span, and the length became `NaN` (`render` in
`src/ui/planView.ts`).

**What was done:** A power of ten above the span is taken one step down (100 here), so the bar is always one of the
round lengths up to the span. Checked on the dev page at every zoom step of the three test stages and the dev world.
