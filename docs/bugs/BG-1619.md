# BG-1619

**What was wrong:** Everyone standing in a title that had an unbuilt way out was simulated in full every turn, at any
distance from that way and whatever they were doing: a farmer at the far end of a big field with a road out of it got
an order, acted and made events like someone beside the player.

**Root cause:** `relevantActors` in `src/engine/tiers.ts` built `edge`, the set of titles with a frontier way, and
counted anyone with `edge.has(a.zone)`. The rule it stood for (NW3: someone about to step into unbuilt ground must be
simulated, since the catch-up stops at the frontier and would never step in) was written as "in a title next to it".
AGENTS.md rule 8: a title only names a space; it never decides who is near a way.

**What was done:** `reachesFrontier` in `src/engine/tiers.ts`: only someone heading for a frontier way (a head routine
whose `dest` is that way) whose walked metres (`pathM`) to the way's point (`frontierPoint`) are within one turn's walk
(`turnMetres(state, a, 'move')`) is simulated for it (WT15 a). The catch-up (`catchUp` in `src/engine/worldClock.ts`)
stopped such a walker as soon as it entered the edge title, which under the new rule could leave it far from the way
and never simulated; it now walks on to the way's point (`desiredState` returns the frontier it heads for).

**Other places:** `headGoal` in `src/minds/routines.ts` picks which edge title to head for by link counts
(`mapPath` length), not metres; left as it is (minds see only MapZone links, the cause behind BG-1614).

**Verified:** a throwaway test (deleted): a head walker 37 m from the way in explore is not simulated and someone idle
in the same title is not either; the turn's catch-up walks it to within a turn's walk, and next turn it is simulated
and steps in (frontier_wait). s208 now stands its walkers a step from the way, as its NW3 cases mean.
