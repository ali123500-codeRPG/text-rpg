# BG-1615

**What was wrong:** An NPC's known map and the Weaver's ground were the whole settlement (or road) by its title, plus
everything within PLAN_VIEW_M of the middle of a title. A guard at one end of a long town knew every lane of it, and the
Weaver's cast was everyone standing in any title of those settlements, however far (AGENTS.md rule 8: a title only names
a space; it never decides who is near or what can be reached).

**Root cause:** `nearbyZones` in `src/scenario/stage.ts` took every title of the top-level place over each place it was
given (`topPlace`), and measured the rest from title to title (`distOrNull` between their middles). The NPC map
(`mapOf` in `src/views/index.ts`) passed the NPC's own place, and the Weaver's view passed the target's and the player's
places; the Weaver's cast was then "stands in a title of that set".

**Why it caused the symptom:** a place's titles were in by name, at any distance, and the cast followed the titles.

**What was done:** `nearbyZones(state, from)` takes points. A title is in when its nearest point lies within
CONFIG.PLAN_VIEW_M of one of them by the walking path: 0 standing in it, else the walk to where it gets in (`wayLeftM`,
enter), or failing that to its middle; titles whose nearest edge is past PLAN_VIEW_M in a straight line are skipped
unmeasured (a walk is never shorter). The NPC's map is measured from its own point. The Weaver's ground is measured from
the player's point and the points where the ways out to the target frontier are left; its cast is everyone within
PLAN_VIEW_M of those points (`distM`), and the title each of them stands in joins the ground so no one is left out of its
place. A head_to walk no longer needs its destination on the NPC's map: its goal is decided from the state
(`views/ways.ts` `headGoal`), so a walk to somewhere past PLAN_VIEW_M still goes there.

**Other places:** the chase range (CH5, `engine/chase.ts`) still takes the titles of the NPC's own place and of the
scenario's ground places by the owner's ground rule. At the story's start or an arrival (the player stands nowhere yet) the
Weaver's ground is measured from the middles of the target place's own titles, where the player is about to be.

**Verified:** `tests/cases/s046-saved-zone-reentry.test.ts` now gives points: the village round the tavern is in by the
walk, and ground about 250 m off stays out. The head-to, frontier-crossing, minds, Weaver-view and NPC-view tests pass.
