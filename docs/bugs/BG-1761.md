# BG-1761

**What was wrong:** In the library viewer at desktop width, the Monitor lizard's race card showed its Natural armor
"Covers" chips one letter wide, running down past the bottom of the card. The same card broke "Unintelligent" across
two lines, and the chips showed raw part ids (`left_foreleg`) instead of part names.

**Root cause:** `.lv-armor` in `src/ui/libraryViewer.css` put the 3D figure beside its fields in a fixed two-column grid
(`auto minmax(0, 1fr)`) and stacked them only on a phone-width screen (`@media (max-width: 760px)`). A screen's width
does not say how wide a card is: at 1440px the Races grid fits three cards of about 416px, the figure column took
about 180px, and the fields were left about 200px. The Natural armor value is a nested box with its own key column,
so its chips got about 15px; `overflow-wrap: anywhere` on `.lv-v` then broke each chip after every letter. Rendered
before the fix at 1440px: the chips ended at 978px against the card's 976px right edge and ran past its bottom. The
race card also passed `naturalArmor` to the generic `val()`, which shows strings as they are stored, so the covers
kept their ids.

**What was done:** `.lv-armor` is a wrapping flex row: the fields need 300px beside the figure or the figure moves
above them, centred on its own line, so the layout follows the card's own room at any screen width (the phone rule is
no longer needed). The race card names the parts its natural armor covers, as the gear-type card already did.
Rendered at 1440, 1024 and 390px: every card on Gear, Gear types, Races and Body types stays inside its edges, 0px
page overflow; the wide Body types cards keep the figure beside the fields.
