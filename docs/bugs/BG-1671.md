# BG-1671

**Symptom:** Two checks in the turn engine went by title, not metres. A blow on someone hemmed in on every side, but
standing in the next title over, was lost as out\_of\_reach, never no\_room. And a give or take still out of reach after
the actor closed in only had the other side step up when the two stood in different titles and a way joined those
titles with the actor's hand at it: someone 11.6 m off across an open title edge (a street and its square), or across
a room, never stepped up, and the give came to nothing.

**Root cause:** `src/engine/resolve.ts` `doAttack` asked `roomAround` only when `t.zone === a.zone`, and
`stepUpToTake` returned false when `t.zone === a.zone` (and `doTake` called it only when `t.zone !== a.zone`); it then
walked the other side to `wayPoint(t.zone, a.zone)` only when the actor stood within grip of `wayPoint(a.zone, t.zone)`.
Both date from before BG-1616 (#1673), when spacing and `roomAround` themselves went by title. AGENTS.md rule 8: a
title never decides who is near or what can be reached.

**Why it caused the symptom:** across a title edge the hemmed-in test never ran, so the reason fell to out\_of\_reach;
and the step-up's title tests skipped every case where the two shared a title, or where no way between their titles
was at the actor's hand.

**Fix:** `doAttack` asks `roomAround` whenever the target is in sight (`sightDistM` finite: `roomAround`'s free spots
need a clear line from the walker), whatever its title. `stepUpToTake` runs on any give or take still out of reach in
metres: the other side, if willing, walks toward the actor until their grips meet; when the next way on that walk is not
open to them (bars, a grate, a shut door: the new `gateToward` in `src/engine/position.ts`, reusing `gateOf`), it walks
to its side of that way instead, if the actor's grip is at the far side, as before (BF12). Spec: action-economy BF35
line and BF34 give/take line.

**Other affected places:** title tests still in `src/engine/resolve.ts`: `closeInOn` reaches over a counter only when
`near.at.zone !== a.zone` (`reachesAcross`, BG-62), `behind` (BG-183) and `takeDash` (BG-284, "only within the zone")
decide by title. Left for their own fixes.

**Verified:** a temporary test reproduced both on `main` (a give 11.6 m across an open title edge: out\_of\_reach; an
attack through a locked grate with every spot taken: out\_of\_reach) and passed with the fix (the coin changes hands;
no\_room). s250 (BF12 grate and solid door) still passes. The temporary test was deleted.
