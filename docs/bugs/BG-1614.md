# BG-1614

**What was wrong:** Code-run NPCs chose where to walk by how many ways (doors, links) lay between titles, not by metres
(AGENTS.md rule 8). A shed 3 m from a threat and a lane 300 m away both scored two doors, so a broken NPC could flee
toward the foe, a chaser or stalker could take the route with fewer doors but more metres, a routine walk took the
fewest-doors way, and a head_to walk bound for a frontier picked the edge title with the fewest doors to it.

**Root cause:** The routines in `src/minds/routines.ts` see only their ActorView, never GameState, and the view gave
them only `map: MapZone[]`: titles and their links. `mapPath` (a breadth-first count of links) was the only distance
they could compute, so `headGoal`, `followMove`, `routineOrder`, `fleeOrder`, `stalkOrder` and the close-in step of the
fight list all ranked and routed by it. The same count chose the route in `src/engine/chase.ts` `metresToGround` (ZR12):
`shortestPath` picked the fewest-ways route to each ground title, and only that route's last way was then measured.

**What was done:** The walks are decided by metres where the state is, and the routines read the answer:
`src/views/ways.ts` builds `ActorView.ways` (in `buildActorView`) and `self.headEdge` (in every view and `routineView`; since BG-1615 `self.headGoal`).
`ways.next` is the first title on the NPC's walk (`walkRoute` over the floor plan, round the ways it knows are shut) to
where its routine wants it and to where it last knew each one it hunts (its last known point when it has one).
`ways.flee` is the flight's title: toward its exit or refuge while that leads away from the nearest threat, else
through the way out whose point is farthest from that threat by walk (`pathM`) among those that lead away. The nearest
threat is by straight line when it perceives them now (`distM`), else by walk to where it last knew them. `headEdge` is
the edge title nearest by walk to the way into the frontier. `mapPath` is gone. `walkRoute` gained `round` (no
fall-back through a way the walker knows is shut), so an NPC that knows its only way is locked still idles as
unreachable (CS22). `metresToGround` now measures the walk to the outer side of every way into the ground. Agents never
see `ways` (stripped in `src/agents/format.ts`; the Voice's view never has it).

**Other places:** `shortestPath` (fewest ways) still picks titles in `src/engine/resolve.ts` (an order's move, owned by
another thread), `src/engine/travel.ts`, `src/views/index.ts` (route words) and `src/ui/minimap.ts` (the Map): left for
their own issues. The chase range itself (CH5) stays a set of titles by the owner's ground rule; only its near edge is
in metres.

**Verified:** The minds unit tests run on the real ways: a broken patron flees into the cellar, away from the player,
and cowers when cornered; a broken slime flees through the gate; a guard whose only way to its post is a door it knows
is locked idles as unreachable. The barrier and window-drop checks of the minds' map moved to the walk the minds now
use (round a known-shut way, never down a drop). The head-to, frontier-crossing, chase, ambush and companion case tests
pass unchanged.
