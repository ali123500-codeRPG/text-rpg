# BG-1583

**What was wrong:** Power and combat level dropped when an actor sheathed its weapon. In the AA-108 simulated playtest the player put the arming sword away on turn 7 and power fell from 76 to 63 while the level rose from 1 to 5; from the save, combat level 12.35 and power 62.5 sheathed against 41.15 and 133.7 drawn. Power also sets defeat XP, defeat quests, essence drops, the outclass check and the stronger-foe achievements, and each fighter's power is kept from the moment it joins a fight, so an NPC that was sheathed then stayed under-rated for the whole fight.

**Root cause:** `freshCopy` in `src/engine/combatLevel.ts`, "a copy of the actor as it would start a fight", reset condition, marks and holds but kept the inventory's grips as they were. The equation's sheet (`readSheet`) then reads attacks through `usableModes` and the defense through `usableDefense`/`blockItem`/`parryItem` in `src/ruleset/gear.ts`, which take held items only, so a sheathed sword and a slung shield counted as fists and dodge. The cache key (`sheetKey`) held each item's grips too, so every draw and sheathe worked the level out afresh with the other answer. Every reader of power (player and NPC alike) goes through `combatLevel`, so they all shared the fault. In the test village the guard rated 59.1 drawn and 27.9 sheathed; the player 44.0 and 5.5.

**What was done:** PQ4 a: the actor is rated as drawn. `freshCopy` (which now takes the state) stows whatever is in hand, finds every most-full set of the weapons and shields the actor carries that its grips can hold, and puts the set that rates highest in hand (with one such set, that set, with no extra rating). `sheetKey` leaves grips out, so drawing and sheathing keep the cached level. `usableModes` and the defense functions are unchanged: in a real fight what is held is what is used. The spec (PROG-74, PROG-81) and the equations prose say so, and the gear-rating case test now checks that sheathing leaves power as it was while taking armor off still lowers it.
