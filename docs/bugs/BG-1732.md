# BG-1732

**Symptom:** The step up planned before the turn, for talking to, trading with or handing something to someone
(`approachMove`), chose where the walk ends by title. A move toward someone in another title was kept on the actor's
side of a counter whenever any low wall stood between the two titles, wherever the counter lay between the two people.
A move over a counter toward someone standing on the actor's own side ended beside them only when they shared the
actor's title; anyone on that side but in the next title over was walked past, behind the counter.

**Root cause:** `src/engine/merge.ts` `approachMove` branched on `t.zone === here` and asked `reachesAcross(here,
m.toZone)` / `reachesAcross(here, t.zone)` (src/engine/relations.ts: a low wall anywhere between two titles). It dates
from when the title was an actor's only location (BG-62, BG-87). AGENTS.md rule 8: a title never decides who is near or
what can be reached.

**Why it caused the symptom:** the title test says whether two spaces touch at a counter, not whether the counter
stands between these two people, so the plan kept or dropped the "stay on this side" walk by which titles they stood in.

**Fix:** `approachMove` asks `reachOverFrom` (src/engine/position.ts, from BG-1728) whether a counter, fence or rail
stands between the actor and the target. `reachOverFrom` now also counts a counter whose gap the walk to them goes
through, so resolve.ts close-in and the plan ask one rule. A move into their title with a counter between ends on the
actor's side, at the way (as before, for resolve.ts to close in and reach over), unless the player goes behind it; a
move over a counter to someone with no counter between them ends beside them, in whatever title they stand. The marker
spot names the way between the titles, so it is used only where such a way exists. `reachesAcross` had no caller left and
is removed. Spec: action-economy BF35 line.

**Other affected places:** none in approachMove. Remaining title tests in src/engine and src/minds that decide nearness
rather than naming a place: resolve.ts ~3285 (a bed is reached only in its own title, besides BED_REACH_M), ready.ts ~42
(a readied "leaves" fires on a title change), resolve.ts ~1735 (a lost follow is "slipped_away" by title). Left for
their own fixes.

**Verified:** by reading: the counter village draws its titles so that the title test and the counter between the two
people agree in every case tried (four placements across, along and away from the counter, before and after the fix
gave the same moves), so the divergence needs a layout the fixtures lack. s202, s210, s217, s385 (talk moves), s276
(BG-183), s214 and s250 pass.
