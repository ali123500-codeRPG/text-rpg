# BG-1507

**What was wrong:** Three times in one story the World builder's plan was refused with "cast rg_0_0_r3.g1: names no template, composition or actor" (and the same for two groups on another road), so the ground it was building needed a repair round each time.

**Root cause:** A road's groups and a settlement's hooks are roster entries with keys such as `rg_0_0_r3.g1` (`src/land/roster.ts`). A cast entry takes who it is from a roster entry only when it names the key in `roster` (`fillRosterAndGroups`, `src/scenario/validate.ts`). The World builder cast the group under the roster key itself and left `roster` out, so nothing said who the entry was, and the validator refused it.

**What was done:** `fillRosterAndGroups` now reads a cast entry keyed by a roster key, that gives no roster, template, composition, actor or group, as that roster entry. An entry that says who it is keeps that, and a key that is no roster entry is still refused. Covered by the new `s417-roster-cast-by-key`, which fails before the change.
