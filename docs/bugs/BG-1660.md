# BG-1660

**What was wrong:** A title's floor depended on the order titles were listed in. With a yard, a hall level with it, a
tower up from the hall and vaults down from it, listing the deepest vault first put that vault at 0 m and the yard and
hall 12 m up; listing the yard first gave the expected 0 m yard and hall, vaults below.

**Root cause:** `settleFloors` in `src/plan/draw.ts` (PZ7) sets floors from known floors along up and down ways. When
no new title touches a title on a known floor (a story's first titles, or a new building drawn on its own), it set the
first title still unsettled to 0 and settled the rest from it. Which title that was came only from list order, so any
title, a vault or a tower floor, could become "the ground outside" that floors are measured from (GSM-22).

**What was done:** Titles settled from that guessed 0 are kept as one group per guess. Once the group is settled from
its ways, the whole group is moved so that its lowest title in the open (no roof) stands at 0, the ground outside. A
group with no title in the open keeps the old rule (the first listed stands at 0); nothing in it says where the
ground is. Covered by s386 (PZ7, BG-1660): the same keep listed in two orders gets the same floors.
