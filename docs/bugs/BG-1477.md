# BG-1477

**What was wrong:** The hero's take of a pack from a body fell short (BG-1476) for several turns. A bounty-chaser watching then told the hero he had been hitting the dead man for two turns. No one had struck the body, and a character should never count time in turns.

**Root cause:** When someone else's action comes to nothing, the onlooker's view of it (`eventView`, `src/views/index.ts`) held only the kind, `wasted`, and the target. It did not say what was tried for, or why: BG-175 gives the reason to the actor alone. Once the event left the recent window, `earlierLine` turned it into "player wasted a young stranger". In English "wasted" reads as killed or beaten up. The NPC had two such lines, labelled "turn 13:" and "turn 14:", and no speech rule stopped it saying "turns" aloud.

**What was done:** A wasted event seen in full now names what the try went for (`item` in the event view). The earlier line reads "player went for the pack on a young stranger, and it came to nothing" (PM6). Both NPC prompts now say that someone else's wasted event is a try that came to nothing, never a blow that landed. The shared speech rule (`LINE_RULE`, `src/agents/prompts.ts`) now says a character never counts time in turns. Covered by the new `s414-failed-take-seen-as-a-try`, which fails before the change.
