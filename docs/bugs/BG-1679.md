# BG-1679

**What was wrong:** A road bound to a land road ran through the settlement it leaves. In the dev world, the mill road's
4 m band started in the middle of Millbrook's square and ran east over the square and the field edge (the checker:
`MAP-11 zones v_square and m_road partially overlap`, `MAP-11 zones field_edge and m_road partially overlap`). It
showed once roads joined the one map (D-1558); the World view drew a road as a line only.

**Root cause:** A road title bound to a land road (Zone.road, WZ1) takes the whole RoadLine's points as its line, and
`addTitles` in `src/plan/draw.ts` drew its outline as the band along that whole line (`bandOutline`). A RoadLine starts
at its node, which stands on the settlement's site point, and the settlement's titles are laid round that point
(WZ13). So the band began inside the town's ground, against MAP-11 and MAP-18 (never partly over another title).

**What was done:** `addTitles` draws a road along a line only from where the line leaves ground already drawn on the
ground level, at each end (`outOf`), and keeps that shorter line as the title's line. The road and the square it
joins are then placed apart or side by side, and their way is placed as for any two titles (`placeOpening`, BG-7). A
save that already holds such a road keeps it as drawn (MR2, PZ16 b). Covered by the dev world's map check, which now
has 0 findings, and the existing road and travel tests.
