# BG-1728

**Symptom:** Three close-in checks in `src/engine/resolve.ts` went by title. Handing something over a counter to someone
1.5 m along it, the player walked to the counter's gap (the way between the two titles), not straight across from them,
and stood 1.22 m off instead of 0.35 m; a counter, fence or rail standing inside one title was never reached over. A take
with nothing else to do ran over as a dash only when the thing lay in the taker's own title, though the owner ruled a take
is picked up where the run reaches it in any title (MQ22 a).

**Root cause:** `closeInOn` decided to reach over by `near.at.zone !== a.zone && reachesAcross(a.zone, near.at.zone)`
(a low wall anywhere between the two titles) and then walked to `wayPoint` (the way between the titles). `takeDash`
required `near.at.zone === a.zone`. Both date from when an actor's title was its only location (BG-62, BG-284).
AGENTS.md rule 8: a title never decides what can be reached.

**Why it caused the symptom:** the title test says nothing about where the counter lies between the two people, so the
walk went to the way between the titles wherever the target stood; and the dash was withheld by title alone.

**Fix:** a new `reachOverFrom` in `src/engine/position.ts`: the first wall the walking line from the actor to the target
meets; when it is one a grip reaches across (`wallKind.reach`, a gap in it too), the spot on the actor's side of it there.
`closeInOn` walks to it, whatever the titles. `takeDash` no longer tests the title. Spec: action-economy BF35 line.

`behind` (BG-183) is left as it is: it reads the order, not nearness. It is true when the order's move names the
target's title (approachMove keeps such a move only when the player goes behind the counter, `move.behind`), so a title
there names the space the player asked to walk into.

**Other affected places:** `src/engine/merge.ts` `approachMove` still plans the step up by title (`reachesAcross(here,
t.zone)` and `t.zone === here`) before the turn; resolve's close-in now decides where the actor stands, but the planned
move is still chosen by title. Left for its own fix.

**Verified:** a temporary test (the counter village, the trader 1.5 m along the counter) reproduced it on `main` (the
player at the gap, 1.22 m off) and passed with the fix (straight across, 0.35 m). s276 (BG-183), s202, s214, s217, s250,
s354 and s210 pass. The temporary test was deleted.
