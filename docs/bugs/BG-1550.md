# BG-1550

**What was wrong:** In a simulated playtest (AA-108, #1522) the village headman (Nerve Careful, bare-fisted, code-run) and a tavern patron walked some 20 m past the armed player on turns 4 to 6 to punch slimes. Frightened, unarmed villagers charged monsters on sight.

**Root cause:** Two causes, one feeding the other.

1. The Weaver's prompt (`src/agents/prompts.ts`, rule 6 of the stage plan) gave "monsters, a raid" as the examples of a hostile stance and never said that a band's relation is how that band's own members act. Its plan (request 0007) set villagers → slimes hostile while writing the band's temperament as "frightened of the slimes". Hostile is attack on sight, so every villager was engaged.
2. An engaged code-run NPC runs the fight list (`fightList` in `src/minds/routines.ts`): attack a foe in strike reach, else attack a foe it sees (the attack closes in and runs on, BG-255), else close in on where a foe was last seen. Nothing in it reads the actor's Nerve or whether it can fight (SHEET-438: personality does not touch code-run NPCs). So even with the relation set to opposed, a villager a slime strikes turns hostile toward it (`setAttackOverrides` in `src/engine/resolve.ts`) and then closes in on it like a soldier.

Reproduced on `main` (411b2b0) with the village fixture: a headman preset (`TP.headman`, cautious line, Careful, fists only) with a hostile belief about a slime one zone away gets `move … close in on field_mid` from `fallbackOrder`.

**What was done:**

- (a) The Weaver's rule now says a band's relation is how its own members act, every member, armed or not; hostile is only for members who mean to start the fight on meeting; people who fear a monster but do not mean to fight it are opposed. The "monsters" example of hostile is gone. The Weaver spec ("Relations") says the same.
- (b) D-205 b, narrow form (PQ3 c; numbers PQ16 b). `holdsBack` in `src/ruleset/personality.ts` decides it: a code-run NPC on the cautious Nerve line holds back unless it carries a weapon (or has a natural weapon such as a bite or slam) and a fighting skill of `CONFIG.HOLD_BACK_BELOW` or more; one with no weapon and no such skill holds back on any line. The actor view marks it (`self.holdsBack`) and narrows its `withinStrike` to a step and its reach, and the one fight list (`fightList`) then strikes only those, skips skilled rows that strike beyond them or steal up, and flees instead of closing in or searching. Every path that asks the fight list (engaged code-run NPCs, the Play Dead re-pick, a dropped order, hunts during a stretch) gets it from there. Town watch, guards, bandits and beasts still close in.
