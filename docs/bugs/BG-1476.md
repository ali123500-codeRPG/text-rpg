# BG-1476

**What was wrong:** In a carried-on world the hero chose a body's pack in the loot pop-up three turns running. Each time the walk stopped a step short and the take came to nothing (out of reach). A bounty-chaser was standing right beside the body, on the hero's side.

**Root cause:** `reachOf` (`src/engine/resolve.ts`) builds the walk for a pick-up from someone (`from`) with no `toward`. `approach` (`src/engine/position.ts`) looks for another free spot round the target (`ringSpots`) only when the walk has a `toward`. Without one, the walk went straight at the body's point and stopped where the bounty-chaser's body left no room, about 0.3 m outside the hero's grip reach of 0.625 m. Nobody moved, so every turn ended the same way. A search of a body, a hand-over and a filch already pass the body as `toward`.

**What was done:** A pick-up from someone now walks with that someone as `toward`, the same as a search, so the walk steps round anyone in the way. Checked by loading the owner's turn-20 backup into the engine in memory (nothing written), putting the hero back where turn 15 began, and playing the same take. Before the change the hero walked 3.2 m and fell 0.3 m short. After it, the walk goes round and the pack comes off in one turn. Also covered by the new `s413-take-past-a-bystander`, which fails before the change. `s354-take-from-far-body` (BG-284) still passes.
