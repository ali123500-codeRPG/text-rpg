# BG-1506

**What was wrong:** Twice the turn check filed someone whose title changed with no move: a slime from the common room to behind the counter, and a villager from the common room to the kitchen. Each had ended a walk the turn before in the doorway between the two rooms, recorded in the common room, and the next turn, without taking a step, stood in the smaller room beside it.

**Root cause:** A point's title is the smallest title that holds it (`zoneAt`, `src/plan/where.ts`), and a point on a wall that two rooms share lies in both (within `inTitle`'s tolerance). The title someone was in only broke a tie between two titles of the same size. Every turn `placeAll` (`src/engine/position.ts`) gives everyone the title of their point, so someone standing in a doorway was moved into the smaller of the two rooms, with no move event.

**What was done:** `zoneAt` now keeps the title someone is in (`prefer`) when their point lies on the edge of another title: another title wins only when the point is inside it, off its edge. A booth inside a hall still reads as the booth once someone steps into it. Covered by the new `s416-doorway-keeps-title`, which fails before the change.
