# BG-1616

**Symptom:** Placing people, the end of a walk and the room-to-stand check (roomAround) only counted people in the same
title. Two people either side of an open title edge (two streets, a yard and its road) could be placed or stop on top of
each other, 0.1 m apart, and someone hemmed in across a title edge was never found hemmed in.

**Root cause:** `src/engine/position.ts` keyed spacing by title: `placeAll` counted as taken only people with the
placed one's title whose point lay inside it; `othersFor` (feeding `roomAt`, `ringSpots` and `free`) kept only people
with the walker's title; `roomAround` returned true when the two titles differed; `ringSpots` kept only spots inside the
walker's title. This dates from when `Actor.pos` was optional, so the title was the one location every actor had.
D-1623 gave every actor a point and height, which removed that reason, and AGENTS.md rule 8 says a title never decides
who is near or where something is placed.

**Why it caused the symptom:** someone standing 0.1 m across the edge is in another title, so none of these checks saw
them, and the free-spot test passed on a spot their body already filled.

**Fix:** spacing counts everyone whose body overlaps in height with the placed or walking one (`sameFloor`, reusing the
up-and-down gap `sightDistM` already used, now `riseGap`), whatever their title, and measures body to body with
`shapeGap` as before. `roomAround` no longer returns early across titles. `ringSpots` accepts a spot on any title of the
walker's floor (`zoneAt`) with a clear line to it, and a walker stepping onto one in the next title takes that title.
`gripReaches` no longer lets a grip through for the same title: the line and walk tests decide (with no wall between,
they pass as before).

**Other affected places:** `src/engine/resolve.ts` makes the same title test where it asks roomAround (`no_room` only
when `t.zone === a.zone`) and where a take steps up only for someone in another title (`t.zone === a.zone`). That file
belongs to the turn-engine thread (#1645) and is left to it.

**Verified:** a temporary test reproduced all three on `main` (placed 0.6 m apart against PLACE_SPACING_M, a walk ended
touching someone across the edge, roomAround true for a hemmed-in target) and passed with the fix. s171 (ZP1) now also
checks placing and a walk's end across an open title edge.
