# BG-1508

**What was wrong:** A scenario's lost ending, "a rival claimed and cleared the cellar first, or the slimes got into the sacks", had to be left to the Weaver to judge, and was filed as `scenario_ending_judged` (SC7), because no goal check could tell who cleared a place or that a foe got somewhere.

**Root cause:** The ending checks (`checkMet`, `src/engine/scenarioEnd.ts`) had a clear check that is met whoever clears the place, and a reach check for the player only. Nothing counted whose side did the clearing, and nothing checked anyone else reaching a place.

**What was done:** The owner chose to add both checks. A clear ending may give `by` (you or other): the side that downed or broke most of the foes seen standing in the place cleared it, a tie being yours. Each foe goes to the side that last attacked it, so it counts no matter how far off the attacker stood. A foe nobody attacked counts for neither side. Your side is you, your band and anyone following you. A reach ending may name `actor` or `band`: it is met when that person, or any member of that band, stands in the place up and unbroken. The validator checks the new fields, and the Weaver's prompt describes them. A clear with no `by`, and a reach with no actor or band, work as before. Covered by the new `s418-rival-clear-and-reach`.
