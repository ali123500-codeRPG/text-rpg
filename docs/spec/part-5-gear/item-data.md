### 7. Item data

- **ItemDef** (library entry or story blueprint) holds everything fixed about an item. **ItemInstance** (story) holds only what changes in play. Part 4 §9 has both types.
- **Stackable items** (coins, potions) are one instance with a quantity, merged by item id, never equipped as a stack. Everything else is one instance per object.
- **bulk** 0–3 on every item. No carry limit for now: capacity is infinite (D-84).
- **Durability** only for pooled items: shield parts and armor pools. Repair is deferred.
- **Consumables** are a flag: the item is removed on use. Their effects wait for Healing and Status effects.
- **Coins** are a stackable library item, bulk 0. **Value** (src/ruleset/value.ts) is the coin an item's benefit numbers are worth, summed; only consumables and items with no benefit numbers (coin, curio, key, containers) carry a hand-set value, and priced items carry none (IR5, Ali 16/11/2026). A weapon's damage counts as damage a second (IR4): the class's damage (+ the mode's damageAdj), grown with the item's need (the damage step's own growth), over the seconds its attack takes in the Action times table (a blow by class; a shot plus any spanning), best mode, at ITEM_VALUE.dps coin each (value.ts `modeDps`). The class hit bonus is not priced, since it trades against damage. The same rows set how long an attack order takes (actionTime.ts `attackRowIds`).
- **Concealed items** stay out of other actors' views while held or worn, until used in an event that actor perceives; that observer's Belief.revealed then keeps it. Accessories are hidden by default unless tagged visible.
- **Minting** (for now): a variant = a base library item + quality (QUALITY\_LEVELS: Passable, Sound, Exceptional, Flawless) + public name and look. Code reads the tier's bonuses and writes a story blueprint (gear stats scale with need: a weapon's hit QUALITY\_HIT\_FACTOR × need × quality; armor's reduction ARMOR\_FACTOR × need × ARMOR\_WEIGHT\_SOAK[weight] + ARMOR\_QUALITY\_FACTOR × need × quality; a shield's pool POOL\_BASE + POOL\_FACTOR × need + POOL\_QUALITY\_FACTOR × need × quality; a damage boost QUALITY\_DAMAGE\_FACTOR × need × quality × DAMAGE\_CLASS\_FACTOR[class] × DAMAGE\_TREE\_FACTOR[melee or ranged], fractions kept; tools add QUALITY\_TABLE.tool's skill bonus by quality, +5/+10/+15/+20), within the per-scenario minted cap. A minted item with no close library match uses the nearest generic base: improvised\_light, improvised\_medium or improvised\_heavy for an odd weapon, curio (misc, no effects) for a thing with no game effect (Ali, 07/11/2026, loot L2).
- **Improvised items** (Ali, 07/11/2026, loot L3): when the player fights with a scenario object not in the view, the Parser names an improvised base by size and a short name. Code writes a story blueprint (that base, the given name as name and look), puts it in the player's inventory and swaps it into hand before the attack. Improvised bases roll the Improvisation skill (Combat – Unarmed), carry hitAdj IMPROVISED\_HIT\_ADJ (*placeholder*, −10) and no kit bonus.
- **Boon** (Ali, 07/11/2026, loot V8): a reward's worth to the player. An item's Boon is its hand-set boon when it has one, else its value ÷ BOON\_COIN (20); a reward's Boon is that × qty, × BOON\_QUALITY (2/3/5/8) for a minted item of quality 1/2/3/4. Fractions are allowed. A plan's rewards together stay within BOON\_BUDGET by gain: none 0, minor 2, moderate 5, major 10, extreme 20. Boon is the only limit on rewards (no count cap). Only Weaver rewards count: NPCs' own template gear does not. All numbers are *placeholders*, to tune after playtests. The Weaver's item catalog lists each item's boon.
- **Equip requirements** (Ali, 08/11/2026, EQ1–EQ13; `src/ruleset/requirements.ts`). Each weapon, armor and shield has an equipment level from EQUIP\_LEVELS (Crude, Common, Fine, Superior, Masterwork, Beyond, Mythic, Transcendent, Divine), which asks for EQUIP\_NEED true skill (0/25/50/75/100/150/200/300/400, *placeholder*). Past Divine the levels go on as Divine+, Divine++ (keys divine\_1, divine\_2), each EQUIP\_BEYOND\_STEP (100) more, so gear never runs out of levels (IR3, Ali 16/11/2026; the levels past Masterwork are the one ladder past 100 shared with task grades and ranks, Part 1 Layer 2). Read them through equipPos, equipAt, equipName (src/scales.ts) and equipNeed (src/ruleset/requirements.ts). The skill comes from the item's gear class (`gearClass`, a GC id in the library `gearClasses` table): the class sets which skill an item asks for and the level sets how much, so a new item only names a class and a level. The classes are light, medium and heavy melee and ranged weapons, improvised weapons, light, medium and heavy armor, and shields; an item with no class asks for nothing. Each class is tagged by use (`use`: melee, ranged, improvised, armor or shield), so a melee weapon stays melee gear even when thrown (Ali, 10/11/2026); the QE2 melee/ranged damage factor reads this tag. True skill counts, and a stand-in counts at its usual share (EQ3). A minted item of quality 3 or 4 is one level above its base, with no top (EQ11, IR3). Every gear class but improvised has a Common and a Fine starter piece; **Gear types** (IR23, library `gearTypes`, GT ids): every weapon and armor piece is a gear class (skill and weight) plus a gear type plus a level. The type holds the reach or coverage, from its standard real piece, and every piece of the type takes it (pieces round to their type; a piece never states its own): melee Knife 1.0 m, Short 1.3, Sword 1.6, Great 2.3, Pole 2.8 (blade + 0.7 m arm); ranged Short bow 50, Flatbow 60, Recurve 70, Longbow 90, Warbow 150, Light crossbow 60, Stirrup crossbow 70, Heavy crossbow 80 (effective range against a man); armor Cuirass (torso), Coat (torso + arms), Half suit (+ legs), Full harness (+ head, neck), Helmet (head), Helmet with neck. A throw keeps the throw table (THROW_M); improvised weapons and shields have no type. Every gear class but improvised also has a Superior piece (the best real ones: rapier, longsword, greataxe, Turkish composite bow, warbow, crossbow, jack of plates, coat of plates with mail, plate harness, heater shield; reach and coverage cited on each row) and a Masterwork piece tagged 'fantasy' (made past any real smith; it keeps its real form's reach and coverage, only its level makes it stronger; "peerless" names are placeholders) (IR2). Beyond and up have none yet. The library viewer's Gear tab lists every item by level. **Item families** (IR30-IR33, library `itemFamilies`, IF ids; ItemDef `family`, set when the library loads): every equipment piece names its family. Ordinary gear is **Mundane** (IR30 b): it takes every number from its level (need) through one factor each (kit bonus EQUIP\_FACTOR × need, armor ARMOR\_FACTOR × need, shield pool POOL\_BASE + POOL\_FACTOR × need, accessory ACCESSORY\_FACTOR × need), plus its gear class (skill, damage, weight, Dodge cost) and gear type (reach, coverage); a Crude piece (need 0) stops nothing (multiplier 1); nothing is set by hand: no stated bonus, pool, value or Boon (IR31; test s342). The plate harnesses' fixed head pool went with it, and the basket-hilted sword lost its +5 Parry (a plain Fine sword now). A minted piece stays Mundane: its quality adds factor × need × quality on top (IR32). A special family (Threadbound; Relic, parked as D-167) stands on a Mundane base of its class, type and level and adds its own extras, which grow by the family's own rule (Threadbound: by Rank) (IR33). The viewer's Gear tab has a pane per family. Anyone can equip anything (EQ2); every roll with an item you fall short on is one outcome step worse per EQUIP\_SHORT\_STEP (10) points short or part of it (EQ12 c), and worn armor you fall short on reaches Dodge and stealth rolls. Points above the requirement do nothing for now (AR4 d, Ali 09/11/2026). An armor skill grows from hits (AR5): a hit on a part your worn armor covers marks the armor skill for its weight when the attacker's true skill or the armor's requirement is within MARK\_WINDOW of your raw or true armor skill (either one, Ali 09/11/2026).
- **Containers** (Ali, 13/11/2026, Containers and Filching CT1–CT7; `src/engine/containers.ts`). A container is an item that holds items (ItemDef.container): a chest or crate (IT44, IT45, fixed: it stands in a zone and is never picked up), a sack (IT43, carried or set down) and a pack (IT42, worn). ItemInstance.contents holds what is inside, owner whose it is, open whether its lid stands open, and lock, grade, beyond and work a lock like a barrier's (BA2).
  - **Opening** (CT3 a). The action open, within grip reach (NEEDS open: grip), is the turn's action: the container stands open and its contents show in every view of it. Taking an item out is a pick-up (no from needed). A locked one opens with a key naming it (ItemInstance.opens) and stays shut without one; the action pick on the container's id picks its lock exactly as on a way (Lockpicking at its grade, default minor). One's own carried sack is always open to one.
  - **Theft** (CT4 a, CT5 a). Opening, picking or taking from a container whose owner (else its carrier) is someone else, up and not yielded to the taker, is theft. Theft waits for the Filching contest (FL3, and the noise and hearing contest asked on the page as TN1–TN4), so for now it is refused (wasted, reason theft). A downed owner's containers open freely, and so does the pack of one yielded to the taker (its stowed items only; held and worn gear stays theirs).
  - **Worn packs** (CT7 b; CT7b a recommended, awaiting Ali). A worn pack (equippedIn pack) holds its wearer's stowed items: they are its contents, with no list of its own. Every new character starts wearing a plain pack (starterKit). Taking a pack off its downed or yielded wearer moves their stowed items into it; one who wears no pack puts a picked-up pack on, and what it held joins their stowed items. Another pack is carried inside the one worn, its contents kept. Others see a worn pack (where pack); what it holds shows only once it is opened. No capacity limit (CT6 a, D-84).
  - **On screen** (Ali, 13/11/2026). The Inventory shows a carried container as its own pack tile; its name button opens what it holds in place in a dashed box under it, nested containers inside theirs (SD11). Clicking one shows where it is and Take it out, which drafts "I take the healing draught out of my sack" in the Play box (a pick-up; nothing is sent until Send; src/ui/pages.ts insideOf, kit.ts playDraft). A worn pack's contents are the stowed items the pack already shows. Clicking a loose item while a container is carried shows Put in, one button per carried container kind ("Put it in your sack"), which drafts "I put the bandage in my sack" in the Play box (a stow into it; nothing is sent until Send; src/ui/pages.ts putInto). A container never offers to go into another, and something already inside one offers Take it out instead. The same orders come by drag and drop on the Inventory's pack tiles (src/ui/pages.ts dropOrder): drag a loose item onto a carried container's tile, its open box or anything already inside it to put it in; drag something inside a container anywhere else in the pack to take it out. A drop fills the Play box and opens Play; nothing is sent until Send. Moving an item from one container straight to another is two orders (take it out first). Container capacity is parked as D-254.
  - **The Weaver** (CT2 a). A plan's containers list places each one (key, item, carriedBy or groundIn, owner, locked with grade and keys); a bad one is dropped with a note (container\_repaired), never failing the plan. Rewards fill it with place {in: key}; into a worn pack they go to its wearer's stowed items. The container itself costs no Boon. Its catalog items say container: worn, fixed or carried.
- **Needs data.** Every story item blueprint (minted or improvised) is listed on the GM page under Needs data, so the designer can give it its own data and promote it.

Highest quality allowed by the scenario's gain grade (GAIN\_MAX\_QUALITY): minor Passable, moderate Sound, major Exceptional, extreme Flawless. A weapon's, armor's and shield's quality bonus is the need-scaled formula above (QE2-QE6); only tools still read QUALITY\_TABLE (*placeholder*).
