### 2. What is saved

The save has two layers. The library holds blueprints shared by every story. A story holds everything that happens in one playthrough. **The Universe** (Ali, 13/11/2026, HF6, HF17, HF22, HF25): a hidden layer over every world, kept apart from each story's save (db `universe/…`, src/save/universe.ts). It holds the shared calendar: the furthest day any world has reached (`universe/calendar`), raised after each saved turn. A new world starts on the day after it (`clocks.startDay`), so a story's day 1 is no longer always day 1, and the new character's birth day moves with it, keeping the age picked at creation. Universe › worlds › stories: a world is its places, people and everything that happened in it; a story is one character's life in a world. **Carrying a world on** (Ali, 13/11/2026, HF26, HF31 b, HF32 a, WP1–WP5; src/save/world.ts): a story's meta names its world (StoryMeta.worldId; absent = it began a world of its own, whose id is its storyId). A reset writes how the story ended (StoryMeta.end: the character's name, the day, the Threadbound items left in the world). A world is free when every story in it is archived (WP1 b: a character reset while standing left it). The creation screen lists every free world (World: New world, or "<title> · day <last day> · <name> fell, <name> left"), newest first, only when there is one. A world's title is its first character's ("Aldric’s world") until one is given in the game: Rename beside the picked world (db `worlds/<worldId>`; an empty title goes back to the default) (WP5). Picking one copies the world's last save into a new story (carryOn): places, people, their memories, deals and things lying about stay. A character who fell stays where they fell, Dead, as an NPC under their own id (hero_<story>), so everything people knew of them (beliefs, stances, deals, their band and its relations) stays theirs (WP3), except what their foes took: before the newcomer arrives, everyone standing, hostile to them and in the same place (those in their zone first) loots the body; the first takes all the coin, then each in turn takes the most valuable thing left, at most LOOT_TAKE_MAX (6, placeholder) each; worn containers stay, and with no foe standing everything stays (WP6 a; lootTheFallen). A looted Threadbound item keeps its dead owner's bond, so whoever takes it from the looter can wear it; a character reset while standing is gone with everything they carried, and what pointed at them is dropped as for anyone gone (the load's reference repair). The newcomer starts a stranger; NPC plans, the stage, quests and fights start fresh, and the world's turn count runs on (the opening's turn record is marked opening). The world's date jumps to the Universe date, with nothing else changed (D-251 parks a Weaver summary of the gap); the new character's age is still the one picked. Before the opening's Weaver call, the Weaver's view holds the settlement around the start place (its top-level place and every place inside it) with everyone in it, so it sees it as it was left (WI8 a: all built ground is live); the call gets target.arrive and sets arriveAt, where the newcomer arrives: an area already there or a new zone (a new zone is not required, BF82 a, replacing WP4 a's mandatory one). A place or area is built once in a world (WI1 a, Ali 17/11/2026, widening BG-273): any plan that declares a place or area whose key is already built anywhere, on the stage or not, is refused (place\_exists, zone\_exists) and sent back for repair, so code never builds a copy under a new id; only the bare start place (BG-32) is filled in by the plan's place of the same key. A new zone keyed as an unbuilt frontier (any plan, the newcomer's arrival above all) builds that frontier: it keeps the frontier's id and is joined to it as when the player walks through, so a way is never built twice (BF93 a, BG-286); the opening Weaver is told so. Any plan may link to ground already built anywhere (WI8 a: all of it is live); a zone on a road or junction of the land that already has its zone is refused (road\_built), and the land's own ways are made by code (WI2 a, see Roads as zones) (BF95 a, BG-288). HF28/WP2 b: a Threadbound start gets a Threadbound item not already in that world; when none is free the start is off for that world, with a line saying why. WP2: a Threadbound item's bond dies with its owner, so the player can wear a dead owner's and it binds to them; the Weaver is told the player's Threadbound items (WEAVER_VIEW.player.threadbound) and on Critical Failure or worse may bring a strong hostile foe who has come for one. The old story is never changed. Uniques live in the code library (HF34 a); the Universe will record only where each unique is (which worlds, alive or dead). One story runs at a time **per owner** (Ali, 10/11/2026, replacing "one story at a time"): the player's own story, and at most one story the autopilot agent plays on its own save (StoryMeta.owner 'agent'; absent = the player's). Opening the game always resumes the player's story; the agent's story opens only from the autopilot bar, and "On my story" returns. While the agent's own story is on screen, yellow marks it: the pressed "Its own story" choice and the header's "Watch an agent play" switch (no banner row). Each owner has its own pending start (pending/start, pending/agent), and a reset archives only the story on screen. **Watch an agent play (autopilot)** (Ali, 10/11/2026): a floating pop-up over the Play screen (Agent pop-up look). The agent (AgentName 'autopilot', src/agents/autopilot.ts) gets the last 14 story lines, its own last AUTOPILOT_SAID_KEPT (6) commentary lines with the rule never to reuse their exclamations, Japanese words, openings or phrases (Ali, 11/11/2026: they went stale) and a playstyle (the playtest personas: Curious, Eager, Bloodthirsty, Patient, Shopper) and answers JSON {command, say, direction}. The command is typed into the action box where the player can see it and sent like Send; say is the agent's commentary, 2 to 4 short sentences of at most 70 words, spoken as someone living the moment, never reporting it: it reacts first, and the outcome of its last move and what the people around did come out in that reaction, then what it does next and why (Ali, 11/11/2026: the watcher only hears it, and it must not sound like a statement of facts), shown under the bar and acted out by the RPG Voice or read aloud in one of Claude's own voices (Voice on/off). direction is a few English words telling a voice actor how to say the line, and say may carry up to two of the RPG Voice's sound tags where the sound really happens (VOICE_TAGS: <gasp>, <laugh>, <chuckle>, <giggle>, <sigh>, <breath>, <short pause>, <long pause>); readMove keeps those tags and drops any other markup, and the screen and Claude's voices get the line without them (plainLine). Controls: Player's / Agent's (which story), Mode, Playstyle, Pace (2/5/10 s between turns), Style, Speaker (Off mutes the commentary, which is still shown), Speed, One turn, Play/Pause, Close; there is no Stop voice button (Ali). The bar keeps updating while one of its lists is open or its boxes is being typed in: only that part waits, so the status, the agent's words and a guided question always show (Ali, 12/11/2026: the whole bar froze after a pick until a click elsewhere, so the agent looked idle). A Claude call ends when its time runs out even if the page never answers it (sampleClient races the abort signal). The status line counts the seconds of a wait of 5 s or more (Thinking about the next move… 12 s), so a slow call or a stuck turn shows. The agent's /inv, /stats or /quests is answered in the story and never takes the watcher to another page. **Style** (GA18, SP1–SP2): Plain (default) or In character (Ali, 14/11/2026, SP1 a, SP2 b): the agent is the player character. Its commentary and any words its command has the character say are worded from the character's personality line, signatures included, given every move, and that line also decides its moves: a playstyle is only a leaning, and where it clashes with who the character is, the character wins. AI companion and Speaks Japanese were removed (Ali SP1: they failed for now; parked D-315). VOICE_STYLES in src/agents/autopilot.ts. **Speaker** (GA20 a): the RPG Voice's 30 Gemini voices (default Leda, "Youthful"; the list is refreshed from its list_voices tool once it has spoken) and Claude's own voices, Buttery, Airy, Glassy and Rounded, through the page's tts capability; the PC's speech voices were removed (Ali, 11/11/2026: "they all suck"). **RPG Voice** (src/ui/rpgVoice.ts; PARKED by Ali 12/11/2026, "keep the Claude voices only for the time being": CONFIG.RPG_VOICE_ON false hides it and the page never calls the connector; Claude's voices are those tts.voices() reports, four today, since the Claude app's fifth voice, Mellow, isn't offered to pages): Ali's claude.ai connector "RPG Voice" (a relay to Gemini's free speech model, set up in the thread "Find best-in-class voice acting"), reached through the page's mcp capability (tools speak and list_voices). speak({text, direction, voice}) answers a base64 WAV, played in the page at the chosen Speed. Gemini makes a whole clip before any of it plays, so a long line was heard seconds late (Ali, 12/11/2026): each line is now asked for in two pieces side by side, its first sentence and the rest (splitLine), and the first starts as soon as it is made; the agent's command waits for the voice to start, at most AUTOPILOT_VOICE_WAIT_MS (6 s), so what you hear lines up with the move. Its voices show only where the page can reach the connector; when a call fails, Claude's voice reads that line and the bar says why and what fixes it (reconnect or add the connector, allow it on the page, its free allowance used up); a lasting failure stops further tries until another voice is picked or the page reloads (rpgFailure). A Stop voice button shows while one reads. Where Claude can't speak (not offered in this view, or over its allowance) the commentary is only shown, and the bar says why. **Samples** (Ali, 12/11/2026): picking a Speaker, Style or Speed reads a short line from that style's own bank (VOICE_SAMPLES in src/ui/voiceSamples.ts: Plain ten lines, In character eight lines from different people, never the same line twice in a row) in the chosen voice and speed, and is heard only, with no text in the bar (Ali, 12/11/2026: "remove the useless text"), so each voice and style can be told apart before play. **Speed**: 1×, 1.25× or 1.5× (default 1.5×, the fastest Claude's voices read). Play and One turn always start: while a turn is still running the bar says it is waiting, then goes on. A move the agent takes longer than AUTOPILOT_TIMEOUT_MS (60 s) to decide is given up and asked again (two failures pause the bar). Every move it is told how it is (health, wounded parts, morale, coin, perk points to spend; condition() in src/agents/autopilot.ts), and where it stands with the ways out it can see and the other places it knows (surroundings(); BG-30: it walked toward a cellar door that wasn't there, because it only had the story lines), so it never needs /stats or invents a way out: a /stats move is always asked again once with "act", and /inv or /quests within AUTOPILOT_INFO_EVERY (8) moves of another look likewise (BG-25, BG-26, Ali: "/stats is stupid"). It never asks for the same thing more than twice: when someone keeps putting it off or the same try fails twice, it tries another way, such as going to look for itself, asking someone else or moving on (BG-183: it asked the barkeep for a cellar key five turns running). **Guided mode, "Asks me at crossroads"** (Ali, 11/11/2026, GA1–GA17, every recommendation taken): a Mode picker in the bar (Plays alone / Asks me at crossroads), on either story and with every playstyle, remembered on this PC. The agent plays most turns and hands the big calls to the watcher. All kinds live in one table, CROSSROADS in src/agents/crossroads.ts (GA2). Code checks each finished turn (turnSnap before, spotCrossroads after) for: a fight starting that the agent didn't start, asked once per fight (GA3); health first reaching Wounded, Mauled or Critical, or a body part becoming Crippled or Destroyed (GA4); a foe yielding (GA5); and, on the watcher's story only, a perk point waiting, shown as a card that suggests the agent's pick with "Take <perk>" and "Not now", or another perk picked on the sheet (GA6). The agent itself stops before: attacking anyone not already attacking it, finishing off someone down, yielded or fleeing (GA7); agreeing to, turning down or giving up a deal or quest (GA8); selling or giving away gear, or spending more than GUIDED_COIN_SHARE (a quarter) of its coin in one go (GA9); leaving for a place it has never been, judged from the places in the story lines (GA10); and choices that shape who the character is (GA11). It never asks about scenario breaks, arrivals and departures, achievements, skill gains, level-ups, ordinary talk, small buys or a fight it is already in, and the same kind about the same thing asks at most once per scenario; a repeat is answered with its own pick (GA12). The question card sits where the spoken line is: the situation in the agent's voice (read aloud), 2 to 4 choices with its pick marked, a box for the watcher's own words and You decide; the tab title shows "Your call" (GA13). A choice is typed as the next move; own words become the next move and guidance until the scenario ends, shown in the bar (GA14). It waits with no time limit, the action box is the watcher's meanwhile, and a turn the watcher plays answers it; Pause or × drops the question (GA15). Standing orders (GA16) are gone: the player character's signatures replace them (Ali, 14/11/2026), and the guided agent is told them every turn, to keep to and never ask about. The bar shows only the last call and its answer; nothing is added to the story (GA17). It pauses when the character falls, when play stops, or after two failed calls. On its own save the agent spends its perk points itself (the open milestone of its highest skill, as the playtest does); on the player's story it never spends them. While it plays, the action box is the agent's. A reload or a new version of the page in the same tab carries on where the bar was: still open, still playing, on the same story (kept per tab, so opening the game afresh still opens the player's story). **Hall of fame** (Ali, 10/11/2026): resetting a story whose character is down writes a fame entry on its meta (StoryMeta.fame: name, title, classes and level, best true skills, perks, achievements, scars, defeats, day, turn, place and the blow that dropped them); the Hall of fame page lists every fallen character of either owner, newest first, and opens even with no story running. **Name and gender** (Ali, 10/11/2026): the creation screen asks for the character's name and gender (Man, Woman, Other = he, she, they); the build's pronouns become the player's Actor.pronouns, which others use for them.

- **Story isolation.** Every story-scoped read and write takes the story id. Each story lives under its own storage namespace. No function reads across stories. Views are built only from the loaded story.
- **Lookup order.** An id resolves in the story's blueprints first, then in the library.
- **Reference, don't copy.** A story stores library ids, never copies of entries, so library edits reach it at once. The one exception is a rolled NPC's values (section 4).
