### Explore mode

In Explore no Planner runs: unaware actors follow routines, the player is detected only on major actions, and an attack needs to win its Combat Stealth contests to surprise.

#### Routines

- Each actor follows one routine at a time from its routines list, the routines of one library routine set. A routine is a desired-state function (Game state model). At scenario setup Agent Weaver picks the set and binds its slots; code sets the anchor.
- Each turn, every active actor with no higher-priority order reads its desired zone and activity from its ActorView. Elsewhere: it moves one way along its walk there, the shortest by walked metres over the floor plan, round the ways it knows are shut (BG-1614; never a count of ways). There: it performs the activity. Actors are processed in actor-id order; ties use the seeded RNG. If no walk to the desired zone exists, the actor stays in its zone, performing the step's activity, and code logs routine\_unreachable once, with the zone it stands in and the zone it can't reach (CS22; src/engine/resolve.ts noteUnreachable). It logs again only after its routine walks on or it gets there. Whether a stage trim may cut off a bound zone stays deferred (D-46).
- **Clear sight (Ali, 07/11/2026, Q17).** The player's Journal keeps clearSight: each actor the player has seen fully by sight (or made out with a successful look), with the point and height where it was seen (BG-1618, WT14 a: a title only names a space). While that actor stays within CLEAR_SIGHT_STAY_M (placeholder) of that point and can still be glimpsed, its events reach the player as full sight and Who's here shows it clearly, dark included. Everyone the player sees clearly as a turn ends (by distance, by what they did or by a make-out roll) is recorded too, so the next turn, which starts with no events, shows them as the narration of the last one did (BG-1545). A move or flee the player does not see fully removes it; one the player does see records the new point.
- **Seen leaving (BG-23).** A move that leaves a zone is perceived where it set off as well as where it ended, and the better sight counts: someone walking out through a door in plain sight is seen going, even into a room out of sight. A full sight of it records the zone they went into in clearSight, so lastSeen names it ("the barkeep, last seen in the kitchen").
- **Last known place (BG-28).** The Journal also keeps lastKnown: the zone of every actor the player has seen or heard (any perception, a sound included), never forgotten by an unseen move. lastSeen reads it, so someone heard running off is still named where they were heard, and the Parser never asks who the player means when one of them fits.
- Moves and activities emit ordinary move and idle events with the activity's noise and visibility, and go through computePerception, so the player perceives patrols (a partial event, sound only).
- An activity's attention level (the ATTENTION scale) is read only by filching (TN4): sight assumes everyone is looking (parked as D-200).
- Actors that aren't engaged keep following their routines during Tactical turns too.

#### Stealthy posture

Ali, 08/11/2026, ST1–ST10.

- A sneak action sets player.posture = 'stealthy' (display name "Stealthy", POSTURES scale). It stays over moves until the player acts openly.
- Sneak costs time (VS8, Ali 17/11/2026, D-364): while stealthy an actor's time factor (timeFactor) is CONFIG.SNEAK\_TIME\_MULT (1.2, Ali 17/11/2026). Every action takes that many times as long (the Action times rows, words, gear, ways, picking a lock), and movement too: speed is divided by the same factor (speedFactor = 1 / timeFactor), so a walk or dash covers that much less ground. Reaction time is no action and is not slowed. Pointing at Stealthy (the header badge and the status strip) says it slows all actions ×SNEAK\_TIME\_MULT, and the deck's Sneak button notes the same. It keeps the turn's move and main slots. It is offered in the action deck's Stealth group (there is no Hide action, D-327); code picks the skill (Sneaking, or Combat Stealth while engaged or attacking).
- What ends it: speaking above a whisper, any speech action, and a melee attack (the blow shows you). A ranged attack keeps it; the shot is noticed only through the stealth contest (Combat Stealth) per observer.
- While stealthy, the player's events reach only observers already aware of them; everyone else is reached only through the contest.
- **Situation tags** (ST21–ST22, Ali 08/11/2026). Situational modifiers apply by tag: each skill lists its situation tags (docs/skill-tags.md), and each situational table names the tags it reaches and the sign (CONFIG.SITUATION\_TAGS). Light (LIGHT\_LUX through lightMod, read where the action is) hurts sight (every attack, Block, Dodge, Sight) and helps stealth (the three Stealth skills). Cover (COVER\_HIDDEN, a roll modifier by what shows: CV6, CV7) helps hide (all three Stealth skills: HA1) and hurts a ranged attack against a target in cover by the same number. An action can add a tag (a stash adds hide). Staying put (a sneak that stays put with Melt Away, or hiding from a search) ignores the move role, so leg wounds don't count (ST10). In a stealth contest both rolls get their own tags' tables, so darkness also hurts the observer's Sight. Darkness never hurts hearing, so against an observer who only hears the player, light gives the stealth roll no help either (Ali, 08/11/2026, P14). Leg wounds stay on body-part roles. Hearing and Tremor carry a hearing tag (Ali, 08/11/2026); no table reads it yet (the noise work will).
- **Unblinking** (ST23, the sight 60 perk): an observer with it who loses a Vanishing roll must also lose a Sneaking or Combat Stealth contest before losing the player.
- **Slipping away is part of the sneak** (VS1–VS7, Ali 17/11/2026, D-363; replaces the Vanish action, ST11–ST13). There is no Vanish action, button or action-time row (VS6, DU28 retired). On a sneak, each observer who could perceive it rolls by what it knows of you: one unaware or suspicious of you rolls against Sneaking or Combat Stealth as before; one who already sees you (aware or more) rolls against Vanishing (VS1). That roll needs a step (a sneak with a move) or Melt Away (a sneak that stays put in cover or shade, or anywhere with Blind Spot, VS2); a sneak that stays put without it shakes off no one who sees you (ST12).
- **The Vanishing roll** (ST14–ST19, VS1). One who saw you and loses it on every sense that rolled (D-40) drops to suspicious at the zone you left (test 3); a tie or a win changes nothing for them. Light and cover help it, and leg wounds count on a step. Strike and Fade (VS3): right after an attack from hiding, each observer the attack made aware rolls once more, against Vanishing. A foe fighting you within CONFIG.VANISH\_NEAR\_M (5 m, placeholder; by distance from where you started, not weapon reach, so a ranged weapon gets no help) gives the roll a tier shift of CONFIG.VANISH\_NEAR\_SHIFT (-1, placeholder) (VS5). Everyone who saw you losing you in one turn counts for the vanished achievements.
- For a foe who still sees you, being Stealthy changes nothing: they keep seeing you and the engagement goes on (ST15).
- A shot's target that loses the notice contest still turns suspicious, at the shooter's zone (ST19).
- **One contest per sense** (ST4 as changed by D-40, Ali 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Open parked items" and the thread's decision card). Each detection sense that reaches an observer (sight, hearing, smell: DETECTION\_SENSES in src/library/skillIds.ts, each with its reach) makes its own contest against the actor's stealth, each sense at most once per turn per observer; a shot's contests (BG-263) and Strike and Fade's rolls (VS3) are their own, also one per sense. Smell rolls whenever it reaches, seen or heard or not (D-40 Q2; AB18 a still decides only what a smell alone shows). A win on any sense makes an unaware observer aware, else a tie on any makes it suspicious. One who already sees you loses you only when every sense that rolled loses (D-40 Q1): any sense that ties or wins keeps you, and Unblinking (ST23) is tried per sense. An observer who only feels a blow rolls sight, as before. Every Sense One makes sight and hearing one contest, rolled as whichever set-up gives the observer the better total of roll modifiers (sight on a tie) (D-40 Q3). More rolls make sneaking past someone who sees, hears and smells you harder than before.
- **NPC stealth** (NS1–NS10, Ali 08/11/2026, settles OQ-14). Every actor follows the same rules: posture, the three Stealth skills, the contests, slipping away (VS1) and Unblinking. An NPC's sneak names the zone it goes to, if any (Planner action sneak); its hide is the main action, as for the player. A stealthy NPC's events reach only observers who see it (aware). Observers are everyone, the player and its own group and crew included, so NPCs contest each other too (NS5). A hidden character is hidden from its own too (the owner, Questions hub page "Belonging build", 21/11/2026, BB9 b: "a hidden character is hidden, allies can get a +2 at most"): its group notices it, and searches for it, CONFIG.ALLY_NOTICE_TIERS.group tiers easier, its crew (BEL-51) .crew tiers easier, anyone else no easier. The Planner decides when an NPC goes stealthy (NS8).
- **Your belief about a stealthy NPC** (NS2–NS4). You roll for yourself like any observer (NS3). You simply see open actors. For each stealthy one you keep a belief (journal.stealthSeen): unaware hides it, suspicious shows "someone", aware shows it as usual. When an NPC goes stealthy you keep it as far as you see it then (ST12); a stealthy NPC's move you don't perceive at all drops aware to suspicious. When it turns open the belief is cleared.
- **Ambush on the player** (NS6). A stealthy NPC that attacks you while you are unaware of it makes the same ambush as you do against NPCs: its attack's Combat Stealth contests against each of your senses that reaches. A melee blow still shows it.
- **Companions** (NS9). A member of your band on a follow routine copies your posture each turn. A routine can be stealthy (the thief's prowl and lie-low), which keeps the actor stealthy while it follows it.
- **Follow order** (BG-34, Ali 11/11/2026). Any NPC's agent (Planner, Responder or Voice) may switch it to the follow routine when it agrees to come along with the player, whatever its routine set; its deal says why. From then on it walks after the player's last known zone every turn: on code-run turns by the routine, on agent-run turns as a move beside whatever action the agent gave (unless that action is itself a move, or it is in a fight). It ends when the agent switches back to one of the NPC's own routines. A fight, morale break or no path pauses it. Bandmates never take follow from a spokesperson. NPCs never path through a drop (SD7).
- **Head to** (NW2). When an NPC's agent (Planner, Responder or Voice) sends it somewhere further than one move, its output names headTo: a zone or a frontier on the stage. Code switches the NPC to the shared head_to routine (in no set), bound to that place in slot dest, and keeps the routine it had. It walks there one way at a time, by the routine on code-run turns and as a move beside the agent's action on agent-run turns, as follow does. Bound for a frontier, it walks to the zone with a way into it nearest by its walk to that way (BG-1614) and steps in from there (NPCs at frontiers below). Once it stands in the destination it goes back to the routine it had, anchor kept, and walks on from there. A second headTo on the way changes only where it is going. A place not on the stage is refused (invalid_head_to). The same routine carries a yielded foe to its exit at the scenario's end (NW1), and a reused actor to its new start (NW7). When the agent takes someone along (lead: their id), the NPC waits at the end until they arrive or LEAD_WAIT_S (config, placeholder 90 game seconds) pass, then goes back to its routine (BF4).
- **No jumps** (NW1, NW7). An NPC the player has met always stands in a real zone and only ever walks. When a Weaver plan brings back an existing actor, it stays where it is: if the player can't perceive it, it heads to its new start by head_to and then takes up its new routine; if the player perceives it, it is not sent anywhere and its new routine walks it on from where it stands. A Weaver plan may still place a new actor anywhere in it.
- **The village thief** (NS7): template thief (Sneaking 40, Combat Stealth 30, Vanishing 30, Light Melee 35, Light Armor 25, Dodge 30, Sight 30, Hearing 30, Falsehood 30, Guile 30; dagger and gambeson) with the routine set thief (prowl, lie\_low\_alert, wait, wait\_alert). GU1 a (19/11/2026): Guile sits beside Falsehood in the same role bands (Sneak low, Talker moderate), so NPCs who lie in words also keep a straight face.

#### Detecting the player

- Detection happens only on the player's major actions. isMajorAction() counts every player action as major (D-34 a); acting quietly is the Stealthy posture's job.
- Each observer who could perceive the action (the existing perception range) and is unaware or suspicious of the player gets one test:
  - Sneaking player: the observer's perception roll against the player's stealth roll (ruleset.contested).
  - Open player: no detection roll. The observer's perception level of the event (computePerception, after any make-out roll below) decides: full makes it aware, partial makes it suspicious, none leaves it unaware.
- One test per observer and sense per turn: for a hide or sneak, the stealth contests in the simultaneous-order rules (Resolution engine) are the detection test, one per detection sense that reaches (D-40, "One contest per sense" above), and detectPlayer skips observers already contested on that event.
- The result goes to updateBeliefs, which stays the only writer of Belief. A won contest makes the observer aware and a tie makes it suspicious; against an open player, full perception makes it aware and partial makes it suspicious (settles OQ-5, Ali, 07/11/2026).
- Observers unaware of the player don't perceive the player's minor actions at all.

#### Making out what is half seen or half heard

Ali, 14/11/2026, PG3–PG9 all a (Questions hub page "Passive Eyesight and Earshot growth"); src/engine/makeOut.ts, perception faintness.

- **When** (PG3 a). Whenever an observer, the player or an NPC, perceives an event of someone not hiding only in part (glimpsed past the clear range or in the dark, or heard below the clear margin), it rolls that sense automatically, with no command: Sight when glimpsed, Hearing when only heard. Someone stealthy is left to the stealth contests; touch is never rolled; a sleeper perceives nothing.
- **How hard** (PG4 a). A sight or hearing function roll against a task grade (gradeMod). Sight: how far past the visibility's clear range it is, as a share of the way to its glimpse range (CONFIG.SIGHT\_M): up to 25% Minor, 50% Moderate, 75% Major, else Extreme (MAKE\_OUT\_SIGHT\_GRADES); darkness where it happens is MAKE\_OUT\_DARK\_STEPS (1) harder, which Night Eyes cancels; the easier of where it happened and where a leg set off. Sound: the dB it reaches above HEARING\_FLOOR\_DB: 11 or more Minor, 7 Moderate, 3 Major, else Extreme (MAKE\_OUT\_SOUND\_GRADES). No light modifier: darkness is in the grade. All placeholders.
- **What it gives** (PG5 a). Success or better turns that perception full: who it is, what they do, and their words when heard. The Q17 rule then keeps a still person seen clearly.
- **Named everywhere** (BG-252, PG11 a). Someone made out by sight counts as seen clearly that turn wherever the game shows who is seen (seenActors: Who's here, the Narrator's people, the actor views, name learning by seesClearlyNow), for every observer (src/perception/madeOut.ts keeps the turn's results). The player keeps anyone seen clearly (by distance, a look or a make-out roll) named while they stay put and can still be glimpsed: the glimpse range of what they did, never less than a still figure's (Low), in the dark too (stillGlimpsed). A half-seen move forgets it, and a new make-out roll decides.
- **How often** (PG6 a). At most once a turn for each observer, person and sense; that result holds for the person's other events that turn.
- **Words** (PG8 a). Someone seen clearly whose words reach the observer only as a voice: the observer rolls Hearing (the sound grade) and a success catches the words.
- **Learning** (PG7 a). It learns like any check against its grade (with PG2 a, any pass teaches).
- **Shown** (PG9 a). Only the player's roll is kept (GameEvent.madeOut), and only the Rolls panel shows it (Show rolls on); the narration tells what was made out.
- **Scaling.** Chance of Success or better: Minor 45 / 70 / 82 / 95% at skill 0 / 50 / 75 / 100, Extreme 22 / 47 / 60 / 72%; at 150 every grade is 95%. Past about 150 it stops mattering, because the clear and glimpse ranges and the hearing floor are human numbers: senses past 100 widening those ranges is parked as D-321 (PG10 a).

#### Attention

Ali, 21/11/2026, D-200 a and D-200b b (Questions hub page "Open parked items 3"); src/perception/attention.ts.

- **Facing** (D-200). Every body faces the way it last walked, or turns toward what it strikes, looks at or speaks to (engine/position.ts). One that has never walked or turned has no facing yet and sees all round it. Turning by choice and rotation speed are parked (D-439).
- **Rungs.** Sight runs down one ladder: clear, then a glimpse that is Minor, Moderate, Major, Extreme to make out. Each rung of attention is one step down it. Attention alone never takes sight away: a glimpse stops at Extreme. Darkness and distance still can.
- **Off the facing** (D-200, D-200b b). Within ATTENTION\_SIDE\_DEG (60°) of the facing, sight is as usual. Past it, ATTENTION\_SIDE\_STEPS (1) rung worse: clear becomes a glimpse, a glimpse is a grade harder. Past ATTENTION\_BACK\_DEG (100°, behind, outside the eyes' field), ATTENTION\_BACK\_STEPS (2) rungs worse. Real figures: each eye sees about 60° toward the nose and 100° toward the temple (Spector, "Visual Fields", Clinical Methods, 1990).
- **Busy** (D-200). ATTENTION\_BUSY\_STEPS (1) rung more while busy: a long action under way (Actor.busy, not paused), or fighting someone else (it struck at someone this turn or last, and not at the one seen).
- **Where.** Every sight check for everyone: events (sightAt, and the make-out grade in faintness), Who's here and the actor views (seenActors), filching and the Insight look. The Q17 rule still keeps a still person the player saw clearly seen clearly. A sleeper still sees nothing.

#### Searching

Ali, 11–13/11/2026, SE1–SE25 (questions page "Search redesign"); fight reach SA8 b (page "Search achievements"; dropped by LX3 a). A search may be repeated every turn with a fresh roll (SE12 a); its event is quiet and normally seen (SE13). Use, interact and other keep the scene-number check until their own deep dive (SE18 a; the numbers are D-7). The look in a Search has no scene number (SE15).

- **The Search action** (SE1, SE19; LX1, LX3, LX4, Ali 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Merge Search and Look around"). A search is the main action and takes the Action times row "look around", 3 s, in a fight or out of one (LX3 a; SE1's 6 s and 1 minute were never built); the move slot stays free. Search and Look around are one action, **Search** (LX1): around the player it is the look (below) and the search, so "search the room", "look around" and "look and search" all play it. Searching a person is its own action, **Pat down** (LX4), with the person search's rules below; a guard searching someone does the same. The Parser's action is "search", with target null for Search or the person for Pat down; code picks the sense, so its skill is left empty. The action deck offers Search (no picks) and Pat down (whom). Search takes the turn's action: it is not free.
- **What it covers** (SE20, SE21, SE24, SA8; BG-314, BF100 b, Ali 19/11/2026). The search area around the searcher (a circle of that many m²), in a clear line from the searcher, whatever title anyone stands in: a title's edge stops nothing, a wall does (PZ11 a). SE20's "the searcher's own area, all of it when no bigger than the search area" is dropped as zone-era. Search area = the circle of SEARCH\_RADIUS\_AT\_0\_M (2 m) doubled every SEARCH\_RADIUS\_DOUBLING (50) points of the rolling sense's skill (LS9, Ali 20/11/2026, a decision card in the thread: "Both senses, double per 50", after TL17's ladder: every 50 points past 100 doubles what a body can do), × (1 + the owner's searchArea % ÷ 100), + SEARCH\_M2\_PER\_REACH\_M (120) m² per metre of searchReach. The circle is the same in a fight and out of one (LX3 a: SA8 b's fight reach, area ÷ 120 m, is gone), and its radius is never under SEARCH\_MIN\_RADIUS\_M (2 m, LX2). PerkDef and AchievementDef carry searchArea (%) and searchReach (m); every source adds up (achievements along their chain, plus active perks). All placeholders. This settles D-422.
- **Against a hiding person** (SE2 b, SE3, SE4 c; D-411: LS1–LS8, Ali 19–20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Searches that find hidden people by sound"). Each sense reaches a hider by its own rule and makes its own contest against the hider's Sneaking (Combat Stealth in an engagement) with light, cover and the hide tag, as in the passive stealth contest (LS1 a, LS6): **sight** a clear line out to the look's 25 m, never in pitch black; **hearing** the hider's own sound carried to the searcher above HEARING\_FLOOR\_DB (LS5 a), by the real decibels hearing uses (metres, walls, doors, the path through open ways), with Keen Ear and the search's own 10·log10(that sense's search area ÷ the circle at skill 0) dB (LS3 a, LS9: 0 dB at skill 0, +6 per 50); the hider's sound is its latest step this turn or last, else HIDE\_STILL\_NOISE (faint, 20 dB: breathing, cloth; LS2 a; perception soundOf); **smell** its usual reach (Scent in metres along open ways, AB18; LS8 a). Each sense has its own search area from its own skill (SE21). The best contest counts: a win makes the searcher aware (the player keeps them seen while they stay put), a tie suspicious ("someone"), a loss changes nothing; a find by sound is the same (LS4 a). Every sense takes the same rings by its distance (LS7 a): inside its search radius SEARCH\_AREA\_SHIFT (+1), out to SEARCH\_RING1\_RADII (2) radii SEARCH\_RING\_SHIFTS[0] (±0), past that SEARCH\_RING\_SHIFTS[1] (−1) (LX5 b, LX8 a). So that a Search never finds less than waiting: inside a sense's search area it uses up the pair's passive contest (SE5); where a sense reaches the hider only in its rings, that sense's passive contest still runs that turn and the better counts (WC1, WC2, Ali 20/11/2026, decision cards in the thread: "Keep both contests", then "Past the search area", so that a nearer hider is never harder to find). A Pat down (a search aimed at one person) contests only them, by every sense that reaches, with no shift. Hidden items make no sound: the item search stays sight only. A hider found by sound and still heard but not seen shows where they are as "someone", named only if heard clearly (LS4 a; views seenActors).
- **Nothing to find** (SE6). With nobody hidden in reach nothing is rolled. A lost roll reads the same as an empty spot; the search's dice show only with a find.
- **One contest per turn** (SE5). A search always rolls, even after a passive contest that turn, and each sense it tests counts as that sense's contest for the pair (D-40), except a sense that reaches the hider only in its rings (WC2, above).
- **Hidden items** (SE8, SE10). An item on the ground can lie hidden (ItemInstance.hiddenAt, an outcome tier) at its point (or its area's centre). Only those who know where it lies (knownTo) see it or can pick it up. A search makes one sight function roll, in the area's light, against every hidden item in reach it doesn't know of: a result above an item's tier finds it, the same tier gives a hint ("something here has been moved") and finds nothing. Nothing hidden: no roll. The Weaver may hide a ground reward (place.hiddenAt), story items included for now (SE10, flagged for later review).
- **Stashing** (SE9). The Parser's action "stash" with a carried item is the main action: a stealth function roll with light, cover and the hide tag, as for a hide. Its tier becomes the item's hidden-at tier, and the item lies where the stasher stands. The stasher, and anyone who sees the stash clearly, know where it is. A stash is a hushed sound and a low-visibility sight.
- **Searching a person** (SE11; Pat down, LX4). A search aimed at someone in view. Hands-on (within grip reach of someone down, asleep, surrendered or held by the searcher) finds all their gear, concealed and stowed, with no roll. Anyone else is only looked over: one sight roll against each concealed item they wear (ItemDef.hiddenAt, hidden blade Great Success, placeholder), found when it beats that tier. What is found is revealed to the searcher (the player's journal.revealed, an NPC's belief) and shows in its view of them, stowed items included. Consent (a willing person) is not built: there is no way yet to tell that someone agrees.
- **The look** (SE14, LX1, LX2 a). The player's Search around them also makes the look: a sight roll with light only (SE15) whose Success or better shows everyone in sight clearly. Its roll always shows, so a Search's dice show even with no find (SE6 is for the search part). The hiders are the search's own per-sense contests above (D-411), which reach the look's 25 m by sight. Measured before hearing joined (LX8 a, the village thief and the starting character, 300 turns each): waiting 29%, a Search 60% inside its area, 38% in ring 1, 27–28% in ring 2. Measured with WC2 (`npm run measure`, 3000 trials): the starting character against the thief 58% at 2 m, 50% at 5 m, 40% at 8 m, waiting 29%; a guard against the hiding starting character 100%, 99.9%, 99.6% at 2, 5 and 9 m, waiting 96% (before WC2, 92% in ring two). Look at and Listen keep their look and one weak search over the look's range, LOOK\_SEARCH\_SHIFT (−1) tiers down. An NPC's search is the same search, rings and senses included (LX7 a), without the look's roll that shows everyone.
- **Marks** (SE7, SE25 a). Marks are invisible. Both sides of a contest against a hider mark as in the passive contest. An item search learns like a check against the best-hidden item it searched for: a stashed item keeps its stasher's true stealth skill (ItemInstance.hiderSkill) and that is the number; an item no one stashed (Weaver-hidden, concealed gear) counts its hidden-at tier as HIDDEN\_TIER\_SUCCESS\_SKILL (50) at Success and HIDDEN\_TIER\_SKILL\_STEP (25) more or less per tier step (Great Success 75, Critical Success 100, +25 per step beyond; never under 0). A stash learns against the best true sight skill of those who see it clearly, and learns nothing unwatched.
- **Secrets** (SE17). A player's search aimed at someone also rolls, once per sense, for that person's unrevealed check\_passed secrets whose skill is a sight or hearing skill: a roll at the secret's minTier or better reveals it (journal.secretsKnown; effect found\_secret) and shows the dice. It learns against the secret's minTier on the same table. None to find: no roll.
- **NPCs** (SE16, LX7 a). NPCs search by the same rule, rings included, the player included as a hider. The combat fallback's search, the alert sweep (search\_route\_alert, which searches at each stop) and the careful patrol (careful\_patrol and night\_careful\_patrol, which walk the beat at a patrol's pace and search each stop while calm, for anyone lying in wait) use it.
- **Scaling** (LS9). The radius doubles every 50 points and never caps: 2 m at 0, 2.8 at 25, 4 at 50, 8 at 100 (the human limit), 16 at 150, 32 at 200 (four times the limit, as TL17's Mythic), in a fight too. Hearing gets the dB that carry sound as much further (+6 dB per 50): someone keeping still is heard 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16 m off at 0 / 50 / 100 / 150 / 200, a sneaking step 10 / 20 / 40 / 80 / 160 m, before walls and doors. Sight still stops at the look's 25 m. The contest itself has the shared ceiling past about 200 (D-211, D-213; SE22).

#### Ambush: attacking from Explore

When the player attacks an actor that is unaware of them, code resolves the ambush before the blow:

1. **The ambush is a Combat Stealth roll like any other** (BG-343; Ali, 20/11/2026, in the thread: "combat stealth obviously. and they should roll as any combat stealth roll and not something special, the only special thing should be the outcome"; decision card AQ1–AQ4). Nothing special is rolled: the attack's own stealth contests ("One contest per sense" above: Combat Stealth, ST2, with light, cover and the hide tag, against each detection sense that reaches each observer unaware of the attacker, once per turn) are run before the blow and decide both who notices and the surprise (AQ1 a). The attacker need not be Stealthy: any attack on someone unaware of it rolls them (OQ-4, AQ3 a). The target is caught off guard only when it rolled and every sense lost; a win or a tie on any sense is no surprise (AQ2 a). A later blow on a still-unaware target in the same turn keeps that result and rolls nothing new (AQ4 a). Against the Odds counts once for the whole act (CS25).
2. **Every sense of the target loses: surprise.** The target keeps its routine order this turn; like any attack's target that loses, it suspects where the blow came from (ST19). The target's defense roll takes the unaware situational modifier (value placeholder). The ruleset may also apply an unaware tag to its initiative.
3. **Target wins on a sense: failed ambush.** The target becomes aware before the blow and defends normally. Its onAlert fires: this turn if it hasn't acted, otherwise next turn unless it then has an agent order. It is engaged from the next turn, and a significant target's Planner order outranks onAlert. No Planner runs for it in the same turn (D-36 a): its Planner picks it up from the next turn.
4. **A witness wins.** It becomes aware and its onAlert fires the same way. If it is hostile to the player it is engaged from the next turn; otherwise it is alarmed.
5. **Nobody wins, or nobody perceives the attack.** Observers stay unaware. A kill nobody perceives leaves the game in Explore.

#### Alarm

- A non-engaged actor that perceives an attack or an alarm becomes alarmed. The onAlert order of the routine it is following fires once (Hidden state persistence). Code saves that routine and its anchor in Mind.alarm.returnTo, and the actor switches to that routine's alert variant, anchored at the current turn.
- It calms after DECAY\_AWARE\_S (120 game seconds) with no attack or alarm perceived and returns to the routine in returnTo with its saved anchor, so a schedule continues in phase. An actor whose routine has no alert variant keeps that routine throughout. If an agent switches the actor's routine while it is alarmed, the switch stands and returnTo is cleared. The return to routine uses the timer only, but the alarm stays armed (its onAlert does not fire again) while the actor still knows of someone it is wary of (CS2).
