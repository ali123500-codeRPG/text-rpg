### Information model

Each actor knows only what it perceived itself, plus what reached it through a communication event the engine resolved. There is no band-wide knowledge. A bark is not a communication event: it copies nothing.

**Communication events.** Speech (whisper, normal, shout), call\_help, alarm and a face-to-face report are ordinary events. They are perceived by volume and zone links like any other event. A whisper to someone it does not reach, even after stepping up, is said low instead: the quietest sound up to normal speech that the listener hears clearly (BF3, logged whisper_raised). A dialogue call's line (Responder, Voice) is for the player whatever action carries it, and one order's words are said once a turn (BG-132). When an actor perceives one, code copies the sender's belief to the listener:

- source becomes 'told'.
- status is capped at 'suspicious'; being told never makes an actor 'aware' or 'tracking'.
- lastKnownZone and lastSeenTurn are the sender's values at the moment of sending, so passed-on information is dated and can be stale.
- No model is involved in passing information on.

**Belief decay.** Code lowers a belief one step when lastSeenTurn is older than a config value: tracking → aware → suspicious → unaware. The step lengths are engine config (DECAY\_TURNS), not ruleset numbers. Without decay, actors never calm down and the game never leaves Tactical.

**Foes share what they notice** (WN1-WN13; engine/warnings.ts, engine/ruses.ts). Knowledge passes only by a warning someone actually gives; nobody learns by being near a believer.
- **Warning.** Order.warn is a free shout given beside the action (0 s), with event kind 'warn' and loud noise. An intelligent race shouts words (where the foe is, and the trick); a semi-intelligent race gives a cry (where only); an unintelligent race never warns. Words reach allies who hear the shout clearly; a cry reaches allies who hear it at all. A warning ends Stealthy and opens the warner's posture. Killing a shouter before the turn lands still silences it.
- **What a listener gets.** A 'told' belief about the foe, capped at suspicious, plus each trick fact (revealed `ruse:<trick>`). The fact passes even to listeners already aware of the foe.
- **Trick known.** Knowing a foe's trick gives +WARNED\_SHIFT (2) tiers on the answer roll against that trick, for good. Play Dead, Feigned flight, Bluff and Draw Them In each keep the foes they fooled; a believer who is told re-rolls once at turn end with the shift, and a win frees it. A real Down is never doubted because of it.
- **Hidden foe pointed out.** The listener gets the pointed spot and +POINTED\_SHIFT (1) tier on its next roll against that foe within POINTED\_TURNS (6); there is no extra contest.
- **W1 Warn the others** (FIGHT\_RULES FX12, Battle Sense 10). A code-run NPC warns only after seeing through a trick or spotting a foe by its own roll (never hearsay), once per foe and trick per fight, and only while an ally who lacks the news can hear. An NPC playing dead never warns. Outside a fight, a code-run companion following the player (a bandmate, or one on the follow routine, together with it) gives the same warning, once per foe and trick (D-51 b).
- **Player.** The deck shows "Warn your side" (talk group) when the player has news and an ally in earshot; the Parser can return warn, and legality drops a warn the view does not offer.
- Parked: D-387 silent hand signs, D-388 supernatural warnings past 100.

**Hunting and engaged** (YD6 B, Ali, 11/11/2026; BG-4, BG-5). Two awareness states sit above tracking. **Engaged**: the two traded blows. A real (not sparring) blow engages the attacker with its target, and the target with the attacker once it knows who struck (at least aware; a shot from hiding leaves it suspicious). The target's bandmates who perceived the blow and know who struck become engaged with the attacker too. **Hunting**: a hostile that knows where someone is (aware or better) and moves closer to them (into a zone nearer them, or a step inside one zone that closes in on them), before any blow. Both stay up while either of the two keeps attacking or closing in on the other (Belief.closedTurn). They drop to tracking after CONFIG.ENGAGEMENT\_END\_TURNS (3) turns in which neither did, and ordinary decay takes over from there (src/engine/engagement.ts noteFighting, run each turn after the blows). Seeing someone never lowers hunting or engaged. **Being in a fight** means hostile and engaged, either way round (fightingWith). The player holds no beliefs, so a fight with the player is read from the NPC's belief about the player. A fight engagement counts on the turns either side attacks or closes in, so the quiet turns that drop engaged also end the engagement: a fight you walk away from ends ENGAGEMENT\_END\_TURNS turns later, even if the foe stays hostile and aware of you (BG-5). Tactical mode still starts as soon as a hostile is at least suspicious of someone (isEngaged), as before.

**Bandmates together, the band apart** (YD7, Ali, 11/11/2026). A band's members who each perceive the other right now (in sight, or close enough to hear a faint sound) are together. Members apart act as the band's faction: same friends and enemies, but a deal one of them makes on the spot binds only those together with it. Band membership (bandId) is permanent; only code changes it. Organizations, ties and the band as the group are decided and not built: [Hierarchy and belonging](hierarchy-and-belonging.md) (D-1768; D-196 is merged into it).

**Stance.** stance(a, b) = a's override for b if it has one, else the relation of a's band to b's band; same band is friendly. The stance scale (STANCES), coldest first: hostile, opposed, unfriendly, neutral, friendly, friend, loyal (Ali, 07/11/2026; unfriendly, friend and loyal added with RS1–RS7, 09/11/2026). **Hostile** attacks on sight: it engages, attacks, chases, blocks rest and counts for the crowded-zone modifier. **Opposed** is at odds but never strikes first: an opposed actor is wary, so its onAlert fires when it notices the other, but it does not engage, attack or chase. Code sets a's override to hostile toward an attacker when a perceives an attack on itself or a bandmate. The Planner, Responder or Voice may request a one-step change of its override toward an actor whose speech it perceived since its last call; the engine applies it (friendlier or colder: one move on the regard score, below). **Regard and strength** (D-206, Ali, Questions hub page "Attachment and stance strength (D-206)", 21/11/2026, BN1–BN5 a; src/engine/regard.ts): a personal stance keeps a regard score from −100 to +100, and each step of the scale is a band of it (REGARD\_EDGES; a score on an edge counts toward neutral). A stance set whole (a cast, an attack) sits at its step's middle (REGARD\_MID). One step said by an agent or done by a deed (a quest's end, a filch caught, a suspicion) moves the score REGARD\_STEP, so from a step's middle it moves one whole step, and a bond or grudge deep in its step can take a move without changing step (BN1 a). Attachment scales a friendlier move by 1 + A ÷ ATTACHMENT\_DIVISOR and a colder move on a friendly or warmer stance by 1 − A ÷ ATTACHMENT\_DIVISOR: the attached bond fast and forgive their friends (BN3 a). **Fading** (BN2 a, BN4 a): once FADE\_PAUSE\_DAYS of game time pass with no move between the two, a personal grudge (colder than the band's stance) fades back toward the band's stance at GRUDGE\_FADE points a day × (1 + F ÷ FORGIVENESS\_DIVISOR), F being Forgivingness (the most vengeful never forgive); bonds never fade, band-to-band relations never fade, and a grudge over an attack on oneself fades no warmer than ATTACK\_GRUDGE\_FLOOR's middle (forgiven, not forgotten). A grudge back at the band's stance is dropped, so the stance follows the band again. **Strength** (BN5 a): how deep a personal stance sits in its step, by thirds from the side nearer neutral (slight, plain, strong; REGARD\_STRENGTH), goes beside the stance in an agent's beliefs and in an Insight read (read.strength); code acts on the step only. **What provokes** (D-361, "Personality and provocation" PV1–PV13): a colder step is a grudge the character will carry, not a reaction; answering in the moment (a laugh, a retort, a dare, a spar, walking off) needs none. Each character takes an insult as its personality line says; banter from a friend and a challenge to one's skill are not insults unless the character is touchy; an insult to a top drive can cut anyone; who said it and who heard it count; a true accusation stings only the proud or dishonest; respect won in a fair contest can make someone friendlier. A deed that code already steps the stance for by itself (a quest's end, QT13; a suspicion raised or cleared, GD2/GD4; a filch caught) takes no agent step on top of it (BG-1551). The rule covers words from anyone the character heard, not only the player. It is one shared rule (STANCE_RULE in src/agents/prompts.ts) for the Planner, Responder and Voice. Words only: Resolve does not thicken skin, and a power gap counts only as the character perceives the speaker (PV11).
