### Play screen

These are the game's own design rules (Rules kept only because of HISAB, HS6 to HS13, 17/11/2026). They started from the HISAB design system (https://claude.ai/artifact/PsxYHump4Sjy9iz9GvXkfx), which was only ever meant to speed up early work; HISAB no longer binds the game, and nobody needs to read it before building. This section is where the play UI design rules live: the project instructions only point here, so agents make every later edit here (HS13). The game is a build-crafting game first and a story second: the build screens (creation, Character page, perks, essences, gear) lead. The look is Graphite, picked in the look study (LK4 b, below).

These design rules are binding for every play screen:

- **Platform:** one self-contained page for PC only (no phone rules), no network calls except the game's own Claude and database capabilities and the owner's connectors it names (RPG Voice; Claude Code Remote for the Republish button). One dark theme, no theme switcher, no picture credit (personal use). No max width, 32px gutters, 1.3× zoom at 2400px and wider. Text size (B4): the browser's zoom is the text-size setting; every page still works at 125% zoom on a 1920×1080 screen, with nothing clipped or overlapping (checked 18/11/2026 on creation and every rail page; the personality axes were the one overflow, fixed).
- **Type:** HISAB Round for all UI text (look study LK4, 17/11/2026: its letters stay readable at small sizes); story text in Literata (SIL OFL, embedded). Tabular figures everywhere.
- **Look (LK4 b, 17/11/2026):** Graphite, picked in the look study (hub page "Choose the game's own look"): a neutral graphite ground (--bg, --surface, --field darker for text boxes) so the game's own colours (skill groups, perk series, essence, health) stand out; one light-blue accent (--accent) for links, the active pill and main buttons; main buttons are the light accent with dark text (--btn-fill, --on-btn); borders are faint see-through lines; placeholders, selection, caret and scrollbars take the theme.
- **Text size floor (LK6 a, 18/11/2026):** no UI text is smaller than 13px; the only exception is the step numbers inside the perk ladder's diamonds and circles. Small capital labels keep their capitals with .06em letter spacing. A perk name beside the ladder wraps to a second line rather than being cut short. A styles test keeps the floor.
- **Tokens (HS10):** every colour, radius, space and duration comes from a named token in `:root`. New tokens are fine when they are named for the game (like --perk-20, --essence); a rule never uses a raw hex (tests/unit/styles-tokens.test.ts).
- **Words:** plain words, one name per thing, no internal ids. Colour always comes with a word. Real-world dates are DD/MM/YYYY; in-world time reads "Day 2 · Morning". Buttons start with a verb and say what happens (Equip, Take, Move to here); no button names a key (A8). Every game term on screen (tier names, Burden, Thread, Near Miss, All-50, Breakthrough) has one tip with its plain meaning, from one table in src/words.ts (GAME_TERMS, termAttrs), the same on every page, marked by a dotted underline (G3). Built on the check line's tier chips, the skill pop-up's Burden line, the gains panel's Thread heading, and the perk board's series key and All steps; a new term goes in the table and gets termAttrs where it shows.
- **Navigation (HS8):** an icon-only rail (60px wide), 52px items with no gap, each name in a tip on hover or keyboard focus; the active item is a pill filling the whole item. Nothing shrinks on short windows; the rail scrolls on its own. Its pages \[Proposed\]: Play, Character (/stats), Inventory (/inv), Quests (/quests), Journal and, when on, GM debug.
- **Tiles (HS9):** a page leads with tiles only where it has figures worth leading with. Every tile has a label and one figure with its unit; a sub-line only for the exception, and what the figure means is the tile's tip (SD4; the GM page keeps ⓘ). Coin always shows in coin words, never as a decimal amount.
- **Three depths (A2):** every figure has three depths: glance (the figure on the page), hover or keyboard focus (a card with its exact rows), click (a pop-up with every part). Nothing is only on hover.
- **Visibility (Shown / Left out) (HS11):** the one filter pattern for long lists (Quests, Achievements, Scars), used only where a page has a list worth filtering. Clicking the big box cycles Shown → Left out → off (several Shown = either). Clicking the small heading ("QUESTS ›") opens that group. Use the words "Shown" and "Left out", never "Only". A figure that can't be clicked this way goes in a text line under the row, not in the tile row. The Inventory page has no tiles: its boxes only repeated the group counts, so it shows the equipment panel and the Coin line, and lists each item once (SD11: no table under the pack).
- **Matching tiles:** tiles in one row share the same units and tip kind. When one page gets a feature, matching pages get it too.
- **Tables and grouping:** related entries fold under one head row with combined values (sum, earliest start, latest finish, average, "Mixed" when values differ, latest status) and a plain count chip ("3") that opens the sub-rows (HS4 a, 17/11/2026: ↻ is kept only for "↻ Another" on the action deck). Rows sort by the page's own key (turn, name, state), then title in natural number order; sub-rows are numbered only where their order means something, and no game table has such groups today. Clicking a field's text opens the details panel with that field highlighted, never an edit box, since the game writes every field (HS5 a); clicking empty space folds or unfolds. Every column title sorts (1st click biggest or A–Z first, 2nd reverses, 3rd back to page order). A folded row never shows more than the open one. Charts go under the table they explain. Number columns are right-aligned; numbers inside sentences are not (P4: groupedTable right-aligns any column whose every filled cell is a number).
- **Estimates (HS6):** every estimated number starts with ≈; its source line is the tile's tip and its link stays in the tile (SD4). It is editable only when the player sets the input behind it (the creation screen's practice swings).
- **Depth:** no glows and no shadows except the toast shadow (also the motion rules). Hover and focus never change size or position: only colour, opacity, background or an outline; tests/unit/styles-tokens.test.ts fails on a :hover or :focus rule that sets width, height, margin, padding, border width, font size, font weight or letter spacing (P6).
- **Tips (A7):** a hover tip waits --m-tip-delay (150 ms); keyboard focus shows it at once; once one tip is open (or closed within twice the delay), the next shows at once; leaving hides it at once (tipOn/tipOff in src/ui/kit.ts for item cards, Loot, the rail and the minimap; a transition-delay for the CSS tips). The minimap's hover text (rooms, rings, doors, stairs, furniture, people, its buttons) is this tip, not the browser's (Ali, 20/11/2026). Elsewhere the browser's own title tips keep the browser's timing.
- **Choice states (P5):** Available (normal, with its verb button), Can't afford (shows the shortfall: "needs 1 more point", "12 copper short"), Locked (a known requirement not met, shown), Sealed (unknown: a number only, K4). Each says its word or shape; none is colour alone, and Locked is never used on a sealed perk. Built: a perk's Take with no point reads "Needs 1 more point"; a class not yet open is a dashed card reading "From …" ("Locked" is its tip and screen-reader name, SD10); sealed perk milestones show their number only.
- **Risky choices (A6):** a choice that can be undone doesn't ask. A choice that can't be undone asks once, in place (a confirm row beside it, never a browser dialog), saying what is lost. No hold-to-confirm. The creation screen's Reset and Use recommended build get an Undo (until the build is changed again); an essence button that gives one up, and Let it go, get a confirm row (askInPlace in src/ui/kit.ts: what is lost, the choice, Keep it).
- **Previews (P3):** a choice with an exact result made outside a turn (taking a perk or essence, creation points, equipping) shows its change on the affected bars and figures before it is made, as a still striped segment. Rolled results and anything during a turn are never previewed (M-06); the ghost after a turn stays as M-33a. **\[To build\]**
- **Empty states (G4):** every page and pop-up has an empty line in plain words that says how it fills ("No essences yet. A foe you defeat can offer one."; wording *placeholder*). Checked 18/11/2026: every rail page, the Journal tables, the Gear and Essences pop-ups (since SD3 the Essences pop-up's empty cue is its dashed Empty slot box); the ones that only said "nothing" now say how they fill.
- **Contrast (G5):** after LK6, tests/unit/styles-tokens.test.ts fails when a text colour token (text, muted, faint, accent, ok, warn, danger, the perk colours and the skill-group colours, which name the trees) is under 4.5:1 against --bg or --surface. All of them clear 4.5:1, so the 3:1 large-text allowance isn't needed; --c14 was lightened a step (#b27567 → #b4786a) to clear it.
- **Cards and pop-ups (HS7):** tiles and the Character page's character card sit flat on the sky with no box and no lines (Ali: "I hate your stupid tile boxes", then "stupid lines"): space alone parts them, a hover tints the tile under the pointer, and a lit tile shows its figure in its colour. Pop-ups stay as built and movable ("the pop-up is nice"), and so do the game-moment cards (gains, perk unlocked, strike card). The Play column has no extra veil of its own (Ali: not transparent "for no reason at all").
- **Design changes:** every change to the look, big or small, is made with the five design skills loaded (interface-design, impeccable, frontend-design, ui-ux-pro-max, show-dont-tell-ui). Only a major change is shown first, as 2 to 4 named options drawn over the real page, one recommended, and Ali picks one; small fixes and bug fixes ship without a mock-up.

* **Creation screen:** the approved layout at https://claude.ai/artifact/HW45ivigqwZ5VDo4PWPv9u (Part 4 §4, Player creation). The story starts from it.
* **Loot:** when the character can see things to take where they stand, a Loot chip shows by the action box with how many ("Loot ×7"). It opens a large partial pop-up (src/ui/loot.ts) listing them by where they are: each body that is down (what it wears and what a search turned up, plus an open pack's contents), each open container on the ground (a chest's contents; a chest itself stays where it stands, ContainerView.fixed), and the ground. Each thing is a tick chip; Pick all picks everything; Take types one order for the picks, one phrase per place ("Take the bandage and the key from the halt-keeper, and pick up the sack") in the action box, unsent, like Move to here, so the turn still decides what can be taken. Built from PlayerView only. Back, ✕ or Esc closes it (a partial pop-up opened with `back` adds a step to the browser's history, as the full-screen one does, BG-220).
* **Item value:** an item's hover card and stats list its Value in coin words (itemValue, the price a trader starts from), each and for the whole stack when there are several, or Worthless.
* **Header:** where you are: the character's zone as the heading (Ali: the story's premise as a title told him nothing); under it the turn, then the place and parent place the zone sits in. Pointing at the sun icon shows the day, the time of day and the clock to the second ("Day 2 · Morning · 07:30:15"; Ali). The day and time of day follow the turn (T11). No mode word, Claude-call count or background-call note: each changes with the encounter mode, which is hidden. The call count and the running agents show in the GM view (play screen review UI1, UI3, 09/11/2026).
* **Your deals** (Ali, 08/11/2026, PM5 and Deals display K1 c): an "N deals" chip beside the scenario title opens the player's deals with NPCs (PlayerView.agreements) in a gold-edged, gold-tinted list: who each deal is with, what each side will do, and the turn it was made. A click outside or Escape closes it. There is no chip while there are no deals. A deal leaves when a party ends it as done or broken; deals between NPCs never show.
* **Layout A, "Book with a status strip"** (play screen review UI8): fixed rows (header, who's here when it drops below the header, story, input) so nothing jumps; one reading column of about 70 characters a line (Literata, sized by the fit-the-screen rule, line height 1.6) on the left, on the same see-through sky as the rest of the page (HS7: no extra veil behind it); the free space goes to the right margin. The side sheet is a narrow status strip by default (health level and bar, damaged-part chips, what is held, Stealthy); a click opens the full sheet as the 420px side panel, which pushes the story narrower. Open or closed is remembered per PC.
* **Who's here:** sits in the header line, between "Turn N · place" and the switches, while everyone fits; when too many people are around or the screen is narrow, it drops to its own fixed-height row under the header. It lists the actors the character fully perceives (PlayerView), by label until the name is known; "Nobody in view" when empty. Others' health shows only from Wounded down (Part 3), never as a bar (Voyage C2, U6, U7).
* **Narration log:** checked narration, newest last. While a turn runs, one neutral line for every turn, "The story is moving… 12 s", with "Taking longer than usual" after 90 s; it never names the agent (play screen review UI2). Story text is set in Literata.
* **Turn results** (UI4): under each turn's prose, in one place: the check line, every roll made against the player that they perceived (LogLine.incomingChecks), the turn's changes row, gear changes (LogLine.gear, ready-to-show rows the game writes when the turn ends; with the place, scenario and the player line's aimed flag they survive a reload), skill gains, XP, and gold cards for level-ups, achievements and perk milestones, which show even with mechanics off. The check line never sits above the prose it describes. There are no pop-up notifications: every change lands here in the M-21 order, and the story is the history. Gear rows merge the same item and say how many, in the game's ×N count ("Picked up: arrow ×3", "Used: bandage"; A1).
* **Check line:** the player's own roll this turn with no numbers: skill, tier, the other side's tier, how far apart they are ("1 tier ahead") and the result, e.g. "Medium weapons Great Success vs the slime's dodge Success · 1 tier ahead · full hit, body" (Voyage U10). It opens with an icon for the kind of roll (attack or talk), and each tier is a chip tinted green (Great Success and up), blue (Success), amber (Near Miss) or red (Failure and worse) (Ali, 07/11/2026).
* **Speaker lines:** each line the character heard, word for word under the narration: the speaker's name in a column on the left, sized to the longest name, with how it was said (whispering, shouting) under it, the line on the right, vertically centred (Narrator rule 5; Voyage U9, U12, style A picked by Ali 07/11/2026). *Stage directions* show in italics without the asterisks, outside the quote marks; the player speaks as "You".
* **Turn changes:** one folded row per turn ("Turn 14 3 Your right arm is crippled", the 3 a count chip, or "3 changes"; the summary names only Condition rows, since the bars already show health and morale, SD5) that opens to one typed row per change: Combat, Condition, Health. Built by code from the combat packets the character perceived (Voyage U14 to U16).
* **Commands and Ideas:** two buttons in the tool row under the input open a panel above it, at most 40% of the story's height; Escape, a pick or a send closes it. Commands lists the / commands (D-100). Ideas lists everyone in view for Fight and Talk and up to three actions each for Move, Stealth and Use, built by code from PlayerView (D-107); the same name twice reads "the other …". Picking one fills the box; nothing is sent until Send (the panel doesn't say so: SD4). The character's personality orders what is shown (src/ui/ideaLeans.ts IDEA_PULLS): each group and idea has pulls from Nerve, the personality axes and held values (a value pulls IDEA\_VALUE\_PULL, *placeholder* 50, points), groups and the ideas in each sort by total pull (under PERSONALITY\_LEAN either way counts as 0, ties keep their place), and an idea pulled away from by IDEA\_AGAINST\_NATURE (*placeholder* 50) or more is marked against your nature: the action deck shows a cracked mask after its name (tooltip and screen-reader name "Against your nature"; the icon is `against_nature` in scripts/icons.mjs). Nothing is added or hidden; the three-a-group cut is made before ordering, and a broken nerve's Get away stays first. A cautious, quiet character sees Stealth first and Talk last, marked; a daring, quick-tempered one sees Fight first. The Commands panel ends with a short Keys list: Esc, Enter, Tab, PageUp and PageDown and what each does (A5; KEYS in src/ui/play.ts, beside the handlers).
* **Input:** one free-text box across the reading column with Send beside it; a line starting with / is a command (Part 1 §7). The tools (Commands, Ideas, Same again, Aim, Sneak, the quick-heal slot) sit in one row under it. Pointing at (or tabbing to) someone on Who's here shows a tip with how far they are, their zone when it isn't yours, and whether your longest weapon in hand reaches them: "34 m away · in the yard · in reach of your shortbow (50 m)" or "beyond your arming sword's 1.1 m" (src/ui/play.ts whoDistance; PlayerView.player.reaches, each weapon in hand at its longest reach). Who's here lists everyone you perceive clearly in any zone, so a far foe in sight is there to aim at. Same again (BG-190) fills the box with the last order the game played out (a clarify and / commands are skipped; src/ui/play.ts lastOrder); nothing is sent until Send. What the player put off last turn shows as a dimmed suggestion in the empty box ("From last turn · click it or Tab to use, Esc to clear", with Use it and Clear buttons); clicking or tapping the empty box, Tab, or Use it puts it in the box, selected, so typing over it starts afresh; nothing is sent until Send. The next step of a queued order (D-20 b) shows the same way with "Next step of your order · Enter plays it, Esc drops the rest" and Play it and Drop the rest: Enter in the empty box sends it. PageUp and PageDown scroll the story while the box has focus. When the game asks a clarifying question with no answers of its own, the people, places and things it names that the player's view knows (people seen now or earlier, zones, items in hand or on the ground; matched with or without articles, longest first) can be tapped: a tap fills the box with the order it asks about, with that phrase in place of the name the order used ("I punch the man" and a tap on Osric Fell give "I punch Osric Fell"; with no single known name in the order, "…, I mean Osric Fell"), built from the first order of that run of questions so taps never pile up (BF72), and nothing is sent until Send. A question that came with its own answers has no tappable names: the answer buttons are the one way to answer it. The words sent after a question reach the Parser beside it (ANSWERING: the question and the first order it was about; src/agents/answering.ts), and Parser rule 5 reads them as one order (src/ui/play.ts clarifyPicks). The fake page's "punch the man" asks one.
* **Input keys:** the action box keeps focus; what it sends is trimmed, and an empty line sends nothing. Narration is paced (Play screen motion, M-10); skipping uses a click on the story or Esc, never a key that types (Voyage A7, decided by Ali 07/11/2026; was OQ-24; M-12).
* **Aim toggle:** marks this turn's attack as aimed (Part 1 §7); an aimed turn's player line ends with "· aimed" (from the line's saved `aimed`).
* **Rolls:** while SHOW\_MECHANICS is on, each turn's dice, modifiers and damage fold under "Rolls" in that turn's results, wrapping. A "Show rolls" icon switch in the header (d20 icon, aria-pressed and an accent fill when on, SD7) sets the story's showMechanics (UI6). Every turn keeps its rolls on its story line whether the switch is on or off, and the switch only shows or hides them, so turning it on shows past turns' rolls too. A roll whose outcome moved by whole steps (gear beyond the holder's skill, cover, tier perks, the natural edge) shows the outcome before them, the steps and their sources, and the outcome after (BF70, `tiered` in src/agents/game.ts): "d100 49 → Near Miss [weapon +9], 8 steps down [Short of an old sword's requirement -8] → Critical Failure−−−−−−"; the same for attacks, defenses, contests and checks. An NPC's own gear in its view says when the gear is beyond its skill and by how many outcome steps (OwnGearView.beyondYou), so it can choose its fists or another weapon.
* **Strike card** (Ali, 13/11/2026: "hitting someone is not satisfying"). When the player's own blow lands (grazed or better), a card rises over the story as the turn lands, before the prose (BF66; after the gains moment when one opens): the outcome in large letters, "Your arming sword · the slime's body", and what the player saw it change ("The slime is Wounded"), from LineCheck.strike (the combat packet's target, weapon and visible changes). Tiers by outcome: a graze only arrives; a full hit draws a slash across the card and pulses the word; a heavy hit (warning colour) or crushing hit (danger colour) adds the hit-stop on the word and a short shake of the card only. It lives a fixed --m-strike (1600 ms) whatever the turn held, takes no pointer events, is hidden from screen readers (the results row carries the same words). It runs on its own clock: a skip (M-12) only lands its motion and never removes it, (BF66; src/ui/play.ts strikeCard). The fake page's "attack", "attack light" and "attack hard" show the heavy, full and crushing cards.
* **Undo:** after /undo the undone turn's lines fold under one "Undone · turn N" row (struck through when opened), and the note reads "Turn N undone. Dice are not rolled back."
* **Story list:** the story is an append-only list (role=log) that announces each new turn once; the waiting line is a separate status. It draws the last 50 turns with a "Show earlier turns" button, and a redraw never closes an opened fold.
* **Out-of-character notices:** the scenario-planning failure marker (Agent Weaver), the rate-limit notice and the stop-play message (Leak prevention, validation and failure handling).
* **Character page as one screen** (Ali, 14/11/2026, CP1–CP5): the page fits the window with no page scroll: the character card on the left (who, health, level and XP, capstone choice, then the body figure beside power, standing attack and defense), the skill trees on the right, and a strip of four tiles along the bottom, Essences, Perks, Gear and Personality, each with a one-line summary, each opening its section in a pop-up. Two kinds of pop-up (CP2, src/ui/pop.ts): partial ones float, drag by their head and leave the page usable (Essences, Gear, Personality, a skill, the body, power); the full-screen one covers the page for a board too big for a panel (Perks, CP3), and partial pop-ups and the perk card open on top of it. Both close with ✕ or Esc (the topmost first) and open and close with one short fade (exit faster). Pop-ups form one stack (B1): Esc, ✕ and the mouse's Back close the topmost first, and focus returns to what opened it; only a full-screen pop-up blocks the page under it, partial pop-ups never do. Every figure on the character card opens its parts, read from the function that makes it, so the lines add up: standing attack and defense (effectiveSkill's own line, then each gear, trade and augment line), total health (base, Endurance, then body and prefix multipliers; totalHp lists them) and max morale (base, Resolve, achievements; maxMorale lists them, opened from the morale numbers on the Play sheet, where morale is shown) join skills and Power (P2). Health and morale add what is lost and what is left under the total. Clicking a skill opens what makes up its number (CP5): true and raw, each part that adds to it (perks, achievements, each essence, Hollowed), its rolls' augments and trades, what it adds to, its learning range and its next perk milestone, read from trueSkill itself (it lists its parts) so the lines always add up. Clicking the body figure opens each part with its state and pool; the Gear tile lists what is held and worn, and a piece opens its stats (the Inventory's rows) with a link to it in the Inventory; the Personality tile opens the Personality block. Links from Play (the Essences row, the level-up line's perk link) open the page with that pop-up up (charFocus).
* **Perks (Character page)** (Ali, 07/11/2026, K1–K4, S1, S7): a perk point is spent any time on the Character page, from the Perks tile, which opens the board full screen; the rail shows a dot while one is unspent, and the level-up line on Play links there (K2). A taken perk can be undone until the next turn is played, then it is permanent (K3). Taking or undoing a perk, abandoning or pinning a quest, and picking a defense on the action deck, is saved at once (the saved turn is amended, no new turn), so a reload keeps it (CS12, Ali 13/11/2026). So is a command that changes the character: /essence take, pass, give, sell and remove, and /title (BF40, BG-208). Every skill tree shows at once, each in its own compact box (the boxes side by side, wrapping into rows; a box as wide as its skills need), with no tabs and no By effect view (Ali, page comment, 14/11/2026). In a box each skill is a ladder of its seven milestones on a gold line showing true skill, the rail at the left of its column with the perk names beside it (cut short with the full name on hover in a narrow box), the All-50/100/150 steps as dotted lines across the box; on narrow screens the ladders become horizontal tracks. The Story / Effect toggle for the perk write-ups sits where they are written: in the perk card's head and beside the Innate and Linked headings, kept per browser. Milestones not yet reached are sealed and show only their number; the page never hints at what comes next (K4). Tapping a taken or open milestone shows a small card beside it with the perk's text and, when open, "Take · 1 point". Mock-ups: https://claude.ai/artifact/TVfF7qs3WnDdDZzzM6Yjf5
* **Journal page:** People (actors seen clearly now, or whose name is known; a label until the name is known; health only from Wounded down; then a Heard of group: names heard of people never met, from PlayerView.heardOf, leaving it once the person is listed above; src/ui/pages.ts heardOfNames), Places (the current place and those above it, plus places of facts the character witnessed, folded under their parent place; the map draws their zones) and a Timeline (turns played since the page was opened). Built from PlayerView, the Journal and the story log only, never the hidden store (Voyage U26, U28; Ali, 07/11/2026).
* **Power (Character page)** (Ali, 13/11/2026, D-273, FS17): the character card shows power() (level + combat + non-combat parts, the measure defeat XP and the stronger-foe achievements use) as one number above Standing attack and defense. Clicking it opens the details pop-up listing its parts from the same function (powerParts in src/engine/xp.ts, src/ui/power.ts), never a second formula: Level N (LEVEL\_RATING\_SHARE of the XP to reach it), Combat level L (the standard soldier it fights evenly; "placeholder" shows only in the GM view), and each non-combat group that adds something, largest first, with its rating and its share in words, then the total. The lines always add up to the number. Below the total a closed Ratings list (GR7) shows every compound row and skill group with anything in it, in skill points, with its gear portion.
* **Skill rows (Character page):** each skill takes two lines so the bar always spans its tree's width: the name with its true value at the right, then the bar with its learning window. When the true value differs from raw, the value gets a dotted underline and the raw value shows in a small card beside it only on hover or keyboard focus.
* **Inventory equipment (Ali):** the Inventory page opens with what the character has equipped, the way MMOs show it: one slot per Grip part (a two-grip item sits in its first grip; the other reads "(both grips)"), Body for armor, then each accessory slot, with empty slots dashed. Beside it, "In your pack" shows every unequipped item as an icon tile, with plain stacks of the same item merged and a ×N count; a container never merges, and its name button opens what it holds in place in a dashed box under it (nested ones too). Clicking a tile opens its side panel. A tile carries a mark only for the exception: damaged (a pool below 100) and locked (short of the requirement), each with its words in the tip (SD11). Each item has a line icon drawn for its kind of thing, tinted by item kind. Hovering an item, or focusing it from the keyboard, shows a card with its exact stats (the same rows as the side panel). Equipped items offer Unequip; weapons, shields, armor and accessories in the pack offer Equip. Each button sends the words a player would type ("I draw my…", "I put away my…", "I put on my…", "I take off my…") as a turn, so drawing, putting away and armor on or off all stay free. The buttons wait while a turn runs or after the player falls. **Gear compare (P1):** hovering, focusing or opening an item the player could equip, here and in the Loot pop-up, adds "Compared with your <what it pushes out>" (or "nothing (a free slot)"): each figure that changes (standing attack and defense, naming the weapon or defense when that changes too; the longest reach for a weapon; protection on each part the piece covers; the effective value of each skill its effects name; Power), with its word, old → new and ▲ +n (ok colour) or ▼ −n (danger colour), or "No figure changes". It equips the item on a copy of the player the way Equip would (into free grips, else in place of what is held of the same kind, else in place of everything held; armor in place of the pieces covering the same parts; an accessory into a free slot, else the first one) and reads the same functions as the Character page, never a second formula.
