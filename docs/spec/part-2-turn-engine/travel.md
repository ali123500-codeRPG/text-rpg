### Travel

Settled by Ali 11/11/2026 ("Travel on the Road", TV1–TV9). Code: src/engine/travel.ts. Roads are one long zone (Zone type, Roads).

- **When (TV1):** a player move with no main action whose route (the walk by walked metres, BG-1708) uses a road, outside tactical mode, with nobody hostile engaged with the player or perceiving them (suspicious or more), is a trip walked in one turn. So is a walk to an action (ACT-334) whose route uses a road: a real road trip (encounters, nightfall), the action done on arrival as crossing on at a road's end is (MQ15 a / MQ16 a; Ali, 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Map and minimap: one map, doors"). Otherwise it is a normal move, a slot at a time. A frontier off a road is at the road's far end (the side its label's compass names, else the end farthest from the player): the trip walks there, then crosses it.
- **Speed (TV2, SR5):** travel has one pace (Ali, 14/11/2026, SR5: the slow, normal and fast paces are gone). Travel speed is the walking speed (body type, armor, speed factor: moveSpeed) × CONFIG.TRAVEL\_SPEED\_SHARE (0.9), which gives a 1.4 m/s walker 4.5 km/h (US Army FM 21-18: about 4 km/h on a day march), and × TRAVEL\_DARK\_SPEED\_SHARE (0.6, 3 km/h) in the dark. NPCs moved on by the clock keep the same speed (NW11). Each hour of travel adds TRAVEL\_STRAIN\_PER\_HOUR (1) point of strain straight to the long-term stamina bar, whatever the armour, for everyone in the trip (payTravel in src/engine/fatigue.ts). Everyone travelling together goes at the slowest one's speed. Mounts and carts are parked (D-201).
- **Legs (TV4):** code walks the trip in legs of TRAVEL\_LEG\_MIN (60) game minutes. Each leg runs on the world clock (RE5, Ali 17/11/2026, "for now, to be checked"; resolve.ts tripWorld): it is walked in the same steps as a walk or a rest (comingNear stepS), each step moves the clock on with natural recovery, everyone else moves on their routines (catchUp, their legs summed for the Narrator) and the trip's group walks beside the player; the trip stops (`met`) at contact, the coming-near check, someone new in the player's senses (a traveller seen on the road ahead) or a fight in them, and the travel event carries a `stopped` effect (why, by) and the log a `stretch_stopped` line of kind `trip` ("Trip stopped after 57 min: someone came into sight or hearing"). Road minutes walked before it stopped are kept for the next roll. Minutes walked on a road count up across trips (RE1, Ali 17/11/2026; Actor.roadMin): every full TRAVEL\_LEG\_MIN of them, whichever trips it took, rolls once with a chance of ROAD\_ENCOUNTER\_PCT for the danger of the road the player is on then (× NIGHT\_ENCOUNTER\_MULT 2 in the dark), and the count starts again; a leg ends where the count fills, so the meeting happens there. Minutes walked on open land off the road count too (D-245 a, The owner, Questions hub page "Open parked items 4", 21/11/2026: `wildGround`, a region point whose land cover is not a settlement), and a count that fills there rolls at the wild rate (ROAD\_ENCOUNTER\_PCT wild), the Weaver told the danger is wild; a count filled in a settlement waits. A long walk whose route uses no road is no trip (tripGoal) and runs on the world clock as a walk (runStretch), but its minutes on open land count the same way and a full count rolls at the wild rate (BG-1831, `legEncounter`, the one roll trips use): a meeting stops the walk there (a `stretch_stopped` line of kind `walk`, why `encounter`), breaks the scenario as on a trip, and the walk carries on after (AT49). On a meeting, code makes the event roll (the encounter's tier) and the trip stops there: the scenario breaks (`encounter`), and the next scenario starts with that roll, its people placed on along the road the way the player was walking, where a still figure is first glimpsed (RE6, Ali 17/11/2026: CONFIG.ENCOUNTER\_VISIBILITY low, the sight table's 60 m). The Weaver gets `target.encounter` (zone, danger) and who it can be (RE4, Ali 17/11/2026): the road's unfilled travellers (NR18, cast by roster key) and the wild table for the land cover at the encounter point and the time of day (NR19, cast as a group); it casts from them first and invents someone only when the story needs it. Like any event roll, an encounter can be good or bad: its tier sets which (Ali, 11/11/2026); the danger only sets how often one happens. **A road encounter may open a side way (RE8 a):** the Weaver gives the zone beside the road `sideWay` {toward?, name?} (toward: a site id or a landmark name or id); code puts a junction on the road at the encounter point (a new node of the region, not a road end, so the road stays one zone) and a path from it to that site, to the landmark (its far end named after it, RoadNode.feature), or, with no toward, SIDE\_WAY\_M (800 m) off the road; the path keeps the road's danger. The zone becomes the junction's zone and is joined to the road's zone, and the path is built like every land way (WI2): a way to new ground along it, labelled from the land. Only with a running encounter on a land road; one per plan (validator bad\_zone).
- **Night (TV5):** a trip that reaches dusk stops there (`nightfall`); the next order is the answer: travel again to press on, or camp (sleep, wait). A trip stopped on the way (nightfall, an encounter, someone met) that carries an action keeps it with the walk: Keep walking travels on and does it on arrival; any other order cancels both (MQ15 a / MQ16 a; Ali, 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Map and minimap: one map, doors"). A trip that sets off at dusk, evening or night (CONFIG.ROAD\_DARK) presses on: at the dark speed, the event roll gets NIGHT\_TRAVEL\_EVENT\_MOD (−15, type situational, source "night travel"), and encounters come twice as often. Hunger and fatigue are parked.
- **Who comes (TV6):** anyone following the player (the follow routine) who sees the player and is seen by them travels along and stands beside it at the end (BF9). Being a bandmate alone takes no one along (D-202 b): a bandmate comes only by following. Agreeing a time to set off is parked (D-202).
- **What is shown (TV7):** one `travel` event with one `travelled` effect (metres, minutes, night, stop); the Narrator gets it as one passage ("you travel 9.2 km in 2 h 2 min"). The mechanics line reads "Travelled 9.2 km · stopped at nightfall" and "Time passes: …". On the Map, a road you are on shows your point along it: your pin at your own position (Actor.pos) on the road, and the road's card says how far along you are (alongRoad in src/ui/mapData.ts; MAP-74). Landmarks are parked (D-203).
- **Noticing (TV8, RE6):** no roll of its own (RE6, Ali 17/11/2026): the encounter's people stand where they are first glimpsed, in the dark only half seen, and anyone lying in wait hides by its routine (such as bandit\_ambush), so the game's own sight and stealth rolls (AT81) decide who notices whom. At the land's 100 m grain no land cover blocks sight inside 60 m. What else could be hidden by a road (side paths, lost things) comes with landmarks.
- **Off the road (TV9):** parked. NPCs travelling between places use these rules in the NPCs-stay-in-the-world work.
