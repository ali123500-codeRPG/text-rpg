### Who thinks for each actor

Every actor gets exactly one order per turn, from the first controller in this order that has one: fired trigger > broken (Part 3, Morale) > agent (Planner, Responder or Voice) > onAlert > routine (desired state) for an actor not engaged, or its type's combat list for an engaged one (Fallback behavior). An inactive (incapacitated) actor gets none. Significance (control: agent or code) only decides who thinks when the actor is engaged.

| Actor this turn | Controlled by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inactive (incapacitated) | Nothing | No order, trigger, routine or reply; a revived actor is active again next turn |
| A trigger it owns fired | The trigger's order | Overrides everything below; when several fire, the first in plan order wins |
| Addressed spokesperson | Its dialogue call: Responder if it has an agenda, else Voice | Its Planner or combat-list order is discarded this turn |
| Significant and engaged | One Planner call | Engaged = active, and believes (suspicious or higher) in an active actor it is hostile to |
| Significant and in a fight, between turning points (NV1 b) | Its type's combat list (Fallback behavior) | A Planner call comes only on its first fight turn and at a turning point: someone on either side goes down, breaks or yields, it is asked to yield or offered terms, or a newcomer joins (src/agents/game.ts fightKey). Schemers and goal holders, deal partners, errand runners and the startled keep a call every turn (plansEveryTurn); schemer = holds a secret, a unique, or the sneak role \[Proposed\] |
| Minor and engaged | Its type's combat list (Fallback behavior) | \[Proposed\] |
| Over the call cap | Its type's combat list | \[Proposed\] |
| First leaving unaware of a wary actor (opposed or hostile toward it), or first perceiving an attack or alarm. A wary actor it only heard of in talk (hearsay) does not count; one passed on by an alert (a call for help, an alarm, a warning) does (PQ11 a, W1) Knowing one from a scenario's opening is not leaving unaware (BF1): the cast starts aware of everyone it can see except those set up hiding, armed with no alarm | Its routine's onAlert order, once, on the next turn only: a higher order that turn (trigger, flight, its agent) spends it (BG-94) | Then the alert variant of the routine it was following, until DECAY\_TURNS pass with no attack or alarm perceived; then it returns to the routine saved in returnTo. |
| Not engaged | Its routine's desired state | Even during a Tactical turn; companions default to follow |
| No valid order | Engaged: its type's combat list. Not engaged: its routine's desired state | Failed or late calls, dropped orders |

Every code mind, combat lists included, reads only its actor's ActorView, never GameState. `desiredState` takes only the part of it that it reads (`RoutineInput`: the routine, zone, slots, the belief about the player and the map), so the world clock can ask it for every NPC on every step without building the whole view.

**Creatures that can't speak (NV5).** An actor whose race is below `intelligent` (slimes, beasts) is never a spokesperson and gets no Responder or Voice call; code runs it. Words the player says to it give a `not_understood` effect: the story says it does not understand words.

**Call cap.** At most MAX\_PLANNER\_CALLS Planner calls run per turn (config). Engaged actors beyond the cap run their type's combat list (Fallback behavior), the same one minor actors use \[Proposed\]; actors nearest the player get calls first. Dialogue calls are outside this cap; spokespeople are capped by MAX\_SPOKESPEOPLE. The values and the ordering rule are still to be reviewed (D-18).

**Control by actor and state.** Who controls each kind of actor in each state:

| Actor | Calm | Alarmed (not engaged) | Engaged | Addressed | Incapacitated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Player | Parser, after submit | Parser | Parser | Not applicable: NPC replies reach the player as perceived events | Flagged: a story branch (deferred) |
| Significant actor (control: agent) | Routine, at resolution | onAlert once, then its alert routine | Planner, in the background before submit; over the cap or late: its type's combat list | Responder if it has an agenda, else Voice, after the Parser; returns its one action; its Planner is cancelled | Inert |
| Minor actor (control: code) | Routine | onAlert once, then its alert routine | Combat list, at resolution (Fallback behavior) | Responder or Voice, as above; its combat-list order is discarded | Inert |
| Companion (player-band member) | Follow or wait routine | onAlert once, then its alert routine | Planner or combat list, by significance | Dialogue call, as above: you command a companion by talking to it | Inert |
| Guarded NPC (agenda, not engaged) | Routine plus its own triggers | onAlert once, then its alert routine | As significant or minor | Responder | Inert |
| Ordinary NPC or bystander | Routine | onAlert once, then its alert routine | Only once hostile (attacked, or persuaded into it): Planner or combat list | Voice | Inert |

In both tables, "its alert routine" means the alert variant of the routine the actor was following (Routine.alert). An actor whose routine has none keeps that routine.

In every row a fired trigger replaces the actor's order at turn start, or mid-turn if the actor hasn't acted; for an addressed actor, its dialogue call still runs and its line is kept only as a bark. Every controller reads only its own actor's view: OppositionView for the Planner and Responder, DialogueView for the Voice, ActorView for code minds and defaults.

When each controller runs in a turn: turn start (triggers fire, engaged set computed) → background (Planners) → after the Parser (dialogue calls, in parallel; replies in the exchange slot) → resolution (code minds, deeds, mid-turn triggers, onAlert) → Narrator.

#### Fallback behavior \[Proposed\]

An engaged actor with no agent order this turn gets one order from its type's combat list. That covers minor actors, actors over the call cap, actors whose Planner call is late or failed, and actors whose order was dropped. An actor that is not engaged and has no valid order follows its routine's desired state. There is no separate default behavior.

Code checks the combat list top to bottom; the first rule that matches gives the order:

1. **Flee or cower:** the actor is broken (its morale bar reached Broken, Part 3, Morale). A broken nerve blocks attacking and any walk toward a foe (Ali, BF37, BF79 b): any attack, grab (restrain) or filch, and any perk move except Battle Cry (a broken one can still roar; minds NERVE\_ALLOWED\_PERK\_MOVES). A walk or run away is allowed: one that ends farther from every foe it fears (anyone still in the fight who is its foe, or whom it yielded to, in its zone or the one it walks to; src/engine/nerve.ts walksAway, asked before every walk). Talking, begging, persuading, lying, yielding, giving, using an item, picking up, hiding, and unlocking or opening a door or container within reach still work. Its Planner call is skipped; whatever order it does get (a dialogue reply, a trigger, an alert) loses only the blocked parts (minds nerveHolds: an attack's own close-in walk goes with it) and may keep a walk, which runs only if it leads away. With nothing allowed left it flees (minds fleeOrder): one link toward its exit or refuge slot, unless that way runs into a foe's zone, else into a linked zone farther from the nearest foe; it cowers (activity cowering) only when no way leads away or it is already at its exit or refuge. Slimes flee too (BF79 b). Fleeing NPCs stay in real zones. At **Cowering**, below Broken (CONFIG.MORALE\_COWER\_AT × max morale lost, 1.2, a balance lever), it cowers and cannot even flee. A surrendered actor (Part 3, Morale) holds.
2. **Attack:** an active hostile actor it believes in (aware or tracking) is in its zone. It attacks with the skill of the weapon it holds, else its type's attackSkill (BG-258: a guard with a sword in hand never punches because the foe stands within fist reach), targetPart null, aimed false. Target: the actor that last hit it, else the player, else an rng pick.
3. **Close in:** it moves one link, by the shortest path, toward the lastKnownZone of its most recent belief about a hostile actor. Never into a frontier.
4. **Search:** it is already in that zone and no hostile actor is there. It searches by the player's rule (Explore mode, Searching).
5. **Hold.**

**Skilled rows (D-17, Tactics tree, TC17-TC23).** Between rule 1 and rule 2 sit the rows of the library table FIGHT\_RULES (src/library/fightRules.ts), checked in table order (TC21 a, set by hand). A row may fire when the actor's value for its skill reaches the row's minimum: its own skill, or the best Command of a bandmate whose shouted words it hears clearly, whichever is higher (TC7 a). What a row needs comes from what it perceives (TacticsView, src/views/tactics.ts; given only to the combat list, never to its Planner). Built rows (Battle Sense; minimums are placeholders):
- **B1 Call for help** (20): its fight has more foes than its side (anyone not hostile to it who fights one of its foes); it calls for help, once a fight.
- **B2 Gang up** (35): an ally fights a foe within one move (turnMetres, carried time included) of it; it attacks that foe.
- **B4 Back off when hurt** (65): Wounded or worse and outnumbered, with a bandmate in sight who is not fighting; it walks to that bandmate instead of attacking.
- **S1 Draw into the pack** (Stratagem 40): it fights one foe with no one on its side, and a bandmate in sight who isn't fighting stands too far to strike that foe (hostileNearM); it walks toward that bandmate, so the foe follows into a fight it is outnumbered in.
- **F1 Out of the shooter's sight** (Fieldcraft 30): a foe shot at it this turn or the last from beyond striking reach (hostileNearM) and still sees it; it walks to the nearest zone within one move the shooter can't see.
- **F2 Into cover** (Fieldcraft 50): a foe it sees holds a weapon with a ranged mode; it walks to the nearest zone within one move with more cover than where it stands.
- **F3 Into the dark** (Fieldcraft 75, TC25 a): it walks to the nearest darker zone within one move from where its own sight still reaches every foe it fights clearly (no guess at the foe's eyes).
The Fieldcraft rows (TC18 a) walk a step inside the doorway they enter by (views/tactics.ts oneMoveZones: every zone reached by its own walk within turnMetres (carried time included), however many ways off; BG-304), and none fires while a foe stands near enough to strike it, so a row never walks out of a melee.
- **B5 Finish the hurt one** (75): it faces two or more foes and one looks hurt (others' health shows from HEALTH\_SHOWN\_FROM down); it attacks the one that looks most hurt.
- **S2 Strike from hiding** (Stratagem 55): it sees none of the foes it hunts now, so it believes none sees it; instead of closing in openly (rule 3, same pick and chase limit) it sneaks one way toward that foe, so its first blow can come from hiding (minds/routines.ts stalkOrder).
- **S3 Feigned flight** (Stratagem 70; TC19 a, TC24 a, TC50 to TC52 a; engine/feint.ts): outnumbered, not Wounded and not broken, once a fight, it runs as if broken (fleeOrder). Each foe that sees it clearly rolls Insight against its Guile; one that does not win outright believes it and sees it Broken, and is never told; its own band is not fooled and its morale is untouched. While the feint is on, a believer within one move of it with no other foe within one move of that believer is turned on and struck, and that blow lands as on an unaware foe; with no such follower the feint ends after CONFIG.FEINT\_S (18 s, placeholder). A feint under way comes before every other row.
B3 Ready the shield (Battle Sense 50, FX3; Ali's pick, since a Parry defender facing a missile already uses its best usable defense): shot at this turn or the last by a foe it fights, with no shield in hand, it draws a working shield it carries stowed into free hands. The draw goes beside whatever the fight list chose (a free gear op before the action, minds/routines.ts fallbackOrder), never instead of it. S3 (feigned flight) is built (TC50 to TC52).

Like every code mind, it reads only its ActorView. The rng pick uses the turn's seeded rng, so replay stays exact. Its orders carry source 'fallback'.

**Per-type data.** Each body type, or the NPC template when it sets one, names fallback.attackSkill, used when the actor holds no weapon (one rule, ruleset fallbackAttackSkill, also used for the ambush roll and shown to its Planner). Slime: unarmed\_natural; slimes have only the general area, so targetPart is always null.

Rules 1 to 5 stay fixed in code; the skilled rows between them are data (FIGHT\_RULES, D-17).

**The Planner's tactics word (TC4 a).** Bands carry no tactics word any more; the Weaver no longer writes one. Each NPC's word (simple, competent or cunning; CHARACTER.tactics, Planner rule 5) is the last TACTICS word whose CONFIG.TACTICS\_FROM value its own Battle Sense reaches (0, 35, 65; placeholders, past 100 is D-370). The Voice never gets it.

**How the Tactics skills learn (TC6, TC15, TC16; src/engine/tacticsMarks.ts).** Only the player learns. Battle Sense marks on the reaction roll at the start of a fight, against the best Reaction skill among the foes in it (D-132 closed). A fight the player survives (still up at its end) marks each Tactics skill once, against the strongest foe's (highest power) same skill. A foe untrained in the skill never teaches; the learning window holds as for every skill. A row or perk that fires teaches nothing yet (TC6 c, TC15 b inert, D-373).

**Tactics 20 perks (TC28 a, TC36 c, TC30 a; rules in docs/perks.md).** Press the Opening (Battle Sense 20): an ally's hit opens that foe to the owner's next attack, +1 tier, until the owner's next turn ends. Fall Back on Me (Command 20): a broken ally in earshot of the owner may walk toward the owner even past a foe (src/engine/nerve.ts nerveLetsWalk), and a code-run one runs there instead of fleeing. Hug the Cover (Fieldcraft 20): in cover, the first ranged attack on the owner each turn gets −1 tier. Draw Them In (Stratagem 20): a free move once a fight; each foe that sees the owner clearly rolls Insight against Stratagem, and one that does not win comes at the owner on its next turn whatever it chose (merge.ts), and the owner's next attack on it gets +1 tier. Play Dead (Guile 20, TC32 a, TC44 to TC48 a; src/engine/playDead.ts): the turn's main action (DU44, 1 s), open the turn a blow leaves the owner Wounded or worse but not Down and the next. Each foe that sees the owner clearly rolls Insight (its answer to a lie) against Guile; one that does not win outright takes the owner for Down: its views show the owner incapacitated, its blows at the owner are dropped (resolve.ts doAttack, merge.ts), and it never learns it was fooled. A foe that first sees the owner clearly later rolls the same way, and a believer within grip reach rolls again once a turn. Each foe judges on its own until the foes-share page (WN13) says otherwise. Any act, move, words, gear or way ends it for every foe, and so does defending against a blow; the owner's first attack on a foe that still believed it is unaware.

**NPC perk moves (TC49).** Every perk move is usable by NPCs. An agent-run NPC's view lists the perk moves it owns (self.perkMoves, as the player's) and its Planner or Responder may give a perkMove; one it doesn't own or can't aim is dropped (invalid_perk_move), and one that takes the turn turns its action into hold. A code-run NPC weighs the moves it owns on the order its fight list gave (TC53 to TC59 a; engine/npcPerkMoves.ts), one a turn, first that fits: Play Dead when its window is open and it is Afraid or worse (CONFIG.PLAY_DEAD_AT) or two or more foes (CONFIG.PLAY_DEAD_FOES) are within one move, a broken one too; Rally when an ally it would reach is Afraid or worse (CONFIG.RALLY_AT); Battle Cry on the fight's first turn with a foe in sight and none within melee reach, roaring while it closes in; Dirt in the Eyes with its first melee attack of the fight, at that foe, with a free hand; Bluff at each foe the first time it comes within melee reach; Draw Them In on the fight's first turn with a foe in sight and none within melee reach, unless it backs off or flees. A move that takes the turn makes the order a hold that keeps its walk. While it plays dead, it keeps still until no foe that saw through it is in sight and no believer is within one move, then sneaks away by its flight way; with no way away it stays down.

**Tactics 100 perks (TC35 b; rules in docs/perks.md).** Never Flat-Footed (Battle Sense 100): a foe the owner has seen this fight can never catch it unaware. At each turn's end the owner's engagement notes everyone it sees (src/engine/flatFooted.ts noteSeen); an attack by one of them is no ambush and doesn't make the owner act last, and Ghost Blade doesn't land. A sleeping or downed owner is still struck as unaware. Rally (Command 100, TC39 a): the turn's main action once a fight (1 s, all-out, DU43), a Loud call; each ally who perceives it at any level or stands within the projected reach (TC37 a, TC38 b) makes the rally roll with the owner's Command (TC43 a, src/engine/auras.ts rallyCall): 10 morale per tier above Near Miss, Rallied (TC41 a), and a lifted break (TC42 a). Foes and the owner get nothing.

**Tactics skills on NPCs (TC9 a).** Role bands: Soldier High Battle Sense, Leader High Command, Hunter High Fieldcraft, Sneak High Stratagem, Skirmisher Moderate Battle Sense, Beast Moderate Battle Sense and Stratagem (only where its race can learn the tree, so never a slime). A banded skill raises the NPC's level from its skills like any other (a Trained guard is level 9, not 8).
