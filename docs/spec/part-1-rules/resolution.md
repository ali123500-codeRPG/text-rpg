### 4. Resolution

The dice deal the hand; skills and perks decide what the character can do with it.

**Layer 1 — event roll.** Rolled once per scenario, for the player, at scenario setup (Part 2, Scenario setup). It deals the hand: it decides the who and what of the scenario (its event, cast, challenge and gain), not how alert anyone is. Code reads it with a fixed base in place of skill (EVENT\_BASE, *placeholder*, 50):

```
margin = d100 + EVENT_BASE + net modifiers − 100
```

Only three sources may modify the roll, each its own modifier type: perks, gear and statuses (blessings, buffs, curses, debuffs) whose library entry names the event roll. A gear effect counts only when its library entry names the event roll; it may be active while only carried (Part 5 §2). Code reads the player's inventory once, at scenario setup step 2. Injury, place, difficulty, skills, terrain and tactics never modify it. It reads the normal roll table (MARGIN\_BANDS, the same bands as a skill roll; ER3), so with the placeholder base the totals (d100 + net modifiers) read as follows. Without modifiers of 25 or more, only a natural roll reaches a critical tier:

| Total (d100 + modifiers) | Tier (ID) | Stakes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under −25 | Critical Failure (−3) | A real challenge with little gain; the harder of the two **\[Proposed\]** |
| −25 to 24 | Failure (−2) | A real challenge with little gain |
| 25–49 | Near Miss (−1) | A fair event tilted slightly against the player: gain one step below the challenge (ER2) |
| 50–74 | Success (0) | A normal event, gain matched to challenge |
| 75–124 | Great Success (1) | Good fortune inside a real conflict: challenge moderate to major, a prize one or two steps above it (SW1 b, SW2 a) |
| 125+ | Critical Success (2) | The best of luck: gain extreme, challenge major or extreme, the same as Critical Failure (D-247) |

**Natural edges** apply after the bands: a natural 01–05 drops one tier and 96–100 raises one tier (CONFIG.NATURAL\_EDGE, 5 at each end); no natural roll moves two tiers (Ali, 12/11/2026: the old 01 −2 and 00 +2 are gone). The result stops at Critical Success and Critical Failure unless the player has opened tiers past them (`tierUnlocks` on perks and achievements, D-211). Each opened tier widens the natural critical range by EVENT\_CRIT\_NATURALS (5): with one opened above, 96–100 is Critical Success+ and 91–95 Critical Success; failures mirror from 01. The Weaver is told the tier with its plus or minus signs.

**Layer 2 — acts on things** (OD1 b, OD2 a, OD3 a, OD4 a, OD11 a, questions page "Obstacle difficulty"). No-dice checks are gone. An act with a skill on a thing (use\_item, interact, other, and observe with a skill that is not sight or hearing; GRADED\_ACTS, resolve.ts doGeneric) is made against a grade through DIFFICULTY: none 20, minor 35, moderate 50, major 65, extreme 80 (placeholders). The grade is the thing's own (a piece of furniture, a container, the barrier on a way checked), else the Parser's (action.grade, from fixed meanings: none anyone manages it, minor a trained amateur, moderate an average professional, major a seasoned expert, extreme about the best real person), which is then kept on the piece of furniture or container so asking again can't regrade it. An NPC's act takes its grade from its own agent (Planner or Responder: action.grade, the same meanings; OD14 a), and a beat (a trigger's then, from the Planner, the Weaver's plan or a check-in) carries its own grade and fall (BF96 a; a word off the scale or a fall that is not a distance is dropped in bindTemplate). Every agent that orders an act gets the same sentence, built from GRADED\_ACTS (prompts.ts gradeRule). An act with neither is Moderate and is flagged every time (log code grade\_gap, a bug code, so it shows on the Bugs page). **Sizing someone up is a contest, never graded** (BF97 a): observe with Insight at a person is the looker's Insight against the other's Guile (speech.ts sizeUp; BG-320: the face kept is a lie told with the body, as in Play Dead and feigned flight, while Falsehood is for spoken lies), only on someone the looker sees clearly (else the look is wasted, out\_of\_sight, with no roll). A win reads what they show (PersonRead): their mood (the morale word), how they feel about the looker (their stance toward them) and whether they hide something (a secret they hold or a lie told lately), never what. An NPC keeps it on its belief about them (Belief.read, shown to its agents); the player's goes to the Narrator (event read). A loss or a tie reads nothing beyond plain sight. Any act on a person is never flagged grade\_gap. Parked: an Insight read of someone's power (D-365). **Roll only when in doubt** (OD1 b, obstacleGate): effective skill OBSTACLE\_SURE\_GAP (25) or more above the grade is a sure Success with no roll; the grade more than OBSTACLE\_REACH\_GAP (25) above it is out of reach with no roll (the event carries beyond for the Narrator); in between one roll with SE23's grade modifier (gradeMods, source grade). Both gaps are placeholders (D-343). Trying a thing shows its grade to the one who tried (OD12). **What each tier does** (OD5 a, resolve.ts tierResult): Critical Failure (or worse) is a mishap, the first that fits: the tool used breaks into its broken version (BK3; the item used for use\_item, else the tool picked; never one that can't break), a climb or balance falls its drop (action.fall, metres, on the falls table, SD7), else a loud noise, one step up. Failure puts the thing beyond the actor at that skill until its effective skill beats what it had (as BA2; kept on the thing's beyond, by actor and skill, so only on a furniture, container or barrier record). Near Miss: not yet, try again next action. Success: done. Great Success: done, one noise step quieter. Critical Success: done, and the Narrator may add one small extra the act opens up in the telling, never a person, item, exit or clue. The act also learns (recordUse) and can meet a check\_passed secret (its skill, aimed at its person, at minTier or better; a sure act counts as Success). The scenario's challenge grade only steers the Weaver (OD4 a). Past Extreme the table goes on for tasks beyond a human body (CR4): mortal limit 100, beyond 150, mythic 200, transcendent 300, divine 400, each the skill that just manages it (Transcendent added to match the rank ladder, D-335). These are **the one ladder past 100** (Ali, 16/11/2026): task grades, NPC ranks and equip levels all end in the same rows, names in `PAST_100` (src/scales.ts; ranks keep their saved keys through `RANK_KEY_PAST_100`) and numbers in `PAST_100` and `PAST_100_STEP` (src/config.ts, placeholders), which DIFFICULTY, RANK\_ROWS anchors, RANK\_BEYOND\_STEP, EQUIP\_NEED and EQUIP\_BEYOND\_STEP read, so a step is added or moved once. These five are task grades only (CR12): a scenario's gain, a lock's grade and the Weaver's choices stay within none to extreme, and nothing picks them in play until D-219 says when they apply. The same rule applies to every actor (OD9 a). As rolls, these take wounds like any roll. Exception, looking around (Ali, 07/11/2026, Q17): the player's observe action with a notice skill (sight, hearing) is a roll, not a check: d100 with the skill and the light modifier where the player stands, with no scene number (SE15); it learns like a solo roll at Moderate. Success or better shows everyone in the player's zone and the zones in sight of it clearly. Look around is also a weak search (SE14, Explore mode, Searching): a contest against everyone hiding within its range, one tier down.

- **Combined checks:** some perks let a check add several skills, or half of another skill.
- **Gear:** flat skill bonuses from item effects that are active (Part 5 §2–3). They enter effective skill; item stats and the rare noted roll modifiers are read separately.
- **Wounds:** superseded by Part 3, which is the latest for all HP-related items.
- **No critical rolls.** There is no separate AGG-style Critical Roll; Critical Failure and Critical Success are ordinary tiers with no special effects (critical effects are deferred).

**Layer 2b — combat rolls.** Attacks and defenses are rolls, not checks. Both use one function:

```
margin = d100 + SKILL_WEIGHT × effective skill + roll modifiers − ROLL_NEEDED
SKILL_WEIGHT = 0.5, ROLL_NEEDED = 63.5 (placeholders); d100 runs 1–100 (00 = 100)
```

Tiers on margin: below −75 Critical Failure; −75 to −26 Failure; −25 to −1 Near Miss; 0 to 24 Success; 25 to 74 Great Success; 75+ Critical Success. Margins may end in .5; compare exact values.

**Scale keys.** Standing design rule for anything in the code with levels (Ali, 07/11/2026, restated 08/11/2026 NV3): every ordered scale (tiers, band tactics, zone light and cover, stance, noise, visibility, and in turn the others) has one table in `src/scales.ts`: a row per level, lowest first, each a stable key and its display name. Code and saves use only the key, a level's order is its row position, and display names appear only in text for the player and for Claude, so adding or renaming a level edits only the table (Ali, 07/11/2026). Numbers that go with a level (a reach, a modifier) are keyed by the level's key in config, never by its position. Noise, quietest first: silent, faint, hushed, quiet, murmur, normal, raised, loud, blaring, deafening (murmur, raised and blaring added 11/11/2026). Each noise level is a loudness in decibels at 1 m from real references (CONFIG.NOISE\_DB; the library's Noise levels table gives each its reference: faint 20 dB breathing, hushed 30 a whisper, quiet 40 a library, murmur 50 low talk or rain, normal 60 conversation, raised 70 a busy tavern, loud 88 a shout, blaring 100 a hammer on an anvil, deafening 120 a thunderclap). **Hearing by real metres** (BG-57, HB1–HB8, Ali 12/11/2026; perception/noise.ts `soundDb`, `hearingOf`): a sound's decibels at 1 m less 20·log10(metres) (the inverse-square law; never nearer than SAME\_ZONE\_M, 1 m) less what it loses at each crossing, along whichever route loses least (HB4; D-199 a, Questions hub page "Open parked items 3", 21/11/2026: by material and thickness, `soundLossDb` in plan/line.ts): each wall, shut barrier or pane loses its material's soundDb (library barrierMaterials, from real insulation figures, STC) at that material's example thickness, plus CONFIG.SOUND\_DB\_PER\_DOUBLING for each doubling of thickness (the mass law). A wall or barrier kind whose sound is open (a low wall, fence, arcade, grate or arrow slit) and bars lose nothing; a floor or roof reads CONFIG.FLOOR\_MATERIAL, timber boards (Ali, decision card, 21/11/2026). A barrier loses nothing while it stands open (BG-70) or is broken through; a way with no barrier loses nothing (a doorway holds a door unless it is doorless, BG-312), a window with no shutters a glazed wall's pane; two zones with rectangles that touch on the same floor with no link lose by the edge between them (wall, window, open or low). It is heard (a sound, not who or what) while at least CONFIG.HEARING\_FLOOR\_DB (20 dB, a quiet place at the old 0.5 game share), and clearly from CLEAR\_ABOVE\_FLOOR\_DB (15, ANSI S12.60) over it (HB1). Per-zone background noise (HB2, HB3, HB6: a names table from still to crowded, a background per zone kind, a busy tavern room) is removed for now (Ali, 12/11/2026, FR1) and parked as D-231. A whisper said to someone within WHISPER\_REACH\_M (1 m) is said leaning in, from WHISPER\_LEAN\_M (0.3 m) (HB8, an estimate); a whisper not meant for you is heard, not understood. Voices do not rise in noise (HB7: no Lombard effect). In the open a normal voice is clear to 18 m and heard to 100 m (the library's Noise levels table gives every level, `openAirReachM`). A perk's `hearingDb` makes every sound that many dB louder for its owner (RD4: Keen Ear 10, about three times as far); silence stays unheard. Visibility, least first: unseen, faint, slight, close, low, normal, high, bright, glaring (NV1–NV6, Ali, 08/11/2026; slight and bright added 16/11/2026 to close the two biggest jumps). Each is how far it is made out clearly and glimpsed, in metres, in daylight with a clear view, from real references (CONFIG.SIGHT\_M; the library's Visibility levels table): faint 0.5/2 m a palmed coin, slight 2/6 m lips moving or a lockpick being worked, close 6/20 m a nod or an expression, low 15/60 m someone keeping still (where witnesses stop naming faces), normal 25/200 m someone walking, high 50/500 m a fight or someone running, bright 100/1000 m a galloping rider or a banner waving, glaring 200/2000 m a fire or a torch at night. Perception measures them in metres (step 2 PR 2, `sightLevel` with position `seeingM`): the straight line with sight from the observer to where the event happened (GameEvent.pos, the actor's point), never less than SAME\_ZONE\_M (1 m); full out to the clear metres, partial out to the glimpsed metres. In a zone without rectangles the same zone counts as SAME\_ZONE\_M and each sight link as LINK\_PACES × PACE\_M metres, which gives the old links (`sightReach`, still in the library table): faint [−1, 0], close [0, 2], low [1, 7], normal [3, 25], high [6, 62], glaring [25, 250]. A still figure (NS2 stealthStart) is kept out to low's 15 m and a look (Q17) makes out figures to normal's 25 m. A dark zone caps sight at partial. **Words carry only by sound** (real distances, 11/11/2026): a line's words reach an observer only when its sound does, and heard clearly (Ali, 11/11/2026, BG-17: a sound heard only partly is a voice without its words; Every Sense One still carries them) (the perception's `heard` flag, `hearsWords`), so a talker seen across a field is seen, not heard; this covers the words shown, what is passed on (told), deals, quest hand-ins, NPC speech rolls at the player and Overheard. A line said beside another action (a bark) is as loud as the louder of the action and the voice. A careful look (Q17) makes out a still figure as far as a moving one (normal's clear reach), and a figure turning stealthy stays seen as far as a still one is made out (low's clear reach). Addressing everyone reaches those who hear the player's voice clearly at the volume chosen (BG-71); that check, a called order (trade) and stepping up to speak all use perception's one rule `hearsClearly`, with the listener's hearing perks (BG-88). Armor weight, lightest first: light, medium, heavy. Worn armor makes body-moving events (move, attack, perk move, break free, pick up) louder by CONFIG.ARMOR\_NOISE\_STEPS noise steps: light +0, medium +1, heavy +2 (placeholders); a stealthy posture doesn't cancel it (AN1–AN4, Ali, 08/11/2026). Tier keys, worst first: critical_failure, failure, near_miss, success, great_success, critical_success. Tier arithmetic (shifts, gaps) works on a tier's level, its row position; a roll shifted past either end keeps the end key plus `beyond`, the steps past it, shown as one + or − per step (Critical Success+). Saves store keys (schema v3; v1 and v2 saves are migrated on load). Schema v5 split hostile from opposed: older saves' opposed becomes hostile, so they play as before, and a logged more\_opposed step becomes colder.

**Tables added by the code scan** (Ali, 12/11/2026, CS7 to CS9): hit outcomes (HIT\_OUTCOMES, missed to crushing hit; src/ruleset/hits.ts names the shared checks: struck = the body was reached, met = the body or a shield, defended = dodged, parried or blocked), grades (GRADE\_LEVELS, none to divine; the Weaver, barriers, gains and locks use none to WEAVER\_GRADE\_TOP, Extreme) and minted item quality (QUALITY\_LEVELS; a level's multiplier in the quality formulas is its position + 1; Passable, Sound, Exceptional, Flawless; quality 2 was renamed from Solid so it never shares a word with the fragility row Solid, IR7). Round 3 added the activity attention table (ATTENTION, alert to asleep); filching reads it (TN4), sight does not yet (D-200).

Modifier types (each type diminishes within itself; values per type are deferred):

- Gear and situational skill perks: inside effective skill; flat skill perks: inside true skill (§3).
- Roll perks: flat roll points.
- Gear roll modifiers: rare noted modifiers from items, such as the weapon hit adjustment, at full value (Part 5 §3).
- Wounds: health-track level plus body-part role penalties, plus −5 while an essential part is Crippled or worse, in roll points.
- Situational: flanking, outnumbered, unaware, terrain, range, light, dodge vs missiles. **Situational modifiers are a % of the roller's average roll** (D-76, as wounds are, PW1 b): 50.5 + SKILL\_WEIGHT × the roll's effective skill, shown as "−7% (−5.3)" (pctMod, src/ruleset/body.ts; rollSetup turns the % into points). The %s are the old flat numbers ÷ 75.5, rounded as wounds were (5 → 7, 10 → 13, 15 → 20, 20 → 27, 30 → 40), so skill 50 plays as before; bonuses scale the same way. This covers outnumbered (OUTNUMBERED\_MOD −7%: −3.5 at skill 0, −5.3 at 50, −7.0 at 100, −10.5 at 200), awareness, dodge vs missile (+13%), light, cover, range, hostile close, a filch's attention and item size, and repeat try. It does not cover the difficulty grades (Waking, How faint, Lock or grade: SE23's gradeMod stays in points, parked as D-431), Can't defend (no dice), Coin offered and Rallied by (already scale with level or skill), or Night travel (an event roll has no skill). A defender's awareness of the attacker reads one table, CONFIG.AWARENESS\_DEFENSE (0, −13%, −27%): suspicious sits on step 0 (no change), unaware on step 1 (−13%: −6.6 at skill 0, −9.8 at 50, −13.1 at 100, −19.6 at 200); the Combat Stealth 40 perk Unseen Strike moves both one step on (D-397), so unaware against it is −27% (−13.6 / −20.4 / −27.1 / −40.6).
- Aim: its own type, so it never diminishes with situational modifiers.

**Rounding (R4).** A formula rounds once, at its end. The screen formats the value the engine used and never rounds it again its own way.

**Stacking within a type.** Within one modifier type, bonuses and penalties are ranked separately by size. Each group stacks by the one power rule (RN1, runaway-stacking page, 14/11/2026; src/ruleset/stacking.ts powerStack): n equal items total n^p, so the k-th largest counts k^p − (k−1)^p, with no ceiling. Roll modifiers use p = MOD\_STACK\_EXP (0.5, RN2 b): the largest in full, the second at 0.41, the third at 0.32, the fourth at 0.27. The type's value is the bonus total plus the penalty total. MOD\_STACK\_EXP is a placeholder. Example: bonuses +6, +4, +2 give +8.29; penalties −8, −3 give −9.24; the type adds −0.95. Other scenarios set their own exponent: payoff pieces PAYOFF\_EXP 0.8 (RN4); the 120 perks' shares and percent augments on one skill add in full (RN3 c, p = 1); perk sleep multipliers (sleepNeed, sleepClear) multiply together in full, as the spot, armour and body type multipliers do (RN5 c; review if growth multipliers above ×1 start piling up); chance and % reductions are decided one by one as they are built (RN6 b). Earlier: the halving series STACK\_RATIO^(x−1) (Ali, 08/11/2026).

One roll point equals two skill points; write roll modifiers small.

**Tier shifts**, applied after banding: natural edges (01–05 −1 tier, 96–100 +1, never two: Ali, 12/11/2026) and tier perks. They apply to attack and defense rolls, never to the location roll. There is no clamp: a shifted tier may go past Critical Success or below Critical Failure. Narration and the packet show the nearest of the six named tiers with one + or − per step past the end: ID 3 is "Critical Success+", ID 4 "Critical Success++", ID −4 "Critical Failure−".

**Gap shifts**, applied to the gap (Part 3, Damage flow): heavy weapon vs block −1 and perks that add a tier to block or dodge. Cover is a roll modifier, not a gap shift (CV6).

The margin bands stay open-ended: a margin of 75 or more is Critical Success, never more, unless a perk or achievement unlocks tiers past it. Otherwise only shifts move a tier past the ends.

**Unlocked tiers (D-211, Ali, 11/11/2026).** A perk or achievement may open tiers past either end on one roll kind, on every roll made as one side of a contest (roll `contest`, D-292), or on every roll (`tierUnlocks`, read by `tierReach`). On skill rolls each tier opened adds a margin band TIER\_EXTRA\_BAND (50) wider past the end: one tier up makes 125 or more Critical Success+, two make 175 or more Critical Success++; one tier down makes below −125 Critical Failure−. Natural edges and tier shifts still apply on top. The event roll reads its unlocks differently, on purpose (Ali): skill can raise a skill roll's margin, so an opened tier there is a band skill can reach; nothing raises an event roll, which must stay random, so an opened tier there widens the natural critical range instead (EVENT\_CRIT\_NATURALS). The two rules are not to be merged.

**Contests.** Every roll with two sides (perception against stealth, an ambush's Combat Stealth against perception, speech against the listener) uses roll() for both sides and is read by tier difference, like the attack and defense gap: gap = the opposing side's tier − the acting side's tier, after tier shifts. The acting side wins below 0, the opposing side wins above 0, and 0 is a tie. ContestResult carries the gap; what a larger gap does is not defined yet. Modifier types are those of Layer 2b except aim, stacked the same way. Wounds reach a roll through the skill's body-part role tags (Part 3, Roles).
