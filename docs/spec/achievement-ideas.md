| AA-295 | Temptation refused | 1, 2 | Parked | Moved from D-295. Ali (14/11/2026, round 9, BA13b "a": park it with its own number and this method). A reward for refusing a strong offer. In the novel it is the largest on the list (refusing a Floor Lord's offer pays twice what killing one does), it happens once, and it is hidden.
