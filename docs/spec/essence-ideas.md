## Essence ideas

Parked ideas about essences: where they come from, who holds or trades them, caps and content. They wait here for an
essences thread. Each has a fixed ID (IEI-n), never renumbered or reused. A row moved from the "Deferred items" table
keeps its number (D-227 became IEI-227; the D-n row in the archive points here); a new one is the number of its own
closed GitHub issue, as a D-n is (`docs/instructions.md` section 2). Finished rows move to `deferred-items-archive.md`.
