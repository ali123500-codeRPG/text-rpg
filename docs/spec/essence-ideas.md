## Essence ideas

Parked ideas about essences: where they come from, who holds or trades them, caps and content. They wait here for an
essences thread. Each has a fixed ID (IEI-n), never renumbered or reused. A row moved from the "Deferred items" table
keeps its number (D-227 became IEI-227; the D-n row in the archive points here); a new one is the number of its own
closed GitHub issue, as a D-n is (`docs/instructions.md` section 2). Finished rows move to `deferred-items-archive.md`.

| IEI-262 | Carrying a trait onto better points | 1 | Parked | Moved from D-262. Ali (13/11/2026, FS10 "later"). Replacing an essence loses it whole, so a 4-point early essence with a loved trait blocks an 18-point one. **Suggested method.** When an offer replaces a held essence of the same source table, the player may keep the old trait and take the new points, at the removal price; or a rare service does it (D-250). Matters from skill 100 on. |
| IEI-263 | Swapping an essence for a random one of the same grade | 1 | Parked | Moved from D-263. Ali (13/11/2026, FS11 "later"). **Suggested method.** A rare service or item: the held essence is given up and a new row is picked by weight from the same source table at the same grade, its points measured again; it costs the removal price. It softens bad luck without removing commitment. |
| IEI-265 | A unique foe's essence, and a source for epic and legendary | 1, 4 | Parked | Moved from D-265. Ali (13/11/2026, FS6: "we need to suggest a method in a parked item for these consider later items, to make future discussion easier"). The quality roll tops out at rare (Critical Success), so the epic and legendary grades have multipliers but no source. **Suggested method.** A unique template (D-238) lists its own essence row with a fixed grade of epic or legendary, offered with no quality roll when that being is beaten or grants it; one per character, it cannot be let go, and it carries a quirk tied to the being (FS5). Ali allows such an essence to be a plain best choice because it is rare (FS20). Options for the rest: a epic and legendary only from unique beings; b Critical Success steps past the last tier also reach them. |
