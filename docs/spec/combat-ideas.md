## Combat ideas

Parked ideas about combat and bodies: damage and HP, armor, cover, body parts, status effects, and what follows when someone is down or yields. They wait here for a combat thread. Each has a fixed ID (ICB-n), never renumbered or reused. A row moved from the "Deferred items" table keeps its number (D-54 became ICB-54; the D-n row in the archive points here); a new one is the number of its own closed GitHub issue, as a D-n is (`docs/instructions.md` section 2). Finished rows move to `deferred-items-archive.md`.
