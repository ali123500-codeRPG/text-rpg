They wait here for a vows or pledges thread. Each has a fixed ID (IVP-n), never renumbered or reused. A row moved from
another list keeps its number (IPP-266 became IVP-266; the row it left points here); a new one is the number of its own
closed GitHub issue, as a D-n is (`docs/instructions.md` section 2). Finished rows move to `deferred-items-archive.md`.

| IVP-406 | Curses that pay off at a cost | 1 | Parked | Moved from D-406. The owner, Questions hub page "Vows: goals you set with an agent (D-141)", 21/11/2026, VW13: "move both for now, we can discuss later." Was: Ali (19/11/2026): "park idea for curses that payoff at a cost." A curse that gives a real benefit along with its drawback, so bearing one can be a build choice and not only a misfortune. Not designed or built: today curses are only named as one kind of status that may modify the event roll, and status effects are parked as D-198. Related: D-378 (quirks for power, taken at creation), D-311 (blessings and gods; a curse could be a blessing's mirror), D-257 (a cost that fuels a payoff). Options when it comes back: (a) cursed gear: an item that pays off while worn or carried and cannot be taken off freely (removing it costs something, such as a rite or a god's favour); (b) a curse as a status from a source (a god, a foe, a place, a broken oath) carrying a bonus and a penalty together, lifted by the same kinds of things that lay it; (c) a curse as a trade on two numbers: one rule number up, another down (more damage, but no Parry or no fleeing); (d) a curse whose payoff grows with what it has cost (the harder the drawback has bitten, the bigger the benefit), a fuel and multiplier chain; (e) curses chosen at creation as part of D-378. Scaling: a flat drawback or bonus (±10 on a skill) bites hard at 50, still matters at 75 and is near nothing at 150 to 200, so both sides should be shares or tier shifts, not flat points. At 50 and 75 a curse reads as a real-world bargain (a body pushed past safe limits: no pain, no rest); at 100 the edge of what a body can bear; past 100 supernatural (a god's mark, a cost to the soul), with tiers that keep going as blessings' tiers do. Flag: a drawback that only touches human needs (hunger, fatigue, sleep) stops mattering for beings without them, so every curse needs a cost every body pays (health, morale, Burden or time). |
