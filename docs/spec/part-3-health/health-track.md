### Health track

Total HP is divided into levels by the share of HP lost, following World of Darkness.

| Level | HP lost | Wounds modifier (% of the average roll) | Example hooks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unhurt | 0% | 0 | — |
| Bruised | 1–15% | 0 | Free buffer; a toughness perk can widen this band |
| Hurt | 16–30% | −7% | Perk: ignore the Hurt penalty |
| Injured | 31–45% | −13% | Fear: reaching Injured, Wounded or Mauled costs morale (rolled) |
| Wounded | 46–60% | −20% | Perk: +10 to melee hit rolls while Wounded or worse. Visibly hurt: NPCs react (views give the health level from Wounded down) |
| Mauled | 61–75% | −27% | +20 to intimidate a Mauled foe; Mauled enemies may surrender |
| Critical | 76–99% | −40% | One action per turn; last-stand perks switch on; fear: reaching Critical costs more morale (rolled) |
| Incapacitated | 100–149% | Out of the fight | Lethal fight: the story ends; otherwise a survival roll (D-54) |
| Dead | 150% | Out for good | The player's own Dead is never shown (it shows as Incapacitated). Others show "Down" once they fall, and "Dead" when the player sees them clearly (full sight); the Narrator does see it. No healing row heals it (`revivesDead` is no on every row, a lever for resurrection later) |

HP lost keeps counting past 100% while an actor is down, up to 150%, where it is Dead (Ali, 08/11/2026; first set at 200%). Someone down can still be struck (BG-25): the blow lands as on someone unaware (no defense), and a blow on someone already down does not count as downing them again. Only the dead can't be struck, and an NPC's blow on the downed player is wasted, since the survival roll (D-54) decides the player's fate. A wasted action carries its reason (already dead, out of reach, nerve broken, yielded to, spar held) for the Narrator, which tells that and never makes up a miss (Narrator rule 18). A destroyed essential part (head, neck) is Dead.

The penalty is a roll modifier of type wounds (Part 1 §4, Layer 2b), diminishing together with body-part role penalties. It does not apply to no-dice checks (settles OQ-15).

**Wounds are a % of the average roll** (Ali, 15/11/2026, "Wound and morale penalties as %" PW1 b, PW3 a, PW4 a, PW5 a). Every wounds-type penalty (health, morale and fatigue levels, body-part roles, a crippled essential part, a damaged shield) is a % of the roller's average roll, 50.5 (the d100's average) + SKILL\_WEIGHT × the effective skill, and goes on the margin as points (woundsOnRoll, src/ruleset/body.ts). The %s are the old flat numbers ÷ 75.5, so skill 50 plays as before; they still stack as one wounds pile at MOD\_STACK\_EXP. Rolls show each as its % and the points it came to ("health: Wounded −20% (−15.1)"). How it scales, Wounded 20% in skill points: 20 at skill 0, 30 at 50, 35 at 75, 40 at 100, 50 at 150, 60 at 200, 100 at 400, with no ceiling; its share of skill falls from 60% at 50 to 30% at 200 and settles toward 20%, so past 100 a wound always matters, less than in the human range.

Bands are 15% wide; Critical takes the remaining 24% so a last stand lasts a little longer. Penalty values are placeholders; they stack by Part 1 §4, Stacking within a type. The health track never modifies the event roll (Part 1 §4).

**Morale** (Ali, 10/11/2026, morale bar MB1–MB43). Morale is a second, mental health bar that every actor has, the player included (MB11). Max morale = MORALE\_BASE (50) + MORALE\_PER\_RESOLVE (1) × Resolve (raw), as HP comes from Endurance, plus the flat max morale the actor's current achievements give (the maxMorale reward, lower levels of a chain included; D-278); an NPC template sets its starting Resolve (MB4; a high Resolve is not immunity), and raw Resolve also sets the Nerve tier (Part 4, Personality). Fears cost flat points (MB5), one row each in the library fears table: reaching Injured to Mauled 5 and Critical 50 (rolled, only while engaged, the player while someone is fighting them; the health range can move by steps with a perk's or achievement's fearFrom, MB21), a bandmate going Down 10 or breaking 10 when the actor perceives it (MB23), a hit that leaves the actor's own parts Crippled or worse (rolled once per hit, paying for each part by its impact: non-essential 5, normal 15, essential 30 for a 6-part body, × 6 ÷ the body's part count; a part Destroyed rolls again at 1.5 × those costs, read from the Crippled row; Ali, 11/11/2026), losing to an intimidation 10 per tier of the gap, and downing a foe gives back 10. A rolled fear makes the morale roll (Resolve, with renown on every morale roll of a foe fighting the player, MB24, and rally) and pays the cost × MORALE\_TIER\_MULT for its tier: Critical Failure 2 (+0.5 for each step beyond), Failure 1.5, Near Miss 1, Success 0.5, Great Success 0.25, Critical Success 0. The level is the share of max morale lost (MORALE\_TRACK): Calm, Uneasy 15%, Tense 30%, Rattled 45% (−7%), Afraid 60% (−10%; PW6 a), Terrified 75% (−13%), Panicking (−20%); each a % of the average roll (PW1 b), Broken at 100%, and Cowering at CONFIG.MORALE\_COWER\_AT × max (120%, a balance lever; BF79): morale lost can pass max morale up to there, a broken actor keeps paying fears on the way down (one that surrendered pays no more), and every Broken rule holds at Cowering too (Shattered banked, MB2). The penalty is type wounds, so it stacks with the health penalty and hits the morale roll too: fear feeds on itself (MB3). Reaching Broken replaces the old instant break (MB1): an NPC is broken for the scenario and cannot attack or go toward a foe: it flees, or keeps what it chose that is still allowed and may walk away too, and cowers when no way leads away or at Cowering (Fallback behavior, rule 1; BF37, BF79); if the roll that broke it was Critical Failure or worse it surrenders instead, unless its race is Unintelligent (a slime), which just breaks (BG-268, BF78: Intelligent and Semi-intelligent races can surrender); its perceiving bandmates pay the bandmate-broken fear. The player at Broken keeps control but cannot attack or walk toward a foe, and at Cowering cannot walk at all (MB12, BF37, BF79), and a break counts as defeated (achievements). **The player's yield** (MB40–MB43): saying "I yield" in a real fight (the Parser's yields, as in a spar) or typing /yield asks the foes who hear it (state.yielding, src/engine/yield.ts): every foe fighting the player (hostile and engaged), and hostile foes who perceive the player by any sense (the yield said at normal volume) and are fighting someone on the player's side (YD1, Ali, 11/11/2026). A hostile who is only tracking or hunting the player, such as one listening from another room, does not hear it (BG-4). A taken yield also covers the taker's hostile bandmates who are together with it (YD2, YD7) and did not refuse it themselves; bandmates apart keep fighting. Once everyone who heard the yield has taken it, the player's fight ends that turn (YD3); a hostile who never heard it starts a new fight. Who counts as a foe in the fight log stays parked (YD4). A foe run by Claude, of an Intelligent race, answers in its next output (acceptYield; no answer is a refusal); code-run foes and lesser races fight on. A taken yield is a truce for the rest of the scenario: neither is hostile to the other, the fight ends, and it counts as defeated. Once yielded, the player cannot attack a foe that took the yield or has yet to answer, and a foe that took it cannot attack the player (MB43). What happens after a yield is OQ-30. Broken is a win condition: a broken actor counts on neither side of engagement. Surrender and a break also turn its stance toward everyone it was hostile to into **yielded** (Q1, Ali 08/11/2026); a yielded actor never attacks and is not a threat, so it blocks no rest or waiting; it still pays defeat XP; attacking it again turns it hostile, and talk steps it from opposed. Recovery comes from the library **composure** table, apart from healing so morale tunes on its own (Ali, 11/11/2026, D-183): natural composure is its hourly row (0.1% of max morale per Resolve point an hour, 5% at 50, MB8); Danger passed, when an engagement ends, gives back MORALE\_ENGAGEMENT\_END\_PCT (25%, MB9); Rest gives REST\_MORALE\_PCT (50%) and Clinic CLINIC\_MORALE\_PCT (100%) (MB34). A healing row names the composure row its action also restores (`composure`), so a rest restores health from the healing table and morale from the composure table in one action. One engine call (calm) applies a row; a perk that restores morale directly calls calmFromPerk and counts under the Perks row (CP5, count only); a unit test (morale-writes) fails if code restores morale anywhere else. Downing a foe and being rallied still restore through the fears table (negative cost). When the scenario ends, yielded becomes opposed (a grudge), broken and surrendered end (Q4), a bar still at Broken moves up to Panicking (one point short), and the actor goes to its exit slot (with no off-stage yet, D-46/D-67, that is how it leaves). Others see an actor's morale only from Afraid down (MORALE\_SHOWN\_FROM, MB16); the actor itself always sees its own. Yielded is not on the STANCES scale: agents cannot choose it. **Dreaded on Sight** (D-236): a quirk whose owner frightens and repels at first sight. Whoever outside the owner's band sees the owner for the first time pays the fears-table row "You set eyes on someone dreaded" (15, rolled, the one rolled fear paid outside a fight too), and from then on reads its band's stance toward the owner one step colder until it has a stance of its own. Once per owner, for good; no race, class or template has it yet. What else the player can do with it is deferred (D-98).

**Fatigue and stamina** (Ali, 13/11/2026, Fatigue and Stamina page FT1–FT28; src/ruleset/fatigue.ts, src/engine/fatigue.ts). Every actor has a **stamina bar** in two layers, both sized STAMINA\_BASE (50) + STAMINA\_PER\_POINT (1) × Stamina (raw), as HP comes from Endurance (FT2). The **long term** (its name in game text: **sleep debt**) fills with hours awake, spilled strain, a share of all strain and travel, and only sleep clears it for now (FT18). The **fatigue level** is the share of the long term used (FATIGUE\_TRACK, names in the FATIGUE\_LEVELS table): Rested 0%, Fine 15%, Tired 30% (−7%), Weary 45% (−13%), Exhausted 60% (−20%), Drained 75% (−27%), Dead on your feet 90% (−40%), Collapsed 100% (−40%); each a % of the average roll (PW1 b). The penalty is type wounds, so it stacks with health and morale wounds (FT3). The **short line** (the thin line under the bar) holds the level's band of the bar: 100, 90, 80, 65, 50, 35, 20% (FT19, FT22). **Strain** has no roll and no multiplier (FT24): every row of the Action times table names an **effort tier** (EFFORT, an ordered table; a new tier is a new row), and the action pays that tier's strain (STRAIN\_COST: Base 0, Light 0.5, Moderate 1, Hard 2, All-out 4; FT25) when it is made: once for a row of STRAIN\_LONG\_S (10 s) or less, per 10 s it runs for a longer one (a long action pays for the share it ran). Movement pays per STRAIN\_MOVE\_S (3 s) moved: a walk (move time) at WALK\_EFFORT Base, a run (a dash, main time) at RUN\_EFFORT Hard. A dodge, block or parry, which has no row, pays DEFENSE\_EFFORT Light. Placement (FB1–FB3): Base free actions, words, drawing, stowing, swapping and picking up, a door, bar or key, a drink, rest, sleep, waiting, trading; Light a look round, handing over; Moderate bandaging, picking a lock, hiding (armour on or off is Base and instant since 14/11/2026); Hard a blow, a throw, a bow or crossbow shot; All-out Battle Cry. A crossbow's spanning is instant and Base (ACT-34), so its shot's row sets the effort (BG-340). An action with no row of its own (a grab, a filch, steadying yourself, a shout) takes the default row, Base; picking a lock pays the Pick a lock row. Worn armour no longer multiplies strain (TP6 d, less realism; it was medium ×1.5, heavy ×2). Strain drains the line; what an empty line can't hold spills into the long term at SPILL\_SHARE (half, FT23). Every strain point paid also adds STRAIN\_RESIDUE (10%) of itself to the long term (SR4), so fights carry into the night: at Stamina 50 four 5-turn fights add about 9% of the bar. Travel pays by the hour straight into the long term (SR5, see Travel on the road). The line itself gives no roll penalty (SR1 d). Check: a blow and a defence every 3 s (2.5 a round) empties the line in 1 min at Stamina 0, 2 at 50, 3 at 100, 5 at 200. **Sleep debt** (FT4, FT12): each hour awake adds SLEEP\_DEBT\_FLAT 0.5 points + SLEEP\_DEBT\_PCT 1.5% of the bar, × the body type's sleepNeed (left out = 1, every body type today; 0 = never needs sleep, still feels strain). **Recovery** is its own library table beside healing and composure (recovery, RV; FT7), and every refill goes through one engine call per layer (recoverLine, recoverDebt): **Sleep** clears SLEEP\_CLEAR\_PCT 4% of the bar an hour from the long term × the spot (SLEEP\_SPOT\_MULT: lying on a bed 1, elsewhere 0.75; on a bed means at the bed's spot in metres, not in a room with one, and both sleepers sharing a bed get it: see Sleeping on a bed in the Game clock topic, (MQ13 a; Ali, 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Map and minimap: one map, doors"; MQ17 b / MQ18; Ali, 20/11/2026, Questions hub page "Map and minimap: one map, doors")) × worn armour (SLEEP\_ARMOR\_MULT: all 1 since Ali 14/11/2026, sleeping in armour makes no difference; was light 1, medium 0.75, heavy 0.5), and fills the line (FT8). **Rest** (the one-hour long action) fills the line by the share of its hour that ran. **Catching your breath**: while awake, once BREATH\_CALM\_S (10 s) have passed since the last effort above Light, the line refills BREATH\_BASE\_PCT 15% + Stamina × BREATH\_PER\_POINT\_PCT 0.05% of its size a minute (FT28; to balance later). **Perks** is a counting row for later stamina perks. The clock runs this for every active actor, on stage or off: an NPC's routine sleep is sleep, and off-stage time adds hours awake and asleep (FT11). **Sleep length** (FT9): "sleep" lasts until the long term is clear at this spot's rate, within SLEEP\_MIN\_HOURS 1 to SLEEP\_MAX\_HOURS 24, or until the time of day it names; waking (a blow, a noise, Light Sleeper) or a watch ends it early, and the watches of a shared night still split it (NW15). The Sleep row's own seconds are 0 for that reason. **Limits** (FT10, for now): sleep debt that fills the bar (an hour awake taking it to 100%) puts the actor to sleep where it stands until rested (the player's sleep is a night of its own); spilled strain that fills it leaves the actor **Spent**: no action above Light (it comes to nothing, wasted with reason spent; a dash and Battle Cry are refused) until the bar is below full, which only sleep does. A Spent actor who stays awake still falls asleep once its hours awake alone would have filled the bar (FB4). Falling asleep from exhaustion keeps the guards of falling asleep by routine (BF69, `mayDropOff` in src/engine/sleep.ts): not within FALL_ASLEEP_MIN of waking, nor while alarmed, broken, engaged or held; once those lift, the next minute takes it. Fatigue changes no healing, composure or fear cost, and sleeping gives no bonus (FT13, FT14). **Who sees it** (FT17, FT20): the player sees their own bar (PlayerView.player.stamina: level, points left of the bar and of the line, spent); others' level shows from FATIGUE\_SHOWN\_FROM (Exhausted) down; an NPC's own view and the Narrator get the level word. **On screen** (FT20): the sheet shows Stamina under Morale, in its own colour: on the full sheet the points left of the bar (the level is the bar's tip, SD5), one bar of what is left of the long term with ticks at the level marks, and under it the thin breath line, as wide as the level's band of the bar and filled by what is left of it (hover: what breath is). Tired and worse add "Tired: −7% to rolls"; Spent shows as a chip beside the label (src/ui/charsheet.ts staminaBar). The fake page's "tire" adds sleep debt and spends breath; "breathe" fills the line. Who's here shows someone else's fatigue as a chip with the level word in the stamina colour, only from Exhausted down and never on someone Down; no points or breath line (FT17; src/ui/play.ts). The fake page's guard Bren starts Exhausted. **The Stamina skill** (FT15, FT27): SK37, the third Fortitude skill, so Fortitude's All-50 (Hard to Kill) is earnable; function Stamina (FN17) in its own group Stamina (GR12) at quarter XP. It is buyable at creation like any skill; NPC templates copy their Endurance value (FB7). The player's Stamina is marked once per STAMINA\_MARK\_EVERY (50) points of strain paid, any effort, armour included (FB6); no rolls. **Scaling:** the bar and line grow in a straight line with Stamina, with no cap; strain is paid in full at every level, and supernatural efforts get new tiers above All-out (8, 16 …). Tired comes after 12 h awake at Stamina 0, 15 h at 50, 16 h at 100, 18 h at 200, never later than 20 h: staying up for days is perk territory. A full breath back takes about 6½ min at 0, 5¾ at 50, 5 at 100, 4 at 200. Status effects such as bleeding stay separate (D-198).

**Healing** (Ali, 07/11/2026, "Healing: options" H1–H15). Every source is a row of one library table (healing): HP %, part-pool %, its reach (the worst part state it heals, a key of the PART\_STATES scale, so a perk can shift it by steps), whether it works in a fight, and the place kinds it needs; one engine rule applies a row, so amounts stay consistent (B5) and stronger tiers are new rows. Rows now (placeholders): rest 25/25 (its morale from the composure Rest row), clinic 100/100 (morale from the composure Clinic row) and lifts Crippled parts (resting in a place of kind clinic or infirmary), bandage 10/10, usable in a fight as the main action and used up (an item names its row with `heal`; the player starts with STARTING\_BANDAGES 2). A part worse than the row's reach is skipped; a Crippled or worse part a row reaches starts again from 0 (H4). Rest and bandage reach Damaged; the clinic reaches Crippled. A rest action (the turn's main action) needs no active hostile in the zone and waits REST\_COOLDOWN\_MIN (*placeholder*, 240) game minutes (H5, T7). **Natural recovery** (H12–H15) is the hourly row passive: every active actor on the stage heals endurance (true skill) × 0.01 % an hour (0.5% at 50) of HP and of each part it reaches (never Crippled or worse), all the time, fights included, as the clock moves; a line shows only when a health level or part state changes. The composure table's natural composure row does the same for morale from Resolve (Part 3, Morale). NPCs use the same table when they rest; healing off stage waits for D-67 (H8). A Destroyed part stays gone unless a special perk, item or story event brings it back (settles OQ-6). The turn shows a plain line such as "Bandage: Hurt → Bruised"; its look is the designer's (H11). Later: a first-aid skill (H9, D-14), bleeding and other statuses (H10, D-15). **Healing by source** (Ali, 10/11/2026, D-172): achievements count the HP the player actually gets back, as a flat number (the health % healed × their total HP; body parts not counted), kept apart by where it came from: the healing row's id (rule `healed`, by `heal_source`, name placeholder {source}). Rest, natural recovery, clinic and bandage each keep their own count. Healing from perks counts under the Perks row (HL6, `countOnly`: never applied itself), and only for perks that heal directly (Second Wind's jump). A perk that boosts another source counts as that source: Fast Healer's doubled natural recovery is all natural recovery (Ali). Fast Healer's ×2 (PerkDef numbers.recoveryMult, `recoveryMult`) speeds every natural recovery: health (Natural recovery), morale (Natural composure, from Resolve) and stamina (Catching your breath) (Ali, 16/11/2026); rest and sleep are actions and keep their own rates. Every perk that heals directly goes through one engine call (healFromPerk), so a new healing perk counts here with no extra code; a unit test (hp-writes) fails if code lowers health lost anywhere else. A rest bonus from an achievement (`restPct`) still counts as rest.

**Visibility:** for other characters, Claude is told the level only from Wounded down; Bruised to Injured stay hidden, though code still applies their penalties.

**Thresholds as triggers**

- Perks read the level: they can ignore a level's penalty, widen a band, or switch on a bonus at a level.
- Tactics read the level: enemy AI retreats, surrenders or turns desperate at set levels, and players get options against hurt foes.
- Story reads the level: views give everyone who perceives the player its health level from Wounded down, which scenarios, companions and NPCs can check. There are no separate story flags: what the player did is kept as history facts, deals, stance overrides and quests (C5).

Code fires one event per change, `threshold_crossed {who, from, to}`; perks, AI and story scripts listen for it.
