### Reach, range, cover and light

An attack reaches targets within its attack mode's reach (Part 5 §5), with sight on every link of the shortest path.

- **Situational roll modifiers (ranged):** a hostile actor close enough to strike or grab the shooter (SZ19: within a 0.75 m step plus its own melee reach, between bodies), −10 (RD5: HOSTILE\_NEAR\_STEP\_M, HOSTILE\_NEAR\_MOD; Point Blank removes it). Range (`rangeMod`, CONFIG.RANGE\_SHARE\_MODS): the distance ÷ the target's size factor (SZ13: a horse is as easy as a person at half the metres) as a share of the mode's reach, both from real metres: up to 25% 0, 50% −5, 75% −10, 100% −15. A longbow (90 m, 11 links) at 48 m is −5 where a shortbow (50 m, 6 links) is −15 \[Proposed\].
- **Light** (any roll that needs sight; LT1, LT2): six levels, brightest first, each a real amount of light (CONFIG.LIGHT\_LUX, Wikipedia "Lux"): bright 1,000 lux, lamplit 50, dim 3, moonlit 0.2, dark 0.002, pitch black 0. A roll takes −LIGHT\_DECADE\_MOD (5) for each tenfold drop below FULL\_SIGHT\_LUX (50): bright and lamplit 0, dim −6, moonlit −12, dark −22; pitch black reads LIGHT\_FLOOR\_LUX (−28) and nothing is seen there at all. Night Eyes reads the light ten times brighter, Child of Night ten times darker. The rules that once read "dark" (sight at most partial, a still figure only half seen) hold from CONFIG.DARK\_FROM (moonlit) down. Outdoors at evening and night the sky is moonlit, one flat level until moon phases (LT3, parked). Rooms are lamplit by default. The attacker uses the target's zone; a defender uses the attacker's zone.
- **Cover** (CV1–CV8, Ali 18/11/2026): five levels, each the share of a standing person (1.75 m) the zone's cover hides (CONFIG.COVER\_HIDDEN): none 0, light ¼ (a street's carts and posts, a heath's gorse), some ½, heavy ¾, dense ⅞. What shows of a body: shown = min(1, (1 − hidden) × grow); grow = height ÷ 1.75 for a body no taller than a person (a 0.3 m slime in heavy cover shows 4%), else max(1, height ÷ what the zone's cover stands), what it stands being the zone kind's coverM (a room 1.1 m, a yard 2, woods 20; never under 1.75), so a horse keeps a wood's cover but stands out over a yard wall. A zone with none gives none (coverShown). The roll modifier is COVER\_HALVING\_MOD (10, the aim ladder's step) for each halving of what shows: round(10 × log2(1 ÷ shown)) (coverMod): a person in light cover 4, some 10, heavy 20, dense 30. A ranged attack at a target in cover takes it as a minus (melee never does), and a hide-tagged roll (the three Stealth skills, stashing) takes the same number as a plus (CV7). No ground clutter for small bodies (CV5); cover never shortens sight (CV10); hit locations under cover parked (D-390). Past the Cover (CV8 b): what shows counts 2^PAST\_THE\_COVER\_HALVINGS (4) times as much, two halvings back. Melt Away works where the zone's cover hides at least half of the owner, or in dim light or darker.
- **Cover from objects** (PC1, PC2, Ali 18/11/2026): every furniture kind has a size, [length, width, height] in metres (FurnitureKind.sizeM, placeholders: a bar counter 3 × 0.6 × 1.1, a table 1.5 × 0.8 × 0.75, barrels 1.2 × 0.6 × 1, a cart 1.5 × 1 × 1), standing at its point (furniturePos; along the wall it stands by, east–west in the middle). Seen from a shooter or watcher, a piece the sight line to the target's centre crosses hides what of the target lies below the line from the viewer's eye over the piece's far edge, carried on to the target: hidden height = eye + (top − eye) × d_target ÷ d_piece, distances along the ground; eye = the viewer's floor height + body height × CONFIG.EYE\_SHARE (0.94, ANSUR II); shown = 1 − clamp(hidden height ÷ target height) (ruleset/objectCover objectHidden). So a shooter on a wall or upper floor sees over a low wall, and a piece right in front of the shooter hides nothing far off. With nobody in particular to hide from (a sleeper's ears, Melt Away, Hug the Cover), the tallest piece within CONFIG.OBJECT\_COVER\_M (1 m) of the body hides its own height. Indoors a zone keeps no cover level (clutterCover: walls and furniture give it; Room and Cellar kinds now none); outdoors the zone's level stands for scattered clutter (trees, brush, a crowd). A target takes the larger of the two, never both: shown = min(clutter shown, object shown). How wide a piece is across the line is left out (with hit locations under cover, D-390).
- **Windows and openings** (PC3, Ali 18/11/2026): a window is a drop (seen, shot and jumped through, never walked: SD7) that may carry shutters, the Window barrier kind (BK6: 0.6 × 1 m, sill 0.9 m above the inside floor, sillM; placeholders). Shutters open, shut, lock, bar and break like a door; shut, nothing is seen or shot through and nobody jumps through (the link has sight false). A Window goes only on a drop and a drop takes no other barrier (plan check). Through any opening on the last way of the sight line (a window, a doorway, a gate; a hatch excepted) a target shows only what the opening frames: its sill and top carried along the sight line to the target bound a band of the target's height, and the opening's width carried along the line the same way bounds how much of its width shows (openingShown): shown = band share × min(1, opening width × d_target ÷ d_opening ÷ target width). So an archer standing at an arrow slit sees the whole field, while a target just behind a slit shows a strip. A person 2 m inside a window, shot from 15 m on level ground, shows about 55% (−9). **Window kinds** (PC4 a, Ali 18/11/2026; placeholders): shuttered window (BK6) 0.6 × 1 m, sill 0.9; arrow slit (BK7) 0.1 × 1.2 m, sill 1, always seen through, too narrow to climb; barred window (BK8) 0.6 × 1 m, sill 0.9, seen and shot through, never jumped through until broken; large window (BK9) 1.2 × 1.5 m, sill 0.6, behind shutters. A slit and bars are fixed (BarrierKind.fixed: nothing to open or shut) and see-through (seesAcross reads a kind's seeThrough, as a grate always was). A person 2 m behind each, shot from 15 m: shuttered or barred −9, slit −32, large −4. The window edge kind (a whole side you see across anywhere: open arches, a glazed front) stays (PC5 a).
