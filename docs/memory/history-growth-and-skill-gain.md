# History: skill gain, growth at scenario end, Burden and flaws

Kind: context. What was asked, what was decided and which PR built it, oldest first. The rules are in the spec
(Part 1 sections 5 and 6); every number is in `src/config.ts`. `docs/growth-proposal.md` is the proposal behind
essences and scenario-end growth, kept as history.

- **Skill marks (11-14/11/2026; SM1-SM4; D-318).** Marks stack into more improvement rolls (#374). Instant
  skill-ups were tried and replaced by growth at scenario end. A won contest has no top to its learning window
  (#716). Every mark owes a roll (#825).
- **Scaling past 100 (11-12/11/2026; CR1-CR13).** The owner prefers perks to carry supernatural scaling: base
  sight, hearing, damage and health do not grow with skill by formula (CR5, CR6, CR8, CR9 turned down). Task
  grades go past Extreme (#479), on one ladder past 100 shared with NPC ranks and equip levels (#903). A
  natural edge moves one tier only, and no natural roll forces a skill gain (#525). Parked: D-214, D-215,
  D-219 (D-213 closed, below).
- **Tier unlocks (11-14/11/2026; D-211, D-292).** A perk or an achievement can open tiers past Critical (#451,
  #457, #727). Two rules, never merged: a skill roll gets an extra margin band, because skill can reach it; an
  event roll widens its natural range, because nothing raises an event roll. A first reading, a doubled
  natural edge, was taken back (#456).
- **The roll ceiling past 100 is closed (21/11/2026; D-213).** The owner: "I WILL GRANT MORE WAYS TO OPEN BANDS PAST
  CRITICAL, STOP ASKING THIS." Contests between very high skills get no frame or ceiling rule; more bands past
  Critical open only by ways the owner grants. Never ask about a roll ceiling or contest frame again.
- **Growth at scenario end and essences (12/11/2026; EG1-EG17, SG1-SG3; D-226).** A review found growth landing
  in the middle of fights; the aim became rare, chosen packages into limited room. Achievements, All-X perks,
  skill raises and level-ups wait for the scenario's end (#536, #545). Essences came in #550, and the
  improvement roll reads its gain off the tier table (#554, #558). Parked: NPCs holding essences (EI-227), a
  cap per skill (EI-228). Open: essence rows and traits (D-229).
- **The gains moment (12-13/11/2026; M-39 to M-39b).** The scenario's end shows every gain in one moment that
  may take as long as it needs (#538). The first cut was too fast, and XP must show its sum before the final
  number (#546). It covers the whole screen (#709). Also #556 and #588.
- **Augment kinds (14/11/2026; AK1-AK4; D-276).** Two kinds beside the plain one: a trade moves points from
  the giving skill, and a percentage grows the receiving skill smoothly (#729, #730). Achievement rewards sit
  in side lanes by default, and a row pays true skill only when the deed is about that skill.
- **Burden (14-17/11/2026; BU1-BU6, TB1-TB8; D-374, D-383).** Burden is a brake on skill growth, not carried
  weight. There is no carried-weight rule, and none is to be proposed unless the owner asks (BU6 b). Tags
  lower Burden's Total and help their group's rolls; raw has no hard limit; gain grows with the roll's margin
  (#823, #1000, #1004, #1010). Parked: raising Potential (D-317), true skill or raw in the group bonus (D-376).
- **Flaws at creation (17-18/11/2026; FC1-FC8; D-377).** Before this no character ever had a flaw. A flaw is
  chosen for each tag and earns nothing; each raises Burden in its group (#1009). Parked: quirks for power
  (D-378), tags and flaws changing in play (D-379).
