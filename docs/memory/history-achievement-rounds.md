# History: achievement rounds after the first build

Kind: context. What was asked, what was decided and which PR built it, oldest first. Continues
[history-achievements.md](history-achievements.md). Counts and reward sizes are in the library rows and the D-n
rows of the spec, never here.

- **Finds by searching (12/11/2026; SA1-SA15).** A find is a hidden person or item revealed by a search, once
  each; two chains, items and people, with no repeats and no scar. The reward is reach in metres, not a
  percent, and moves both search fields. The fields exist (#598). The two chains are not in the library;
  they are tracked as D-407.
- **Novel study answers (13-14/11/2026; BA1-BA20; D-277 to D-291, D-294, D-295).** Kills are counted apart
  from defeats, with their own chain; ordeals pay max morale; the survival roll has its row (#725). A fight won
  although morale broke has a chain, for those who chase the extraordinary (D-294, #736). The reward lane
  guide, a guide and not a ban: an ordeal pays what would have carried you through it, a feat done with a skill
  may pay that skill, a named foe pays an answer to what it did, a place pays its theme. Rewards sit in side
  lanes by default, with no limit on true skill from achievements (#729). Agreed, not built: D-283, D-285,
  D-287. Parked: D-280 to D-282, D-286, D-288 to D-291, D-295.
- **Trim (12-14/11/2026; TM1-TM29, TB1-TB6, AS1-AS11; D-297, D-298, D-303, D-304).** The owner wants fewer,
  more focused achievements but no level cap: the top row should be reachable by a player who leans in.
  Instead of cutting or merging, chains branch: a branch has its own link field, because reward carry-up and
  replacement walk other fields (#743). A started chain shows its next count (D-284). The first time an action
  succeeds gets a first row (#740, #745). Attacking a foe who gave up has its own line, with branches for
  killing the surrendered and for fleeing foes, counted only when the player sees the state (#749). Parked:
  chains that fork (D-296).
- **The page as threads (14/11/2026; BG-215).** The owner turned down a table. Chains sit centred in faint
  cards; branches fold under the trunk; only held levels and the next one are drawn; the place in the chain
  stays unknown until the last level is in sight (#774). Check changes with the fake-chains page option, on
  desktop and phone.
- **Rewards for chains that paid only XP (14-16/11/2026; XR1-XR39; D-344, D-357, D-359).** Three rounds added
  reward fields and rewards (#883, #915, #920). Amounts add up: each level adds its own on top of the lower
  ones. Parked: D-345.
- **Achievement XP as a share of the level (18/11/2026; AL1-AL8).** One equation: a fixed part by reward place
  plus a share of the earner's XP to the next level at earning. The share grows faster than a straight line
  and slower than a square, and scars and low places still get one. Quests use it too. The Achievements page
  in play shows no XP; the library viewer does (#1040).
