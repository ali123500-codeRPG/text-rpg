# Achievement design

Kind: decision. How new achievement chains are proposed. The chains themselves are library rows; the trim that
applied these decisions is in the spec's "Deferred items" table (D-297, D-298, D-303, D-304).

- **Fewer and more distinct** (12/11/2026). Propose few rows per chain and low top counts, with no endless repeats
  past a chain's top row. Check every new chain against the existing ones for overlap and say why it is distinct.
  A common chain length is a guideline, never a cap. Reason: the list had grown past what matters, and
  near-duplicate chains dilute it.
- **The first of an action is an achievement** (14/11/2026; spec D-303). A chain that counts an act starts at one.
  A chain is covered when its trunk or one of its branches fires on the first act. Exceptions: amounts, time and
  levels, and free firsts such as areas entered. Reason: a player's first time doing something is a moment worth
  marking.
- **Achievements and scars apply to permanent NPCs too** (09/11/2026). "Defeated" always means down, yielding,
  broken morale or fleeing, for the player as for foes.
- Chain counts and rewards live in the library rows, never in this file.
