# History: All-50, All-100 and All-150 perks

Kind: context. What was asked, what was decided and which PR built it, oldest first. The rules are in the spec
(D-163, D-164, D-197 and the All-X lines in Part 1 section 5); the parts are in `src/library/allPerks.ts`.

- **What an All-50 is (08/11/2026; G1-G8).** A bundle of several effects, never one ability. No weapon
  triangle and no combined check. Skipping a roll stays hard to trigger, because this game's balance is
  tighter than the games it was compared with. All-100 and All-150 are named, stronger ranks of the same tree
  line (G8 b). From the same comparison: effects that last a whole engagement (D-109), automatic successes
  (D-110), bane bonuses (D-111, built), perks unlocked in downtime (D-112).
- **Rounds 2 to 4 (09/11/2026).** The seven bundles got their parts (#133, #136, #139, #142). "Park it" means
  park the perk and offer other options for its slot. Banked perks are sorted by what they are kept for
  (D-118, #159). Never Surprised became Earshot's 100 perk (D-134, #235).
- **Parts belong to skills (10/11/2026; D-163, D-164).** An All-50 is one part per skill and one passive for
  the tree. It is granted once enough of a tree's skills reach the step, by the tree's rating, and each
  further skill adds its part. A part follows its skill when the skill moves to another tree (#297, #298).
  Later rounds: #310 (D-171), #325 (D-175), #595 (D-233). All-100 and All-150 work the same way (D-197, #390,
  #394). Parked: Fade Back (D-232), a refund when an All-X perk reuses a milestone perk (D-221).
